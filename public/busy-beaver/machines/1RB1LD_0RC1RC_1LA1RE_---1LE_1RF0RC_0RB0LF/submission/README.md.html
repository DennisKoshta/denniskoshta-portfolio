1RB1LD_0RC1RC_1LA1RE_---1LE_1RF0RC_0RB0LF never halts

  • Machine: 1RB1LD_0RC1RC_1LA1RE_---1LE_1RF0RC_0RB0LF, a BB(6) holdout (line 214 of the 740-machine list). Viewer: https://bbchallenge.org/1RB1LD_0RC1RC_1LA1RE_---1LE_1RF0RC_0RB0LF
  • Class: "Bouncer + Chaotic Edge" on the spreadsheet. In mechanism terms: a bouncer whose right part is a four-particle system with a closed double-cycle law (the edge is not chaotic).
  • Result: Lemma nonhalt: ~halts tm c0. in Coq 8.20.1 against unmodified busycoq BB6 (commit 0940bb9), importing Individual62, ES_v3 and the Coq standard library. Print Assumptions nonhalt is closed (no axioms).
  • Check time: it compiles in 6.6 s with -native-compiler yes, and coqchk re-checks it, the computed parts included, in 44.9 s (check_kill.txt).

Mechanism

  • Bouncer. Every round trip starts at a fixed 43-cell gadget G at the left end. Right of it are five 1-blocks n, a, b, c, d with four short particles between them. The head crosses blocks by C> 11 -> 10 C> going right and 0 10 B> 000 -> 0 B> 000 11 going left, and a particle turns it back; how far it gets depends on the phase (period 12 trips).
  • Drift and collisions. Every 12 trips, rule S takes K(n,a,b,c,d) to K(n+48, a-8, b+4, c, d+4). When a is down to 2, 4, 6 or 8, a collision of 135 to 147 trips (TO, TE, T6, T8) rebuilds the pattern.
  • Double cycle. On Even(n,x) = K(n, x, (x+76)/2, 35, 18), the chain S^((x-4)/8), TE, S^((x-52)/8), TO gives Even(n+12x+808, x+32) for n odd, x = 4 mod 8, x >= 52, and these conditions are preserved. The blank tape reaches Even(12863, 212) at step 153,126,993.

How the proof works

  • Rules. S, TE, TO, T6 and T8 are one es' each, for their whole domains, on half-length parameters Kr n a b c d = K(2n+1, 2a, 2b, 2c+1, 2d). es' finds the shift rules itself; S needs no case split.
  • Cycle. S_iter repeats S by induction, and DC : Ev N X -->+ Ev (48*X+716+N) (4+X) is four follows.
  • Start and end. init is the 4,097,897-step direct run on busycoq's list configurations, in six vm_compute pieces cut at left records. opening follows 20 macro steps to Ev 6431 20 = Even(12863, 212). nonhalt is multistep_nonhalt plus progress_nonhalt_simple on (N, X), with no invariant.

Template and what changed

Adapted from busycoq's verify/TranslatedBellv5.v, Module TM1: block macro rules by es', repeated by induction and chained with follow. Changes: fixed five-block configurations instead of TM1's lists, and no invariant, so the proof ends with progress_nonhalt_simple as in verify/Bouncer_v2.v Module TM1. The long opening uses nested step counters as in verify/Bouncer_v3.v's multistep_c', cut into six pieces so that coqchk can re-check each computation. The file has 361 lines, every lemma top level.
