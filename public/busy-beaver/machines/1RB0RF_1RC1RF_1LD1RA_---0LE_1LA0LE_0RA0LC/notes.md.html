Notation

  • Hub K(a,b,X) = 0^inf (110)^a 0 (011)^b C> X 0^inf (as before). Record L(n,Q) = 0^inf <E (010)^n 001 Q 0^inf, so the old T is 001 Q.
  • X = x0 g1 g2 … uses 3-cell groups: 000 = digit 0, 001 = digit 1, 010 = inert i, 100 = pending S (only in residue-derived words).
  • A word is normalized when it has no 100 group. Its value is x0 + Σ 2^j over the digit groups, skipping the inert ones.
  • Language L_X = x0 {000,001,010}* {100,010,000}*: a normalized prefix followed by a residue part.

Micro-automaton (how all the rules were derived)

  • Moving right, with F split by its predecessor:
  • A: 0→1,B / 1→0,Fa
  • Fa: 0→A / 1: turn, cells (q-1,q):=(1,0), then D at q-2
  • B: 0→1,C / 1→Fb
  • Fb: 0→A / 1→B (cell kept)
  • C: 1→A / 0: write 1, turn, D at q-1
  • Moving left:
  • D: 1→0,E / 0: halt
  • E: 1→0,E. On 0 at p: if p-1 = 1, keep both and go to D at p-2. If p-1 = 0, write 11 at (p-1,p) and continue as Fb at p+1.
  • So the only ways to halt are (a) Fa turning at a cell whose second-left neighbour is 0, and (b) E finding 010. Neither happens on L_X words in the regimes below.

Non-halting argument and the remaining gap

  • Closure. R, B and E never halt on L_X or on normalized words, and they map back into L_X. So the machine runs forever if the counter is normalized at every eat, i.e. D(Q_t) ≤ B(n_t) - 1 with B the bit length (since n ≥ 2^(B-1)).
  • Trajectory, 200,000 abstract epochs (deepen_invariant.py).
  • The slack B-1-D is ≥ 2 for every t ≥ 16. The tight cases are t ≤ 15 (slack 0 at n=2 and n=5) and then t=90, 93, 138, 595, 2,027 and 62,386 (D=17, B=20).
  • D is distributed with mean ≈ B/4 and a geometric-looking tail (ratio ≈ 0.4).
  • Candidate inductive invariant I(n,Q): Z*(Q) ≤ B(n)-2, where Z* counts the digit groups before the last inert group of the fully processed 1Q (deepen_model.zstar).
  • Lemma 1*: D(Q') ≤ 1 + Z*(Q). Structural reason: the S groups of Q' are exactly the inert groups of the uneaten counter, and those all lie in the processed Q region. Holds on all 199,999 trajectory transitions and on 26,185/26,185 random states (deepen_induct.py).
  • Lemma 2: I(n,Q) ⇒ I(n',Q'). Holds along the trajectory for every t ≥ 16 (fails only at t = 7, 8, 14, 15 with Z = B-1). Equality Z* = B-2 recurs about once per doubling (t = 92, 594, 2,026, 3,621, 11,521, 26,321, 62,386, 140,996, 182,462) and never goes over.
  • But Lemma 2* is not inductive on arbitrary states: 2 failures in 22,747 random states with B ≥ 10 (e.g. n=8107, Q=0SSii000ii → Z*'=12=B'-1), plus 66 failures with B < 10.
  • So the true inductive invariant must use more of the structure of reachable residues. This is the open gap.
  • Why the invariant matters. On artificial records where the counter is not normalized at the eat, 1 of 113 runs halted within 5 epochs (n=2, T=00100010010010000001, step 245); the rest continued. A violation can be fatal, so a proof has to establish the invariant.

New: the epoch map in closed form

The state at an eat is (n, c): - the layout is λ = i^c0 d i^c1 d …, and its d-slots hold bits 1, 2, … of n; - C = Σ c_j 2^j, and J is the index of the top inert slot.

The map: - n odd: M = (n+1)/2 + C, H = c, n' = n+1. - n even: V = n/2 + C, t = number of trailing 1-bits of V, M = V >> (t+1), H = c[t+1:], n' = n + 2 + t + Σ_{r≤t} c_r. - Next layout: merge(H, M). That is H's inert groups, plus one i for each 1-bit of M, with a d at each 0-bit of M. - Normalization depth: D = 1 + #{0-bits of M below J(H)}. The next eat is normalized iff D ≤ B(n') - 1.

Agreement: - with the deepen map: 20,000/20,000 epochs along the orbit, 17,169/17,169 random states; - with the TM: 276/276 records to 1e9 steps.

This replaces Z and D from the deepen pass. Z = J(c) = #zeros(M_prev).

Notation (as proof.md §3)

  • State (n, c) at an eat; P = ⌈n/2⌉ + C(c); B1 = B(n').
  • m(M) = Σ_{1-bits p of M} 2^{r(p)}, where r(p) = number of 0-bits of M below p. This is the weight that M's 1-bits carry into the next layout.
  • L(H,M) = Σ_j H_j (2^j - 2^{r(j)}) ≥ 0, the weight that H's inert groups lose in the merge. So C' = m(M) + C(H) - L(H,M).

B = 9 (n0 = 377) gives a non-normalized eat at n = 386 and recovers at 389. B = 10 (n0 = 761) gives one at n = 770 and recovers at 773; 14 eats by 36M steps.

  • Random states with slack ≥ 0 and no excursion (the hypotheses Lemma X may use):

    • fs_struct_search.py: 41,539 states at B = 10..40 gave violations at B = 10 (×2), 16 and 28. The B = 28 one led to σ0(B).

  • Natural orbits (fs_natural_entries.py):
  • 2,000 orbits × 3,000 epochs (5.9M eats after burn-in): 0 violations. Entries: 1,769 from cascades, 11 from w = 1, 0 from w ≥ 2. Max C/2^{B-1} at odd non-excursion eats is 0.80 (n = 917).
  • Main orbit, 400k epochs: 1 entry (the cascade at t = 350,272). Max C/2^{B-1} at odd non-excursion eats is 0.688 (t = 62,386).
  • A w = 2 entry needs only C ≳ 0.5·2^{B-1} once n is near 2^B, and the orbit reaches 0.69. Magnitude bounds therefore do not keep the orbit away from the family's regime. Only the exact top-run shape and the exact equation P_{t+2} = 2^B do.

Why this is the cryptid exit

  • What happens after an entry is decided by linear equations in the residue counts, e.g. P_{t+2} = 2^B after an entry with V = 2^B + 2^{B-4}.
  • An entry needs ⌈n/2⌉ + C to have a sparse top bit pattern. Both are coincidences along one deterministic orbit, of the kind that ground rule 6 names.
  • The heuristic for non-halting is still strong:
  • 0 non-normalized eats in more than 2·10^9 natural eats, and to 10^10 epochs on the main orbit;
  • natural orbits enter excursions only through cascades and w = 1 near-cascades;
  • even a non-normalized eat recovered on the TM in all 3 family runs.
  • None of this is a proof.

Still open

  • A proof would need an orbit-specific invariant that excludes w ≥ 2 entries with residues that solve P = 2^B. No candidate is known.
  • The other route, rules that tolerate non-normalized eats, does not close either, because some of them halt.
  • Per the cryptid protocol, the rule layer (proof.md §1–§4, §2.5) can still be formalized AntiHydra-style at low priority, as for the other cryptid candidates.
