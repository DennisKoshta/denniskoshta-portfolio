Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Cycle: from the right edge the head zigzags left (rule Z: 20 steps per block, 101110 -> 111110), bounces off the first boundary that does not carry, then runs right (RS: 6 steps per block). The right edge grows 4 cells per cycle. Halting needs 0 <A 101 (rule H).
  • The boundaries between block segments hold the bits of a binary counter: which boundary a cycle reaches follows the binary ruler sequence exactly (37,885/37,885 from step 5e4 to 1e10). Segment sizes double per bit (5, 23, 93, 374, 1496, ~6000 blocks at 1e9). Steps 0..~3.6e4 are a different, left-growing counter (init).
  • The counter grows leftwards into a reservoir (10)^m: m goes 58 -> 11 by step 7.5e9, losing 5 then 2 per new top bit. qsim (block 12): the reservoir is gone by 3.4e12, older prefix gadgets are eaten by 7.7e12, and there is no halt to 8e13. The prefix left after that, 1^4 (01)^11 1 (10)^4, is the open question.

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

First pin down the epoch map: exact rules for the boundary bit flips and the top gadget, then a fast abstract simulator to watch 2-3 overflows. Only if the overflows look self-similar: a Longitudinal.v (segRLs) proof with a binary-counter invariant plus an epoch induction. init covers steps 0..~5e4 (the early left counter).

  • ▲Halting is decided by the overflow, which has not been seen. After the reservoir and prefix gadgets are gone (~1e13), the counter must reach blank tape on the left and seed a new reservoir, as it did once at step ~3.6e4. Maybe ~1e16 steps, past what qsim reaches.
  • ▲The inner boundary gadgets (4 states each, in two block phases) and the top gadget are only read off the orbit; RC2, the deep right-end step, has no exact rule.
  • ▲A proof needs an invariant over an unbounded list of segments plus an epoch-level argument; no template within 2 cells.
