Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Every E left record is K(n; a) = 0^inf <E 01^n 0 01^a1 0 ... 0 01^aL 0^inf: a buffer of n pairs (n+1 per cycle), then walls (single extra 0s); true at all 223,095 records to 1e11.
  • Leftward E/F sweeps close open walls and bounce at the first closed one (L3); rightward A/F or C/D phases shift a wall (R5), open it (R6, R7) or turn back (R8, R9, R11). 20 local rules, 6,000/6,000 checks.
  • a_j is exactly 2^(j+1)-periodic in cycles (j = 1..8) and the right end is reached at doubling intervals: a counter hierarchy. Halts seen only from a1 = 0, n odd; on the orbit a1 is 7 or 8. Twin: line 612 (start E).
