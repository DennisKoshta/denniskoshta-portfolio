Validated macro rules (umodel.py)

  • INC (clean → deep → clean, one counter increment):

  • A returns right (D→z, S→s). At each T it bounces: T→S, and B restarts at the unit to its left.
  • So every t a carry passes becomes s and adds one more increment to the part of the word left of it.
  • Validated: random words over z/o/s/t 400/400 on each of 4 seeds (e+2, f−2 checked), plus the lockstep below.
  • EAT, even (last round clean):

Non-halting argument (what is and is not shown)

  • Two observations hold by inspection of the rules. For a word over {z,o,s,t}, INC is total and stays in {z,o,s,t}*, so rounds cannot halt. EAT is safe when the word has no t at the eat. A t in the eaten path, a final t, or a t o/t s in the kept part each lead to D reading 1. Other t contexts, such as t z in the kept part, are not modeled: the epoch model counts any t at an eat as a failure.
  • t units are created only by eats (s→t) and cleared only when a carry passes them. So non-halting reduces to a size invariant: every pending t is cleared within the next epoch.
  • To 1e10 no eat word contains a t (532/532), and t's last at most 49 rounds into an epoch. In the epoch model, clearing all t's never takes more than 1/4.6 of the epoch's increments for N ≥ 100, and 1/8.3 for N ≥ 10^5. Let h be the number of digits below the highest pending t. Then h ≤ log2 N − 3 throughout (≤ log2 N − 4 for N ≥ 10^5, ≤ log2 N − 5 for N ≥ 10^6; the margin is 16× for N ≥ 10^6).
  • The slowest clearings sit just below powers of two: N = 16,361 (2^14), about 65,500 (2^16), 524,283 (2^19) and 4,194,268 (2^22, 131,017 increments). These are the counter's top-digit overflows. The margin still grows there: 4.6×, then 8.3×, then 16×. So the arithmetic does reach the safety question, through N's binary expansion, but only with growing slack. The invariant proof has to handle these overflow epochs.
  • The size bound is genuinely needed (nfuzz.py, 4 epochs, 300 samples per band, 2 seeds):
  • Real epoch states with N replaced by a random value in [N/2, 2N]: 600/600 safe.
  • N in [3|W|, 10|W|]: 13/600 fail. N in [|W|, 3|W|]: 42/600 fail. N in [1, |W|]: 87/600 fail.
  • Random z/o/s words with N ≤ 4000: 71/600 fail.
  • Every failure is a t still present at the eat.
  • Cross-check by direct TM simulation (nfuzz_direct.py, 120 real states with N in [1, 3|W|], 3 epochs): all 3 model failures halt in the TM, and the 117 model passes do not halt within the step budget. So these failures are real halts, not artefacts of the conservative model.

Open. - The inductive proof that the pending-t height h stays below log2(N) − 2 (equivalently, that the t's clear within ~N/4 increments). It must follow from how the eat word is built: the overflow digits form a spacer-free top block, and the spacers live in the low part. It is not derived yet. - The init segment before step 241. - Step counts per macro rule, which are not derived (the rules are validated on configurations).

Balls-and-gaps view (a restatement of the validated INC/EAT rules)

  • The non-digit units s/t are balls. A ball's gap is the number of digits (z/o) to its right. A t is a pending increment of weight 2^gap. INC adds exactly 1 to (digit value + sum over t of 2^gap).
  • Lemma A (by hand, from INC). A t at gap h with digit value d below it turns into s within 2^h − d INCs. Each INC either passes it, or raises the digit value below it by at least 1 (a lower t that is passed adds 2^h' through its bounce), and that value stays below 2^h.
  • Lemma B (by hand, from INC/EAT): gaps never grow.
  • A ball's identity is its distance from the right end: INC inserts only at the left, the eat drops a suffix (even) or nothing (odd), and the shift is unit-wise on t-free words.
  • After an eat, a ball's gap is the number of z units of U* to its right. That is at most the old gap, minus 1 at an even eat.
  • Checked: 20,596,783 ball-eats on the trajectory to N = 1e7 (firstt.py, results/firstt_1e7.txt), and 743,436 from random starts (reduce_rand.py, seeds 11 and 12). 0 violations.
  • Lemma C (first t-epoch suffices).
  • Each o unit of the eaten word U*_k becomes a ball (an s) at gap h0 = #z to its right. It stays inert during epoch k+1 and is a t from eat k+1 on.
  • Its gap never grows (Lemma B) and the INC count I_k never shrinks, because Nold strictly increases.
  • So it suffices that 2^h1 <= I_{k+2}, where h1 is its gap in W_{k+2}. The balls of the start word szt are checked directly.
  • Lemma D (by hand): the general case. Unless V_k is a power of two and eats k and k+1 are both odd, every ball born at eat k has 2^h1 <= V_k / 2.
  • The top 1-bit gives h0 <= bitlen − popcount − [even], and every other 1-bit gives h0 <= bitlen − 2.
  • V*_k = T_k + I_k + [odd], where T_k is the total value at the epoch start: digit value plus the sum over t of 2^gap.
  • So these births are safe whenever T_k + 1 <= I_k.
  • Trajectory to 1e7: 7,454,109 births. The maximum of 2^h1/I is 0.286 (at N = 13), and 0.250 for N >= 1024 (at N = 16,359).
  • Random starts: 188,785 births, and none breaks 2^h1 <= V*/2.
  • Lemma E (sketch): the special case, V*_k = 2^m with both eats odd.
  • Exactly one ball is born, at gap m, and T_{k+1} is the full potential of W_k's balls.
  • The overflow past 2^m is Δ = (s-ball potential of W_k) − (digit value of W_k) + floor((c0_k + e_k − 1)/2). Δ >= 1 when W_k's top unit is an s.
  • So h1 = Z_m(Δ + 1) <= m − 1, provided Δ + 1 < 2^m.
  • I_{k+2} = 2^m − T_k + (c0_k + e_k)/2 >= 2^(m−1), by T_k + 1 <= I_k.
  • Observed: 3 special eats on the trajectory to 1e7, none after N = 8,139 (N = 11, 238, 8,139). The maximum ratio is 0.33, or 0.126 at N = 8,139.
  • Random: 414 special cases (379 of them with T + 1 <= I), 0 violations.
  • The formulas for I_{k+2} and Δ match exactly on 300/300 random special cases (special_check.py, results/special_check_21.txt).

So everything rests on R1: T_k + 1 <= I_k at every epoch, plus Δ + 1 < 2^m in the special case. - Observed: R1 holds at all 808,539 epochs to N = 1e7 (results/firstt_1e7.txt). - T/I peaks at 0.875 for N in [16, 32), 0.41 for N >= 64, and 0.284 in the 2^13 octave. It stays <= 0.13 for N >= 2^14 to 1e7 (results/binned_1e7.txt).

Why R1 did not close

  • The recurrence. T_{k+1} = a + sum over balls of 2^(Z_gap(V*_k) − [even]), where Z_g(x) counts the zeros among the low g bits of x.
  • When V*_k = 2^m + δ with small δ, Z_gap = gap for every ball above bitlen(δ). The whole ball potential P then moves into T.
  • P itself grows at each eat by sum over 1-bits of 2^(zeros below), which is about 2^(m − popcount δ).
  • Crossings are long. V* ≈ N/2 + T moves by only about e/2 per epoch, so crossing 2^m takes many epochs. At N = 16,0xx..16,4xx that is about 100 epochs at 2^13. P and T pile up there: P/N reaches 0.41 and T/I reaches 0.28.
  • The crude bounds (Z_g <= g, and sum over 1-bits <= x) only give T_{k+1} <= T_k + P_s(W_k) at odd eats, which does not close.
  • Native search: size summaries alone are not inductive. Candidates: Ja = {T+1 <= I, every t clears}, Jb = Ja + {every ball 2^gap <= (N−1)/2}, Jc = Jb + {P <= N/2}, Jd = everything at 1/4.
  • Uniform random states: all four hold over one epoch, apart from 1/2000 breaks of Ja and Jb (randinv.py).
  • Random states tuned so that V* = 2^m + δ (δ in −2..2, 4, 8, 16) break them within 3 epochs: Ja 44/2000, Jb 601/2000, Jc 668/2000, Jd 1614/2000 (results/randinv2_2.txt).
  • The real trajectory itself breaks Jb and Jc at its two post-special epochs: 2^D/N = 0.538 at N = 238 and 0.503 at N = 8,140.
  • From tuned Ja states, 1,497/1,500 stay safe for 20 epochs. The other 3 hit a t at the eat in epoch 2, and all 3 have a top ball with 2^14 >= N/2 or P >= 0.9N, which is off the reachable range (results/randsafe_3.txt).
  • So an invariant has to carry the structure of the crossing, not only sizes.

The arithmetic it would need: a crossing lemma. As V passes 2^m (V = 2^m + δ_k, where δ_k is driven by e_k and T_k), the sum over balls of 2^(Z_gap(2^m + δ_k)) must stay <= c·2^m with c < 1/2. The loop is self-limiting: a larger T means a larger δ, so more ones in the low bits, smaller gaps and a smaller T. Nothing here proves that. - A possible route is scale invariance. Near a crossing only δ and the low part of the word matter. For m >= m0, a finite crossing automaton over (δ mod 2^r, low word) might then be checkable natively, with the epoch model covering m < m0. This was not attempted (budget).

Still open: R1 (with the Δ + 1 < 2^m side condition), the crossing lemma behind it, the init segment before step 241, and step counts per macro rule. proof.md was not written, because the invariant did not close.
