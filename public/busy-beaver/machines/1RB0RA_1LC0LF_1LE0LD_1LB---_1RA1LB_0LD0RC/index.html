Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Tape = L | bell 1 (110)^e (010)^f 1 0^inf, e+f = N per epoch; each round e+1, f-1 and L (binary counter, 3-cell digits) gains 1/2.
  • At f = 0 the bell eats the low 3e'-2 cells of L: f' = N, e' = 1 (odd last round) or 2..47 (even); L then mixes digit kinds.
  • Exact so far: sweeps R1-R4 and INC2 on pure counters; open: carry/eat on mixed words. tm108 cert: 9/12 hold (HL2, HR2, HE2 fail).

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

Deepen before any proof: (1) derive the carry automaton on mixed digit words and the eat automaton (predict e' from L); (2) find a language for L closed under INC2/ROUND and RESET, with a size bound N >= c|L|, and closure-fuzz it; (3) then a BECv1-style file with an Inductive digit list, or a CTL/RWL certificate if the language is regular.

  • ▲The eat size e' depends on the counter's digits at the reset (arithmetic feedback); if the reachable L words are not regular, a hand proof is hard.
  • ▲Real left words with a too-small bell (N < |L|) do halt (2/300 replay starts), so the invariant needs a quantitative N vs |L| bound.
  • ▲Sibling #29 (F0 = 0LB) looks similar but its trajectory differs (1e8: span -1917..265 vs ours -1929..263); a shared proof is not guaranteed.
