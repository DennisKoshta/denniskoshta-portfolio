Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Each visit (right record in C) maps 0^inf L 1^n 0^g C> 0^inf to 0^inf inc(L) 1^(n-2) 0^(g+4) C> 0^inf: the head stripes the block going left, adds 1 to the counter L, zigzags back and erases the block's last 2 cells (early in a phase, filler cells of L can merge into the block).
  • inc is a binary +1: the carry depth follows the ruler sequence (27,084/27,124 visits). Digits are 1 or 2 cells, separated by 00 gaps or 1-runs the carry passes; the layout is rebuilt at each reset and migrates early in a phase (the open part).
  • Reset at n=2: L0 0 1^2 0^g C> -> L0 1^(g+6) C> with g = 4(visits-1), so the block about doubles. All 12 resets to 2.5e9 hit a bit-0 = 0 visit; 35/36 off-sync variants leave an odd pad and halt next visit. Not a cryptid: plain +1, linear timer.
