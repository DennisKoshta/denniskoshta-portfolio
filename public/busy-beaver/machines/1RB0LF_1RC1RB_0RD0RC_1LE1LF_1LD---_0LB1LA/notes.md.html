  • Simulated to 1.2e10 steps with no halt (tools/sim.py C core, work/sim_1.2e10.jsonl): span -175..114,911 and 114,968 nonzeros.   • Visit = the run between two right records in state C. Every visit starts as 0^inf L 1^m 0^g C> 0^inf. 1. C0/D0/E0 fill the pad 0^g (plus 2 new cells) with 1s, walking left. g must be even, or else E reads the block's last 1 and halts (rule H). 2. F/A sweep left over the block and leave stripes (01)^k (rule S). 3. The sweep enters the counter L, carries, and turns. 4. B/C zigzag back right and turn the stripes solid again (rule Z, 10 steps per 2 cells). 5. C erases the run of 1s at the right end, then reads a fresh cell: a new record 2 cells further right. The net effect: the block loses 2 cells at its right end, the pad gains 4, and the counter gets +1.
  • Counter. The carry depth of each visit follows the ruler sequence v2(k+c)+1 (scripts/ruler_check.py). That holds for 27,084 of 27,124 visits to 2.5e9 steps, with one offset c per phase. The 40 misses come early in a phase, where a digit migrates (a filler zero becomes part of a digit). Digit cells per phase come from scripts/counter_check.py, work/phase10_visits.txt and work/allvisits_2.5e9.txt.
