The tape: alternation plus extra symbols ("pair frame")

The tape is the background 1010... with isolated defects 00 (an extra 0) and 11 (an extra 1). Read from the leftmost 1 greedily in 10 pairs: 0^inf (10)^n0 x1 (10)^n1 x2 ... xk (10)^nk [end], x_i in {0,1}. Both sweeps complement every cell they cross (A> (10)^n -> (01)^n, (10)^n <B -> <B (01)^n), so a region crossed twice is restored and only the defects move. scripts/pf.c prints this list at every left/right record in state A (LA / RA records).

Rightward sweeps read pairs alternately in A-mode (A1 A0) and B-mode (B1 E0). Window lemmas (exact finite runs, scripts/val_W.py, 6/6; shifts 120/120): | | window | steps | meaning | |---|---|---|---| | W1 | A> 100 -> <C 101 | 5 | A-mode pair, extra 0: turn, extra becomes 1 in place | | W2 | B> 100 -> 011 B> | 3 | B-mode pair, extra 0: pass (mode parity flips) | | W3 | A> 1011 -> <C 1010 | 7 | A-mode pair, extra 1: turn, extra becomes 0 one pair right | | W4 | B> 101 -> 010 A> | 3 | B-mode pair, extra 1: pass | | W5 | 0100 <B -> <C 1101 | 4 | leftward sweep passes 00, which lands 2 cells left as 11 (C0 D1 F0) | | W6 | 110 <B -> 010 A> | 5 | leftward sweep turns at 11 | W5 is the only place a sweep makes C read 0 next to a 1; with 11 directly left of the 00 (1100 <B) it is the halting window.

Candidate rules (validated)

E, eat bounce (RA record to RA record):

0^inf L 0 (10)^(a+1) 0 0 [(10)^q_i 0]_{i=1..k} (10)^c A> 0^inf
  --> 0^inf L 0 (10)^a 0 0 [(10)^q_i 0]_i (10)^(c+2) A> 0^inf
steps 4c + 4 sum q_i + 2k + 12,   needs a >= 1, every q_i even, c even

The 000 marker walks left 2 cells per bounce, eating the segment to its left and paying 2 pairs to the right end; L is never read. 200/200 random instances (p 1-20, a 1-30, k 0-4, q_i, c even), step formula exact (residual 0). Case split is sharp: with any q_i or c odd it fails 0/n in every one of 24 parity classes (val_E2.py).

L, left-end bounce (LA record to LA record) = a 4-state carry transducer read left to right on (n_i mod 2, x_{i+1}) ('E' = the end):

S : (even,1) n0+=3, x1:=0, n1-=1, STOP      (odd,1) n0+=1 -> T      (odd,0) x1:=1 -> Z+
T : (odd,1)  n_i+=2, x:=0, n_{i+1}-=1, STOP
Z+: (even,0) n_i+=1, x:=1 -> Z-    (even,1) n_i+=3, n_{i+1}-=1, STOP
    (odd,1) -> Q                   (odd,E) n_i+=4, STOP
Z-: (even,0) n_i-=1, x:=1 -> Z+    (even,1) -> Z-
    (odd,1)  n_i+=2, x:=0, n_{i+1}-=1, STOP          (even,E) n_i+=2, STOP
Q : (even,1) -> Q                  (even,E) n_i+=4, STOP

Undefined entries (e.g. Z-(odd,E)) are where the bounce runs to the right end instead (an LA>RA move). Validation: 900/900 random constructed LA configs (3 seeds; n_i in [2,30], [1,30], and [0,30] incl. adjacent extras) and every one of the 14,266 real LA>LA moves to 1e9 (30,720 to 1e10). Macro picture (not understood)

  • At eat-phase starts (58 to 1e10, history_rows.json) the list has 2-13 separators; it does not grow steadily. Tail motifs recur (... 8 |4 3, 44 |4 17 |3 13 |3 1 |3 3, 130 |4 113) while the left digits grow; e.g. from 4.44e6 to 5.62e6 the first six digits move by +180, +90, -30, +30, -10, +10 (powers of 3 per pair), so it looks like a hierarchical counter over digit pairs.
  • Parity law observed but broken: at most eat starts the digits left of 000 are even (gap form) and those right of it odd, but 9 of 33 eat starts to 1e9 (17 of 60 to 1e10) break it (two 000 at once, an even digit right of the marker, an odd digit left of it after an inner eat phase).
  • Not cryptid-like so far: every rule is linear with parity case splits, and nothing multiplies. But each branch is chosen by parities of large transferred numbers, so a pseudo-random parity sequence is not excluded.

Funded stage (2026-10-10, rules_stage)

Run 2026-10-11, ~2 h, light compute (C tracker 1 min per orbit to 1e10; everything else seconds). Files: scripts/rules/.

Tools. rec.c prints every A-record of a run as canonical pair-frame tokens n0 x1 n1 ... xk nk (word from the first 1 to the last 1 (LA) or to the head (RA), read greedily as 10 pairs). Checked: at LA records the head is on the 0 just left of the first 1 and the last visited cell is the last 1; at RA records the first visited cell is the first 1 and the gap is 0 or 1. So a record is fully given by (side, tokens), and oracle.c runs the TM exactly from any such constructed record to the next A-record. model.py holds the rules, validate.py tests them on random canonical configs against the oracle, absim.py iterates the rules alone from the first record and compares every record with the TM.

Rules (all in model.py; random validation: 2,000/2,000 each, seeds 6 and 7, validation_final.txt). - L (observe stage, unchanged table) with its domain now stated: the chain stops inside the list and no segment up to the one after the stop is 0. Outside it L was wrong: in all four orbits every chain that reaches the end entry runs off to the right (LA>RA), never stays LA, so the end entries Z+(odd,E), Q(even,E), Z-(even,E) are not used; zero segments make merges. - R6, end rule: w 1 A> -> w 0 (10) A>, 6 steps (local window, exact). - RE, right-end eat bounce, generalizes E. Read right to left from c = n_k: c even: +2, state P0; c odd: +1, state P1. In P0: even 0, odd -1 -> P1. In P1: even +2, odd +3 -> P0. It stops in P0 at the first turning point. At a 000 marker the segment left of it absorbs the balance, so the total change is +1 pair. At an extra 1 reached through even segments only (rule E1), the segment right of the 1 absorbs the balance and the 1 becomes 0. Domain: the stop is reached in P0, no segment on the way is or becomes 0, the eaten segment is not n0 and stays >= 0 (if it becomes 0, the extra on its left is 0). E is the all-even case. The 76- and 502-step "first eat bounces" are odd-segment cases, e.g. 15 0 9 0 0 0 3 0 1 -> 15 0 6 0 0 0 6 0 2. The RA records with one extra 1 are E1 cases. - F, full return (RA>LA): no marker and every extra 0: every extra becomes 1, the last segment +2, end 1. - FM, full return through a double marker 0000: (10)^a 0 0 0 (10)^q 0 (10)^r ... 0 (10)^c -> (10)^(a-1) 1 (10)^(q+3) 1 (10)^(r-1) 1 ... 1 (10)^(c+2) 1, or c+1 when r is c. Domain: a >= 2, q and every later segment even. - LR, left-end run-off (LA>RA, partial): the chain S(odd,0) then Z+/Z- over (even,0) segments, +1/-1 as in L, but the extras stay 0. Z- passes (even,1) and sets it to 0. Ending (a): Z- on an odd last segment gives an RA record with end 1. Ending (b): Z+ on (odd,1) adds 1 to that segment, makes the extra on its left 1 and every other extra 0, then (even,1) segments up to an even last segment; the end 1 is used up.
