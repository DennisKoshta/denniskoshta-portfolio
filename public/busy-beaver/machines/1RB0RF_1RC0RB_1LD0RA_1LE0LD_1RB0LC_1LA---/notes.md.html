The defect model (dmodel.py): 8 local rules, exact on every tape

  • A defect is an edge e (between cells e and e+1) whose two cells are equal. Both 0^inf ends are chains of 00 defects (every edge there is one), so the tape edges need no rules of their own.
  • The head carries a phase phi in Z/4:
  • leftward cycle: D1 at phi, D0 at phi-1, E1 at phi-2, C0 at phi-3;
  • rightward cycle: B0 at phi, C1 at phi+1, A0 at phi+2, B1 at phi+3 (cells mod 4).
  • At a defect the head sees d = (e - phi) mod 4. As the head reads it, d even means 00 and d odd means 11. D is the defect set and ^ is symmetric difference.
head d=0 d=1 d=2 d=3
leftward pass, phi+1 turn at cell e (C-turn): D ^= {e-1,e} turn at cell e (E-turn): D ^= {e-1,e} pass, phi-1
rightward turn at cell e+1: D ^= {e,e+1} wiggle C1 A1 F0 A0: D ^= {e,e+2}, then e+2 crossed by d=3 (net: defect +2 cells, phi+1, +2 steps) pass, phi-1 pass, phi+1
  • Turns keep phi. Sweeps complement every cell they cross, which leaves defects where they are, so D changes only at turns and wiggles.
  • Halting happens only through a rightward d=1 defect with another defect at e+1 (F reads 1, C> 111).
  • Derived by hand from the table. Validated against direct simulation on random tapes: random defect density 2-35%, 0^inf ends, a random sweep-phase head, up to 60 events each with step indices. Phase rule per trip (level 2), bits.py + check_trip_rule.py

  • Labels. At a right-edge turn, scan the defects right to left with the head's phase:
  • bit 1 if d is 0 or 3 (the head passes);
  • bit 0 if d is 1 or 2 (it would turn);
  • after each defect, phi += +1 for a 00 and -1 for an 11.
  • Labels are z/o (00/11) plus the bit.
  • (L) The leftward head turns at the rightmost bit-0 defect. That defect moves 1 cell left and changes type. With the same phase arriving from the right, its label becomes z0 -> o0 or o0 -> z1. In labels anchored at the left end it becomes z0 -> o1 or o0 -> z0, and every bit to its right flips, which is a binary-counter carry through the trailing 1s (derived from the rules, not separately tested).
  • (R) On the way back, delta starts at 0 and flips 0 <-> 2 at every defect crossed:
  • a z1 met with delta=2 wiggles (the defect moves +2 cells; these are the "+2 resets" of observe);
  • an o1 met with delta=0 turns the head left again (a multi-turn trip);
  • otherwise the head reaches the right edge.
  • (L') If no explicit defect has bit 0, the head enters the left chain. It passes edge lo-1 with d=0 and turns at cell lo-2. The chain start moves 3 cells left and lo-1 stays as a z1 defect, which the return crosses first. This is the left record (3 cells, as observed).
  • Check on the blank run: 93,231/93,232 trip segments to 2e10 match (turn cell, every wiggle cell, and how the segment ends). The one miss is the first left-chain entry at step 1,070 (start-up; the chain is entered with d=2, a 2-cell record). The 11 later chain entries all have d=0.

  • The physical unit is a digit block: one digit defect, then m separators (m = 1 or 3), all separators at one residue c mod 4. Consecutive blocks shift c by 2.
  • Examples at record 21 (absolute cells): (-35; -34) m=1, (-29; -28, -24, -20), (-15; -10, 6, 30), (75; 108, 148, 184), (1157; 3890, 12170, 37082). The fossils are the separator gaps of the later blocks.
  • Left of the chain sits one block with 2 separators (lo+25: (25; 26, 30) relative to lo). It and the m=1 blocks left of it are the left-end counter; not analysed here.

2. Eight block states

Offsets from the block base b (b = c - 1 mod 4); for m = 1 only the first two entries exist, and U2 = D0.

state defects value
U0, U1, U2 (-s, g1, g2, g3) s
W0, W1 (-2, g1+1, g2+3-w, g3) w (W2 would be D0)
D2, D1, D0 (-2, g1+1, g2+1, g3+t) 2 - t
  • U: the digit moves left of the separators (the old "2nd defect at offsets +1/0/-1"). D: the last defect is the digit. W: a block in transit from U to D (the old "short excursions").
  • Every chain configuration in the run reads as a word over these 8 states: 573,000+ right-edge snapshots, intervals 16-26, none unreadable (read_blocks8.py, results/chain_words_readable_16_26.txt).
  • The words always have the shape D^a [W] U^b (left to right). A whole interval of words: results/chain_words_interval18.txt.

3. Mixed chain lemma (all k)

Value of a word = ternary number of the block values, least significant digit on the right. A block is full at U2 or D0.

A visit = the head enters the chain from the right at phase c_K (separator residue of the last block). Claim: - Not all full: the value goes up by 1 and the head leaves to the right. - The turned block (rightmost non-full) moves to its successor: U0>U1>U2, W0>W1>D0, D2>D1>D0. - The full blocks right of it reset to value 0. After a U or W turn they are all U2 and become U0. After a D turn, the D0 blocks become D2, the first U2 becomes W0 (a new wave) and the other U2 become U0. - Exit phase: c_K + 0 if the turned block is D and the last block is D afterwards, else c_K + 2. - All full: the head leaves to the left with the chain unchanged (carry out), at phase c_1 + 2. - Carry back from the left (all full, entry at c_1 + 2): every block resets to value 0 with the D-turn labels.

Proof shape: the 11 single-block entries it uses for m=3 (7 for m=1) are the same for every gap choice and never collide (block_table.py, 206 + 54 gap choices, results/block_table.txt). The phases then compose: a full block passed leftward exits at c+2 = the standard entry of the block on its left; a U/W turn hands c+0 to the right (resets U2 -> U0, each again handing c+0); a D turn hands c+2 (D0 -> D2 keeps c+2; the first U2 -> W0 switches to c+0). That is an induction on the word, and it keeps the shape D^a [W] U^b.

Checks: - Synthetic, compressed TM: every word of the right shape, K <= 5, 0-2 leading m=1 blocks, random gaps including the tight ones: 16,246/16,246 (side, labels, exit phase) (mixed_chain.py, results/mixed_chain.txt). - Blank run, every visit, intervals 16-27: 262,442/262,442 visits from the right enter at c_K and match; 972/972 carry-backs from the left enter at c_1 + 2 and match; no other kind of entry occurs (check_mixed_visits.py, results/mixed_visits_16_27.txt). - An earlier two-frame version (pure U = up, pure D = down) is in chain_lemma.py / check_chain_visits.py. It is a special case.

Consequences: - Between two carry-outs the chain receives exactly 3^K visits. - No collision happens inside the chain, so no halt can start there. The adjacent pairs of the old (I2a) are the U0 blocks with g1 = 1; the lemma covers them. The halting question moves entirely to the two boundaries. - At every left record the chain reads as all-U0 under some grouping (a D-chain regroups as a U-chain). This is why the record words were P U^u T mod 4.

4. The right boundary is a finite automaton per visit

  • Right of the validated chain: zero to two more blocks (it depends on the record class), then W (the right end of X), Y and the right edge.
  • Define the boundary state at each chain exit as: the exit phase class, the residues mod 4 of every defect right of the chain, hi mod 4, and which defects sit within 3 cells of hi.
  • Result (boundary_dfa.py, results/boundary_dfa_16_27.txt, intervals 16-27): at most 10 boundary states per interval. Each state always leads to the same (trips until the next visit, W move, hi move), except at most one transition per interval (none in intervals 18, 21, 24, 27), and that transition is an epoch event (section 6).
  • Two regimes, each an exact 4-cycle. Interval 21 for example:
  • first regime (exit class 2): trips (9, 9, 10, 8), W (0, -1, +1, 0): X frozen;
  • second regime (exit class 1): trips (7, 10, 5, 8), W (-2, -5, -1, -4): W eats 3 cells per chain visit.
  • Which regime freezes, grows or eats, and the cells per eating visit (3 in intervals 21/22, 9 in intervals 19/25), depend on the record class.

3. V1 and the visit count, derived from the chain lemma

  • After a record the chain is one m=1 block followed by K-1 blocks with m=3, all at value 0. A suffix that starts at W0 U0^(j-1) runs through:
  • 2 x 3^(j-1) visits to reach D0 U0^(j-1);
  • 3^(j-1) visits to reach D2 W0 U0^(j-2);
  • then the (j-1)-suffix.
  • So T(1) = 2 and T(j) = 3^j + T(j-1), which gives T(K) = (3^(K+1) - 5)/2. The leading m=1 block plays the role of W (U0 -> U1 -> D0).
  • Exit phase 0 happens exactly when the whole word is D (lemma), and an all-D word stays all-D. So the first T(K) visits exit at phase 2 and all later visits at phase 0.
  • One carry-out happens per 3^K visits, so a record interval has 81 x 3^K visits.
  • v1_count.py checks this on the lemma for K = 2..9, over the full 81 carry-outs.

6. The record is a regrouping (regroup.py, timeline check)

  • The record visit runs: carry-out at all-D0, left episode 81 (left record), carry back to all D2, exit at phase 0.

  • one new left digit;
  • at the right end, by class, the chain drops 1 defect (r5 -> r0, r3 -> r4), takes in 3 defects from the right region (r0 -> r1, r2 -> r3, r4 -> r5), or drops 5 (r1 -> r2).

7. The record step closes on the right too (glue.py, link.py)

  • Symbolically, for all w (link.py) the step works as follows:
  • the tail of the class-r end vector (pair, X, Y, edge) is carried over unchanged to the record exit; the record visit stays left of the old B, and only the inert prefix is re-based;
