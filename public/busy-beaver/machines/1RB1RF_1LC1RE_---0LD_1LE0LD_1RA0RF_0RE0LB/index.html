Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Tape = 0^inf (110)^a 0 (011)^n, then a binary counter Q at the right end (3-cell digits, LSB nearest the head).
  • One trip = zig-zag from the right end to the a|n boundary and back: a+1, n-1. Q gets +1 every 2nd trip.
  • At n = 1 or 2: reset (two full sweeps), a restarts at 2 and L = a+n grows. Trips ignore Q; the reset ignores a.
