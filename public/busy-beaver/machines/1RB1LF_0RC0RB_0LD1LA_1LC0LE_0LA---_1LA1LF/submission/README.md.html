1RB1LF_0RC0RB_0LD1LA_1LC0LE_0LA---_1LA1LF never halts

  • Machine: 1RB1LF_0RC0RB_0LD1LA_1LC0LE_0LA---_1LA1LF, a BB(6) holdout. It is on every holdout list since June 2024 (line 240 of the 778-machine list, line 224 of the 740-machine list). busycoq's verify/ has no proof of it (Eqv_v3.v, TM493 only relates its mirror to another machine).
  • Class: "Bell eats counter" (spreadsheet Results row 1015, no notes or claims). It is BECv5's TM1/TM2 with a different F1 (1LF instead of 0RB / 1LB).
  • Result: Lemma nonhalt: ~halts tm c0. in Coq 8.20.1 against unmodified busycoq BB6 (commit 0940bb9c), importing only Individual62. Print Assumptions nonhalt is closed (no axioms). It compiles in 65.8 s with -native-compiler yes, and coqchk re-checks it in 29.6 s (check_kill.txt).

Mechanism

  • Bounce counter. The tape is 0^inf w <A 1 (10)^(1+n) 0 (10)^m 0^inf, BECv5's w <| R n m. Each bounce applies one map phi to the left word w (read as two interleaved binary numbers, even and odd cells) and moves one 10 from the n-block to the m-block.
  • Two encodings. At n = 0 the right side eats the low end of w and refills n. The left word is mostly a binary counter that alternates between a standard encoding (BECv5's LC0) and a packed one, where the top two bits share one digit pair.
  • Where halting is. Because F1 = 1LF skips 1s, an overflow can leave the word dirty for a while. The machine halts only if an overflow finds the lowest 1 of phi(w) on an odd cell with 01 or 11 above it, which can only happen in a dirty word.

How the proof works

  • Exact rules: the left step phi (as an inductive relation on the pair (e, o), proved against the machine by mutual induction), the right step, and the overflow cases.
  • One sweep lemma for phi on E/O words gives the increments of both counters and the dirty orbits. From it come eleven macro rules (S0–S3 from a standard state, P0–P4 from a packed one), each running from one clean overflow state (typ, N, m) to the next.
  • The invariant I: 1 ≤ m ≤ N plus linear bounds on N in terms of m, chosen by the parities of N and m. Under I exactly one macro rule applies and none meets a halting overflow (coverage), and every rule maps I into itself (closure). The run reaches (std, 1, 1) after 32 steps, and progress_nonhalt_cond with P := I gives nonhalt.

Template and what changed

Adapted from busycoq's verify/BECv5.v, Module TM1 / Module TM2, which differ from this machine only in F1. Kept: the right side R n m and its right step, the standard counter, and the architecture (big-step lemma per rule, start by esx, progress_nonhalt_cond). New: E/O words with phi as the relations PA/PF, the lemma Sweep, the packed counter, eleven macro rules in place of TM1's one and TM2's two, and the invariant I with its coverage and closure arithmetic. The file has 1,217 lines, every lemma top level.
