The configuration family

K(w, n, m) = 0^inf w <A 1 (10)^(1+n) 0 (10)^m 0^inf. A reads the last cell of the left word w. This is the template's w <| R n m (BECv5 TM1/TM2 use exactly this right side R n m).

Blank start: K('',0,0) at step 5, K('',1,0) at step 14, then K('1',0,1) at step 32.

Rules (all validated by direct simulation with tools/sim.py, scripts/validate_rules.py)

  • R1 bounce (n >= 1): K(w,n,m) -> K(phi(w), n-1, m+1). The head leaves the boundary, does one left step phi on w and one right step on R n m, then comes back to the same cell.
  • phi(w): if the last cell is 0, set it to 1. Otherwise take the last adjacent pair 00 (0^inf counts), set its left cell to 1 and every cell to its right to 0. (A reading 1 goes to F. F skips 1s leftwards because F1 = 1LF, and on a 0 writes 1 and goes back to A. A on 0 writes 1 and B erases back to the boundary.)
  • TM1 (F1 = 0RB) and TM2 (F1 = 1LB) instead stop the scan at the first 11 they read. That single cell is the only difference.
  • Overflow (n = 0): let f = phi(w) = x 1 0^q.
  • R2 q even: K(w,0,m) -> K(x, m+1+q/2, 0) (the boundary moves q+1 cells left). This is the template's ROv.
  • R3 q odd, x = x' 00 (or x empty): K(w,0,m) -> K(x', 0, m+(q+3)/2) (a reset: the right side swallows 2 more cells).
  • R4 q odd, x ends in 01 or 11: halts.
  • R5 q odd, x ends in 10: nested eating; no closed form (in random tests it sometimes halts).
  • phi^j accelerated (rules.phij): the cells below the lowest 1 at an odd offset form a plain binary counter on their even offsets. When it fills, the next step lands in the part above. Validated against iterated phi.

Counters and big steps

  • std(N): digits 01 = 1, 00 = 0, least significant digit next to the head (= the template's LC0 N = BinInc <[0;1] N).
  • pk(N) (packed, N >= 2): the top two bits share one pair 1t, and the rest are std digits. phi = +1 on both, and the encoding never changes under phi.
  • With N = (2k+1)2^i - 1 and b = 1+i+m, L = bitlen(k):
  • std(N), m -> pk(2k+b), b when k is a power of 2 or 2k+b >= 2^(L+1); std(b), b when k = 0; otherwise a dirty word phi^b(std(k)0).
  • pk(N), m -> std(2r+b), b with k = 2^(L-1)+r, r > 0, when 2r+b >= 2^L; std(k+b), b when k is a power of 2; std(b+1), b+1 when k = 0 (an all-ones counter: reset first); otherwise dirty phi^b(pk(k)0).
  • So the counter alternates std / pk every cycle (Fig counter_rows). Compare TM1: N' = b-2k, invariant a <= 1+b; TM2: N' = b, invariant a = b.
  • A dirty word is the transient after the overflow left the counter shifted by one cell (H0 = H followed by an extra 0). The orbit of std(k)0 becomes pk(2k+j) at j0 = 2^(L+1)-2k, and the orbit of pk(k)0 becomes std at j0 = 2^L-2r. Before j0, the overflow sees a dirty word. 2.8% of overflows do (473,792 of 17,000,000 cycles).

Where halting could come from

  • Clean counters always overflow with q even (or the harmless all-ones reset). Only dirty overflows can have q odd.
  • In a dirty orbit, q is odd exactly at the landings on odd offsets, about log2(k) points per orbit:
  • R points, ending in 00: resets, harmless. Hit once from blank (cycle 27, step 199,243). Near misses at even distances 2, 4, 6 later. When an R hit is forced at 5 near misses, the dynamics recovers and runs 100k more cycles with no danger.
  • N points (10) and H points (01/11): for std orbits N sits at value 3·2^(L-1), i.e. b+1 = j0-2^(L-1). For pk orbits H sits at b+1 = 2^(L-1)-2r. Hitting one needs b < 2^(L-1) <= k, i.e. N > 2m and N low in its octave.
  • Measured from blank, abstract rules (validated against the direct C simulation: all 1,002 overflow configurations to 1e10 steps match):
  • 17,000,000 cycles (m = 4.25e7, about 5e22 TM steps by the fit steps ~ 0.68 m^3): no H/N hit. Only 1 dirty reset and 4 all-ones resets.
  • Closest approach over the first 6,000,000 cycles: H points >= 0.287·m away, N points >= 0.376·m away. The overflow always comes after them, a stable fraction of m, not a shrinking gap. The margin mu (distance to the H/N value in units of 2^(L-1)) stays >= 0.40 (pk) and >= 0.61 (std) over 400k cycles.
  • Reading: the separation looks like an inequality invariant on (type, N, m) plus the dirty state, not pseudo-random luck. The invariant is not found yet. Simple bounds hold empirically (std: N < 2m; pk: N - m <= 2^floor(log2 N); m >= 0.44·2^floor(log2 N)) but do not exclude the H point by themselves.

Cryptid check (ground rule 6)

  • The abstract system has halting branches (R4, and R5 sometimes), so non-halting rests on avoiding specific points.
  • Avoidance is by a margin proportional to m (>= 0.29·m), not by a 1-in-N chance per cycle. Treating the hits as random (about 0.03 dirty overflows per cycle, about log2 m danger points per orbit of length about m) predicts a few hits within 17M cycles (25 octaves of m). None occurred, and the gap never shrinks.
  • Verdict: not cryptid-like on current evidence. The invariant still has to be found and proved.

What was done

  • Dirty orbits in closed form. The T-count of an orbit is 2·(O-cell barrier) + (E-cell counter), and phi adds 1 to it.
  • gen 1, std(k)0: odd landings at T_g = 2((k>>g<<g)+2^g), one for each 0-bit g of k above its lowest 1. The landing is N if bit g+1 is set, else R. The top landing at 2^(L+1) is a reset with empty x.
  • gen 1, pk(k)0 (r = k - 2^(L-1)): the same with r, the H point at T = 2^(L-1) (g = L-2), and an even merge at 2^L.
  • gen 2, at a dirty overflow that is not a landing: x has E-bits K = k_s>>u and O-bits g = v>>(u+1). It merges into a clean counter at T_x = 2^(lowK+1).
  • closedform.py gives one clean-to-clean step B covering 17 cases: clean, k = 0 / power of 2, top reset, pk merge-at-overflow, gen-1 N/H/R, gen-2 g=0 / merged / merge-at-overflow.
  • Exact parity fact found: in clean steps N' - m' is 2k, 2r or 0. So N = m (mod 2) except after pk with k = 1, which gives N = m+1, and rare gen-2 g=0 steps, which give N = m+3. In the class N = m, i = 0 can never occur twice in a row. This parity was the missing correlation. A cell closure that treats i as free leaks into the H region (signed H margin -0.86·m). With parity-tagged cells it closes at once and keeps the margins (H >= 0.31 m, N >= 0.39 m).
  • Invariant I (inv3.py):
  • always 1 <= m <= N;
  • parity class (N = m mod 2): std even 2N <= 3m+6, std odd N <= 2m+3, pk even N <= 2m+3, pk odd 2N <= 5m+6;
  • broken parity: N <= m+3.
  • The asymptotic constants are the fixed point of the max-ratio recursion (std even 9/7 and pk odd 16/7 on the real run, relaxed to 3/2 and 5/2 to absorb the parity-broken states).
  • Safety: every N/H/R landing at an overflow needs b+1 <= 2^(L-1). That is N >= 2m+4 for i = 0 and N >= 4m+7 for i >= 1, and I forbids both.
  • Under I only std i=0, pk i=0 and pk i=1 states in the parity class are dirty. Each sits in its final gap: barrier 3·2^(L-2) or 2^(L-2), T even so u >= 1, and n+1 > 2^(lowK+1), so gen 2 merges before its overflow (no gen 3).
  • Closure: a hand case analysis covers every case of B (proof.md §6) and is checked by script (below).
  • proof.md written (draft, as the funded option says): rules, closed-form B, I, Lemma S (safety), Lemma C (closure), init, 349 via start F, check list.
