Round 1 (2026-10-13)

Architecture

The proof is split at Theorem U. Everything above it is about unit words only, with no machine steps:

nonhalt  <==  AbsDefined  :=  forall t U, Reach t U -> exists U', Pops (rk t) U U'
  • Reach is the record-level dynamics. It starts at Reach XC U0 and steps with Reach t U -> Pops (rk t) U U' -> Reach (rnext t) (rF t ++ U').
  • Pops k means k times "three carries (Inc), then the first unit is P and is removed". This is exactly the W-rule use of Lemma N.
  • nonhalt_of_abs : AbsDefined -> ~ halts tm c0 is proved (init + windows + progress_nonhalt_cond).
  • So the remaining work is pure unit-word mathematics: proof.md sections 3-7 (conveyor, Lemma T, invariant) and section 6 (Theorem B).

(II) Conveyor + Lemma T + record invariant (proof.md sections 3-5 and 7) - Represent the conveyor as walls with absolute positions: bw base [(x1,b1);...;(xk,bk)] z Z = Ps(x1-base) ++ wallu b1 :: Ps(x2-x1-1) ++ ... ++ wallu bk :: Ps(z-xk-1) ++ Z - One record step = (rk t) times "feed 3 carries at the bottom, then base += 1", then prepend rF t (new walls, R = 0). - Per-bottom-carry lemma by induction on the wall list: - wall_feed gives the wall's firings as Inc3Pops on the rest. - Pops on the rest follows from "IncN (3f) rest (Ps f ++ ...)" plus "the leading P count after 3i carries is ≥ i". Round 2 (2026-10-13)

New idea: families instead of representatives

Lemma L (two runs, a representative and a member) is replaced by a one-run statement. - Once X (the part below the cut) is clean after the first T0 carries, the rest of the step acts on X only through c mod 2^r. - So one concrete run of X at the smallest feed covers a whole affine family of states. - A family (X, y, ay, D, j, aD) stands for, for every u >= 0: zone X ++ bin (y + ay u), D + aD u, j + aD u - κ of Lemma L becomes the coefficient ay. - While ay is odd, the cut shrinks. - When ay is even, fnorm pulls digits from Y back into X. - T0 is the family base's own feed (T0 = c0). No separate T0 is needed: it works for all 4,829 runs.

Plan for the conveyor (part II), worked out this round

The zone side is now a black box: from any reached CSreach Z D J there is a unique cs_step with fixed point m, A, Y*. What remains is AbsDefined, that is the record invariant of proof.md 7 and its closure. Design:

  1. Skip the U0 walls and interval 0 by computation. Run the unit-word dynamics concretely (an executable Pops built on incf, sound by incf_sound) from U0 for about 3 cycles (24 records, 51 carries per cycle), until walls 0..5 are absorbed. Prove those Reach steps and their Pops by vm_compute. From that record on, every live wall has periodic birth data (w >= 6) and every interval is a real cs_step (J >= 1), so interval 0 (33 carries, not a cs_step) never needs a general proof.
  2. Shifted frame (+1) so all positions are nat: b' = b + 1, base' = 1 at XC XD XE ZD ZE and 0 at YC YD YE, zone bottom z = 44 + D_J + A.
  3. Wall data and positions. R_w = carries received since birth; f_w = (R_w + s_w)/2 firings; position p_w = b'w + f_w; state W iff R_w + s_w odd. Chain: R(w-1) = H_(w-1) + 3 f_w. At a record the youngest wall has R = 0, so every R is a function of the record and J.
  4. The word from position p upward, as a relation WdR w p R u (recursion on the wall number w, oldest first): - live w (R < Rabs w): u = Ps (p_w - p) ++ wallu (odd (R + s_w)) :: u' and WdR (w-1) (S p_w) (H_(w-1) + 3 f_w) u'; - absorbed w (R >= Rabs w, with Rabs w = 2(44 + D_(w+1) - b'w) - s_w): u is the zone of interval w+1 seen from p: exists Z D A Y, CSreach Z D (S w) /\ IncN m Z (Ps A ++ Y) /\ notP Y /\ m <= m*_(w+1) /\ u = Ps (44 + D + A - p) ++ Y with m = R - Rabs w. D(w+1) is unique by CSreach_det, so this is a functional description without choice.
  5. Cascade lemma (the only real induction): WdR w p R u -> p <= p_w R -> exists u', Inc u u' /\ WdR w p (S R) u', by induction on w. - SP: the head becomes W, nothing above changes (f unchanged). - W, not the absorbing firing: IncN 3 above by the IH (three times), then the shift lands on a P because p_(w-1) grows by at least one per 3 carries (or the wall above is absorbed and is a P). The result is the same word with p_w + 1: Ps (p_w - p) ++ P :: SP :: ... - W at the absorbing firing (w is top, zone count reaches m): 3 x = c + 3 A(m) - m with c = D + pj = 3(44 + D - b'_J) - E_J (this is bracket), so x = 0 exactly at m = m. Before m, x >= 1, because m' <= c + 3 A(m') holds for all m' <= m (induction on m', using minimality and A monotone). The shift is shift Y*, which cs_step provides, and the word becomes Ps (p_w + 1 - p) ++ Z_(J+1) with 44 + D_(J+1) = p_w + 1. - Mod 3: m is a triple boundary, because m = c + 3A ≡ c ≡ -E_J (mod 3), and the boundaries of interval J are the m ≡ -E_J with m >= r.
  6. Record step. Pops k U U' comes from k applications of the cascade (3 carries each) plus the pop. The leading P count after 3i carries is at least i, since the youngest wall fires floor((3i + s)/2) >= i times. Then prepend rF t: new walls with R = 0, and the old youngest has R = 3 rk = H (36, 6 or 9). The placement table of proof.md 4 gives the gaps >= 0 (a finite check).
  7. Assemble. Inv(t, U) := exists J and the zone data with WdR (youngest) base 0 U. Show Inv for the concrete state after step 1, closure under ReachS, and from it AbsDefined (the Pops of each step exists). Then Lemma nonhalt : ~ halts tm c0. Proof. apply nonhalt_of_abs, AbsDefined_proof. Qed.
