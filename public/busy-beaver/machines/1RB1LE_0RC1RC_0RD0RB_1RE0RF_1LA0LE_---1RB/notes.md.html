Picture (TM20-shaped call stack, with residue branches). A frame Y/X(a,b,c) eats the tape on its right. That tape is a list of columns 0^g 1100 1^m, each left behind by an earlier Return. The zero-gap leftover mod 3 picks the branch: - leftover 1: the frame swallows 01100, then eats the ones; - leftover 0 or 2: the frame suspends as X/Y plus one separator 0, and the ones block becomes a new frame (a call); - b = 0: Return. The frame becomes a column 0^(a+1) 1100 1^(2c±1), and its caller resumes on the returned zeros.

F 0 <E w is the same machine configuration as F E> 0 w (E on the separator cell), so the resume needs no rule of its own.

Halting condition. The machine halts exactly when a right sweep has C on 0, then D on 1, then F on 0. To 1e9 there are 23,450 D-on-1 events, and F reads 1 in every one. The ones run F lands on has length 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 or ≥9. Its 23 local shapes are the same at 1e8 and 1e9.

Fragility (why stuck). - Small sizes halt often. Caller X/Y(20,16,5) E> 0^2 1^n 0^2 1^m 0^g 1100 1^5 with n 2-12, m 1-8, g 2-9 or 30: - 640 of 1584 halt before the caller returns; - halts occur in every class of n mod 2, m mod 2 and g mod 3. - Medium sizes do not. With n 31-34, m 60-62, g 20-25, 0 of 144 halt within 2e7 steps. - The real run comes close. At step 458654411 (epoch 7) it makes exactly this kind of call with room 2: Y(14,10,18) E> 0^2 1^129 0^2 1^570 0^179 1100 .... Changing 129, 570 or 179 by -6..+6 never halts within 5e8 steps (36 runs). - So the danger is confined to small blocks. A proof has to show that small blocks appear only at the low recursion levels, in fixed shapes.

The census is not closed. Round 1 called it nearly closed, but that came from collapsing exponents ≥3 to N. - To 1e9 there are 46,375 E:R moments. 45,716 parse as a standard frame (40 front classes); 659 are transit forms (86 local shapes). - 2 parsed classes and 7 transit classes first appear in epoch 7. - The root at each epoch end is itself transit-shaped: 1^a 001 (10)^b 1 with no separator. The root also drifts right (min head position −16, 56, 142, 242, 394), so TM20's fixed LO/LE root does not carry over.

Still open: 1. Births with room 3 or 5. These include merges like 1^9 0^3 1^7 becoming X(5,8,1). 2. The X-caller version of ResumeTY, and returns of X(0,k,1) callees. 3. What would unstick it: - an abstract simulator over run-length words, driven by the validated rules and checked against the real run at every E:R moment, so the full transition list is generated automatically rather than by hand; - or a decider-style closed-language search with larger parameters; - or Dennis decides this machine is not worth that effort.
