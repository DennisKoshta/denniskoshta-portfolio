  1. New relation Inc for that +1 (on the 0/1 digits, little-endian binary, L skipped), and Inc_spec, which proves it is one h pass on the tape.

  3. Two zero-pass end clauses, Z1 and Z0 (RIncs_h1_z1, RIncs_h1_z0). They add no machine behaviour (z1_eq, z0_eq are tape identities). proof.md §3.3: without them LLLL10LL1L1 is in the domain but its sweep LLLLL1LLLLLLL111 is not.
