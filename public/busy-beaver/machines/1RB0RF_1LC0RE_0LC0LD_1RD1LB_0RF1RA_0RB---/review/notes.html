Candidate rules #

The digit form is the same as TM7's in SOMDCv6.v.

Sweep: S(r) -->+ S(eat(r)), with the wall one cell further left.

eat is TM7's RIncs hx 0, plus the two clauses marked NEW. Together they say: every L entered fires exactly one +1, whether the eater enters it or a drain pass does.

eater, k:   0 -> L, k+1        L -> L, k+1, +1 to the rest (NEW)        1 -> L, enter count mode with k
count, k>=1: L -> L, back to eater with k, +1 to the rest (NEW)
             1 -> L, k-1       0 -> 0, (k-1)/2 if k odd       0 -> 1, (k-2)/2 if k even
right end:  k=0 -> 1;  k odd -> 0, (k-1)/2;  k even -> 1, (k-2)/2   (repeat)
+1 = little-endian binary over the 0/1 digits; L digits are skipped; overflow appends 1
  • Count mode is not "add k to the counter". Each 1-digit absorbs one pass and becomes L. 0-digits and the right end take the leftover count by halving (bijective binary).
    • Example: TM7 from S(L^8 0L11). The eater stops at the first 1 of the tail 11 with k = 10, and that 1 becomes L. The second 1 absorbs one pass (k = 9). The right end writes 9 as 0101, giving L^12 0101. Adding 10 to the tail would have given 1011.
  • A sweep can have several eat-then-drain rounds. A drain pass that reaches an L restarts the eater: this happens in 115 of the 208 sweeps to 1e9.
  • Without the count-mode L clause, the map fits only 95/208 sweeps.

Where the +1 comes from: A1 fires exactly when an L is entered, once per L digit of r in every sweep.

  • TM7's A1 = 0RC turns straight back.
  • Ours' A1 = 0RF runs one ordinary h-pass over everything to its right, then comes back. It ends exactly where TM7 is one step after its A1: state C, one cell right of the A1 cell. The only change is that the digits to the right are +1. The pass itself:
    • B> 100 -> 000 B> (carry);
    • B> 000 -> <C 100;
    • B> 110 -> 011 B>, then on the way back 011 <C -> <C 110.
  • Eater enters L: 1 D> 110 X -> 1 111 D> inc(X) in 6j+13 steps (j = digits before the first 000 in X). TM7: -> 1 111 D> X in 11 steps.
  • Drain pass enters L: B> 1 110 X -> 1 111 D> inc(X) in 6j+12 steps. TM7: -> 1 111 D> X in 10 steps. The eater restarts there.
