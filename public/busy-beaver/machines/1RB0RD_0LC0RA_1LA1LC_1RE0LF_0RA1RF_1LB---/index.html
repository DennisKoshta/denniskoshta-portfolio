Mechanism

What to check here: the pictures show the mechanism that the rule table describes.

How it works: 3 notes
  • At every D-record the tape is 0^inf [1100] P(p1) s^f1 ... P(pk) s^R 100 D> 0^inf, s = 100, digit words P0 = 11001100, P1 = 110000, P2 = 1001100, P3 = 10000. One sweep adds 1 from the right: 3s wrap to 0 (moving 2 cells left), the first lower digit steps up in place. Fields follow from positions; R grows 6 per 4 sweeps.
  • Fields shrink 2 per cycle of their left digit and die; a digit whose field is 0 is pushed one word right instead of overlapping, so the oldest digits form a zero-field cluster at the left and keep counting. Overflows alternate the wall 1100 / none; every other one gives birth to a new digit P3 at the right end.
  • Lines 274 and 514 (start F, mirrored) enter the same language by step 108 / 792 and differ only in one field (+4). Exact sweep simulator (rules + step formula) = C core at 1e11 on both; no halt to 1e16 on either.

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

One closed-language proof: sea-crossing lemmas, 4-phase digit lemmas (stop/wrap), a push lemma by induction over zero-field runs, the two overflow lemmas, and an invariant giving R >= 2k+2 at every O2; then entry lemmas for 274 (108 steps) and 514 (792 steps).

  • ▲The push cascade and O2's extra pass need inductive segment lemmas; that is new formal work beyond Sk1CounterMixed.
  • ▲The domain condition (room at O2) counts, so a pure regular-language certificate is unlikely without extra structure.
  • ▲Scoop risk: Skelet 1-like Class 1 is mxdys's mined class (Sk1Machines.v TM1..TM105 list).
