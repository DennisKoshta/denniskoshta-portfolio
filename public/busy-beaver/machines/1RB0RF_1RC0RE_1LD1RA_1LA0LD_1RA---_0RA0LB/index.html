Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Hub K(a,b,G) = 0^inf (101)^a 00 (101)^b A> 0 enc(G) 0^inf (enc 0=000, 1=010, J=100). R1: each bounce moves the 00 gap one block right and adds 1 to the counter G (J skipped).
  • At b=0 the low digits decide: 0 1^r J^k = partial eat E (J's become k-1 fresh blocks); 0 1^r 0 / 1... = bell B0/B1: sweep to the left end, counter shifted one cell (σ).
  • σ leaves x/z/w marks that the carry cleans within 2^q <= 2^floor(log2 N) increments (19,773/19,773 macro epochs); lockstep from blank holds to 1e9 (all hubs) and 3e10 (b=0 hubs).

C1: 1RA (ours) vs 1RB (tm1 mirrored)
note
Same bouncer: w1 = 101, w0 = 00, and the gap step of R1 is the template's RInc. The counter differs: 3-cell digits 000/010 with transparent J = 100 blocks instead of 2-cell digits (LInc not run on ours: the encoding does not match). No overflow Ov and no fractal recursion: the b=0 step is E/B0/B1 by the low digits, with the one-cell shift σ, and epoch lengths are irregular (2, 5, 6, 11, 18, 44, ...) instead of 2^i - 1. Same family as R2 (BECv1 tm108 twin: 'drop a suffix, shift one cell' eat, pending-size invariant).
Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

Proof plan: K(a,b,G) with G an Inductive group list; R1 by a carry lemma (ind over digits, J transparent); E/B0/B1 as finite case lemmas with σ as a list shift. Invariant: G is standard at every b=0 hub. Open: the cleanup lemma on σ-words (x -> z on the first carry, z x^j -> 0 J^(j+1) on the second; time 2^(p+1) <= N). Start from FractalBEC.v for the bouncer and a BECv1-style digit list for the counter; share the size lemma with R2.

  • ▲Cleanup lemma unproved: arbitrary x/z/w words halt (K(5,6,1z0101) halts in 19 steps), so the invariant must track where σ puts x/w.
  • ▲The carry through z/w is a cell-level transducer with context (z followed by x-runs); formalizing it may take many small lemmas.
  • ▲Same family as R2 and the #10/#30 sibling, which got stuck on a size invariant; that invariant may be hard here too.
