Notation (CB1 of ChaoticBell1Halt.v, renamed to our states)

Paper order (left part far-to-near, right part near-to-far). D n and S n are the two spellings of a digit n >= 0.

name tape role
D n 1^(2n+1) 00 a digit on the right of the head
S n 1 (01)^(n+1) the same digit after the head passed it going right
R l r l B> 0100 r rightward sweep (TM13's A)
L l r l <C 1 r leftward sweep (TM13's B)
J l r l <D 100 r leftward "increment" head (TM13's C)
PL n 101 101 1^(2n) (01)^(n+4) a left block that later replays 2n'+1 calls (when n = 1+3n')

Mechanism

  • A bell: the head sweeps the whole tape. Going right (R) it turns each digit D n into S n; at the right end it appends two zero digits (emptyR) or meets a 10 marker (collide / pairR / defects). Going left (L) it turns S n back into D n.
  • The left part is a stack of loop counters. A group 111 in front of (01)^(b+1) launches one full sweep to the right end and back (one "call"); PL blocks (made by pairR from two equal digits) replay 2n'+1 calls and then increment a digit (finishPL + J). At the far left end the counter 0^inf 1^(2b) ... turns the head around (over1/over2) and the tape grows.
  • What the digit list does over time is chaotic (sheet class name). The same rules make TM13 (start A) halt after 64,832 executor ticks: the leftward sweep meets 11100 (the halt window) in an old fragment near the left end. Whether our start ever produces that window is only decided by running.
  • Direct simulation: no halt to 10^9 (span -518..36,471, 22,051 ones). At every visit of the left end (to 210^9) the tape is a clean list of D digits (odd 1-runs separated by 00): 4,609 digits, largest 33, span 47,146 at step 1.610^9; span grows like sqrt(steps) (work/left_visits_2e9.json).
  • compress.py track (10^7, records): the right edge shows the calls macro, 1^a ... (110)^b, a -3 and b +2 per visit (one 111 consumed, two zero digits appended per call). qsim: 1.19*10^6 steps in 20 s (no useful acceleration, as the ranking said).

Cryptid check

  • Local rules: exact and closed (26/26 validated); no arithmetic in them beyond +1/+2 on digits.
  • Early phase (to ~4*10^5 ticks): chaotic digit-list rewriting (the phase in which TM13 halts at 64,832 ticks). No closed law.
  • Late phase (from ~4*10^5 ticks, seen from two starts): a regular stack of PL(n) runs whose counts fall by about 5 every two levels, with small non-zero remainders (k_n - 5 k_(n+2) in -89..178). A per-level "divide by 5 with remainder" law is the cryptid warning sign named in the ground rules, but the law itself is not fitted yet, so no cryptid flag today: status STUCK, with the fit as the next stage and the cryptid protocol as its stop rule.
