Mechanism

  • In the parent's orientation (this tape is its mirror image), the tape alternates between two blank ends except at defects (two equal neighbours). The defects are a base-3 counter: a left word that counts to 81, a chain of K digit blocks bumped once per visit, and a right region of fossils plus two blocks X, Y that grow in step with the chain.
  • Every 81 x 3^K visits the left word overflows (a record), the chain regroups and the right region moves to the next of six classes. After six classes K is 2 larger and the scale u becomes 9u. The long gaps are affine in u, so each grows by a factor close to 9 but not exactly 9.
  • From F the parent's table reaches the parent's record exit 28 with the parent's exact window (last chain block and right region). Only five deep chain gaps differ, and the chain is 60 cells shorter.

How the proof works

  • The parent's invariant at record exits fixes the left word, the window and the right region. The deep chain gaps are free apart from residue and minimum, which the gap-variable encoding builds in.
  • New base case: init28 runs F;; tape0 to record exit 28 (about 2.63e12 machine steps, 18,571,554 simulator steps in 38 pieces). The parent's base28 and inv28 then hold for this run's nus28, and the parent's inv_step gives nonhalt_P.
  • Conjugacy: perm_phi : Perm (flip tm) tmP phi by case analysis, then busycoq's flip_halts (verify/Flip.v) and perm_halts (verify/Permute.v). No new induction.
