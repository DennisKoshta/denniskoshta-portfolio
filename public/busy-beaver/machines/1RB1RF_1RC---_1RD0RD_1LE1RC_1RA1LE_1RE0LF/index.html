Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Tape = left end ... Z2 S1 Z1 S0 Z0 W: zones Z repeat a 6-cell block (110101, rotated); W = fixed 99-cell right wall.
  • Separators S have 4 states: 3 bounce the head back, the 4th lets it through, so turn-back depth is a base-4 ruler.
  • Z0 gains 1 block per 2 sweeps, Z_k ~ Z_(k-1)/4. The left end, where new digits appear, is not characterised yet.

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

Longitudinal proof in SBCv6a TM1 style: ind lemmas for R1/R2 blocks; one segRLs lemma per separator state (3 bounces + 1 pass) and one for the wall W. The digit list is an Inductive with a WF invariant, closed by multistep_nonhalt + progress_nonhalt_cond. A left-end lemma that creates new digits is still needed.

  • ▲Left end not characterised: deep zones reset at the 1.8e8 left record (Z3 32 -> new 6/13). It might be messy.
  • ▲Separators differ: two phase types (bounce offsets -10 vs -12) and different inert debris (P0 vs P1). Needs a closed family of separator words.
  • ▲Size: several separator/phase cases, so expect 20-40 KB (SBCv6a/SBCv10 scale). qsim and rulefit found nothing, so the proof is by hand.
