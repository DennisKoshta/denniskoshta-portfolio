1. Rest-tape alphabet

  • A rest tape is the tape when the head makes a new right record, at the end of a full right pass.
  • It reads 0^inf 1^t1 0 1^t2 0 ... W, and every token t_i is odd. The only exception is the leftmost token (10 now).
  • Reason: the C/D right pass flips the C-cells and needs every D-cell to be 1, so 0s sit only on C-cells.
  • Below, a token sequence like 7 1 1 7 means 1^7 0 1 0 1 0 1^7 0.
  • A zone block is the pair 1 3, which is 101110. Zk means (1 3)^k.

2. Digit rule, the same at every level (closes "missing phase rules", "separators differ", "carry at S2+")

The rest tape is:

LE' D_top Z_top E { debris_k X_k (1 3)^m_k } (k = K..0) W

Each digit word X_k is one of two types, A or B, and each type has 4 states:

type s=0 s=1 s=2 s=3
A 1 1 1 1 1 1 1 = (10)^7 1 1 1 1 1 7 7 1 1 1 1 7 1 1 7
B 7 1 1 3 1 1 1 1 7 1 1 3 1 1 7 7 7 1 1 1 1 1 7 7 1 1 1 7

Every trip adds 1 at X_0, the S0 digit, as follows: - State s < 3: the digit moves to s+1 and the sweep turns back inside this digit. The zone m_k gains 1 block when the digit leaves s=1, and stays the same when it leaves s=0 or s=2. - State s = 3: the digit goes to s=0 and m_k gains 1. The sweep then runs on through debris_k, which is left unchanged, and into zone k+1 and digit X_(k+1). This is the carry.

What stays fixed: - The debris words never change, and neither does the wall W. - S0 is type A. Its debris is 7 9 7 9 1 3 1 3 1 1 1 9 7 9 7 9 1 1 1 9 1 1 1 9. - S1 is type B. Its debris is 1 1 1 9 7 1 1 3 1 1 3 9 1 3 1 1 1 7 1 9 7 7 1 1 1 3 9 1 3 1 1 1 9. - Deeper digits are type A or B. Some have their own debris and some are bare: S3 at 9825, for example, is just (10)^7. - The bounce offsets −10 and −12 from the observe pass are the two types, A and B. - Earlier rules: the observe-pass rules Ra/Rc/Rb* (turn-back configurations) are this rule for S0 (type A) and S1 (type B), seen at turn-back time instead of at rest.

Time law: - The turn-back level of trip t is 1 + (number of trailing digits in state 3). This is a base-4 ruler. - Between trips 2000 and 5088 the level is exactly 1 + v4(t+31), capped at 4. - The 1.8e8 rebuild changed the phase of the levels ≥ 4.

1. Fast-forward without a size limit

How topsim.py works: - Between events, closed form. Take the counter digits from the topmost digit J down to S0, with value v = Σ s_k·4^k. After N trips, digit k is ⌊(v+N)/4^k⌋ mod 4. Its zone gains the number of odd c in [⌊v/4^k⌋, ⌊(v+N)/4^k⌋), since a zone grows on leaving states 1 and 3. - Events, exact. An event is a carry that leaves the topmost parsed digit, or that has to cross a debris word not yet shown to be transparent. Each event is run exactly with trip1 on a tape where the zones below the event point are capped at 3 blocks. - Checks on each event. After the trip, the lower digits must come out exactly as predicted: state 0, zone+1, debris and wall unchanged. Their real zone sizes are then restored. A debris word that the carry crossed unchanged becomes trusted. - Validation: - It reproduces all 16 round-1 events from trip 19041 to 760417; the tape after each event is identical. - Capped and full-size runs agree on the K=9 events 1481313, 1743457 and 2005601. The full-size trips are 2.6e7 to 3.5e7 steps each. - Run: ./toktrack 2000000000 > tok2e9.txt (10 s), then python3 -B topsim.py tok2e9.txt 16979 80 out.json.

5. Head-level structure (toward lemmas for Coq)

Measured on two trips, 14944→14945 and 15968→15969 (sweep_excursion.py): - In the left sweep, the head never goes more than 15 cells right of its running minimum before the turn-back. - In the return pass, the head never backs up more than 13 cells.

So a trip is two sweeps that each look only a bounded distance back. The head-level rules are then finitely many local rules plus the zone loops R1/R2. They are not enumerated yet. The left-sweep signal is not a function of (state, token) alone; it carries up to about 15 cells of context (sweep_forms.py).

6. What is open, and a decision for Dennis

  • (a) The rebuild rule. Needed: how the return pass recodes a zone of size m (parity, maybe m mod 4) and each digit/debris word. This must come from an accelerated, rule-level model of the recoding pass, because the real trip is about 1e16 steps.
  • (b) The epoch recursion. The tape at the start of epoch n+1 as a function of epoch n, and one family that holds every epoch's counter, top events and rebuild. The round-1 embryo/LE' gap is part of this.
