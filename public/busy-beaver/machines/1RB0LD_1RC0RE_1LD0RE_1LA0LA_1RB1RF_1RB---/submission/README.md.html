Mechanism

  • Same counter as #33. The head works at the left edge. At each visit the tape is K = 0^inf <D (01)^b 000 (01)^a d~_m .. d~_1 1001 0^inf. Each digit is (10)^v 00 (01)^u (bit 0) or (10)^v (01)^u (bit 1), and d_m, next to the block, is the least significant bit.
  • Carry and overflow. Each carry M adds 1. Every digit it touches pays one 10, a digit flipped 0->1 gains a 01, and a flipped digit with no 10 left merges into the next one. On overflow (all bits 1), O re-pairs the halves of every digit, and the counter restarts with one more digit.
  • Different orbit, same macro map. The rules are #33's with head state D instead of C. Only the start differs: from blank the machine reaches K((1,1,9); 8, 1) at step 687 (class A of the invariant), where #33 reaches K((1,1,3); 4, 1) at step 215.

How the proof works

  • Rules. 20 window lemmas S1-S6, T1-T14 (es, then shift_rule_L/R or induction) compose into the digit lemmas Lb/Lf/Rb/Ro, the sweeps LbS/RbS/RoS and the rule specs M_spec, Mdrop_spec (carry with a merge) and O_spec.
  • Invariant. #11's three-phase INV = Common /\ (A \/ B \/ C) (no merges / top digit about to merge / merged), with parities and lower bounds, rules out the one failure case (a carry reaching a bit-0 digit with no 10 left). macro_step shows that every INV state takes M or O to an INV state.
  • nonhalt. multistep_nonhalt with init (c0 reaches K [d1 1 9] 8 1 in 687 steps, solve_init), then progress_nonhalt_cond with P := INV.
