693 bursts (2026-10-13)

Verdict: STRUCTURED. No cryptid flag.

Every burst starts from a configuration that is an exact function of one integer n (the bell is about 2^n); the n = 12 tapes were predicted from the formulas before the run reached them, and appeared exactly. Between bursts, every reset obeys one drop law, and the catch-up after a reset is a fixed list that depends only on the ruler digit j. The odd-n burst is a 3/4 : 1/4 split followed by halving rounds whose offsets do not depend on n.

Everything seen is binary arithmetic with small fixed offsets (powers of 2, a period-3 cycle of counter shapes). No rule seen multiplies by 3/2 or branches on residues of the bell. The only case splits are the counter's period-3 shape and, apparently, j mod 3 in the short tails of the catch-up lists. So this is not Collatz-like.

Two pieces are not yet closed formulas: the catch-up lists for j >= 12 (seen only up to j = 11), and the last few rounds of an odd-n burst (the "cleanup", round 8 onward), which differ from n to n. A proof needs both lemmas.

Simulation is evidence only. Nothing here is a kill.

Notation

At a left-edge record the tape reads

0^inf <A  L  (w1)^c (w2)^d  R  0^inf
  • L is the counter, in the words 010, 001, 100 (plus 1001 and a few mixed words near events).
  • c and d are the two long runs. Their words are the four phases 0011, 1001, 1100, 0110 of one period-4 pattern.
  • s = c + d is the bell.

A head crossing a long run rotates its phase. In state A moving right, 0011 becomes 1001; F right turns 1001 into 1100, C right 1100 into 0110, B right 0110 into 0011. Moving left: D turns 1001 into 0011 and 1100 into 1001, A turns 0011 into 0110, E turns 0110 into 1100.

A reset is a left-edge record at which c falls.

1. The ruler law (between bursts)

To 4.1e11 there are 882 resets. 836 of them have the counter in the form

L = (010)^k 011 (001)^j

and every one of these drops c by exactly

drop(j) = 3 * 2^(j-2) - 2        (j = 2..11: 1, 4, 10, 22, 46, 94, 190, 382, 766, 1534)

No exception (0 mismatches; to 8e10 it was 479 of 518, to 2.4e11 696 of 741).

  • For j = 6..11 the m-th gain is 3 * 2^(j-2-m) + e_m while j - 2 - m >= 1, with offsets that do not depend on j:

2. The bursts are canonical events

The other 46 drops are all accounted for:

  • 12 in the start-up before step 127,661 (bell under 40);
  • the rest at the canonical events below, or inside an odd-n burst. The 7 non-ruler drops between 8e10 and 4.1e11 are exactly E(12), V(12) and W(12,0..4).

Odd n: O(n). The bell reaches exactly 2^n - 1 with d = 0 and the counter all 010:

O(n) = 0^inf <A (010)^(n+2) (01100)^2 (1100)^(2^n - 1) (1001)^2 100011 0^inf

Even n: E(n), V(n), W(n,i). c overflows 2^n and wraps (drop 2^n):

E(n)   = 0^inf <A (010)^(n+2) (0110)^3 (0011)^(2^n - 1) (001)^2 1 (00110)^2 0^inf                 (c = 3)

After W(n,i) the ruler runs with largest j = 2i + 3, then the next W follows. After the last W, i = (n-4)/2, that is j up to n - 1, and then O(n+1) occurs.

3. Inside an odd burst

Put Q = 2^(n-2). Round r is the r-th left-edge record after O(n).

r c d
0 3Q Q + 3
1 Q 3Q + 3
r = 2..7 s - d 3Q / 2^(r-1) - delta_r

with delta_2..delta_7 = 0, 2, 5, 3, 5, 8 for n = 11, 13 and 15. For n = 9 the same deltas hold up to r = 6, and d runs out at r = 7; n = 7 runs out at r = 5. The counter meanwhile cycles through three shapes, eating three 001 words every three rounds:

(010)^r 011 (001)^x (100)^2  ->  (010)^(r+1) 01 (100)^(x-1) (1001)^2  ->  (010)^(r+2) 01 (100)^x  ->  ...

The rounds seen only halve d (d to about d/2, minus a small offset). No round multiplies by 3/2, and nothing Collatz-like was seen; this looks like binary arithmetic, the counter recording the halvings.

Cleanup (r >= 8) and the flip. After round 7 the counter changes shape and the remaining d (about 3 * 2^(n-8)) is used up in a few irregular rounds. Then the field flips to a single run and the ruler restarts with d close to 2^n:

13 88 at r = 7 5 (63, 25, 15, 10, 0) c = 14, d = 8191

The cleanup words differ with n (n = 7 and 13 both use 1001001 words, n = 15 uses 10011001001001 and 10011001100; n = 13 also has one extra 2^n wrap, c = 8203 to 11, four rounds after the flip). For n = 13 and 15 the cleanup takes 5 rounds. For n = 15 its d values still roughly halve each round (376, 207, 97, 47, 18); for n = 13 the first step does not (88, 63, 25, 15, 10).

These are not new words, though. The ordinary catch-up after a large ruler reset uses the same counter words: 129 left-edge records of the main run contain 1001001-words. For example, after the j = 7 reset at step 113,589,153,096 the counter passes through

0 (100)^5 (1001001001)^3        (010)^8 (0100110010)^2 01

which are the shapes of the n = 13 cleanup (0 (100)^7 (1001001)^4 001, (010)^9 (0100110)^2 (001)^4). So the cleanup looks like the catch-up mechanism acting on a small gap, not a separate process.

What is open

  • The catch-up law of section 1b for all j: the leading terms 3 * 2^(j-2-m) + e_m fit j = 6..11, but the short tails (after the 2^0 term) are seen only for j <= 11, and they may depend on j mod 3. A proof needs them for every j, from the word-level rules.
  • The same halving inside an odd burst, whose offsets (delta_r and the cleanup) differ from the reset catch-up because the counter words differ.
  • The cleanup after round 7 of an odd burst, for all odd n. Its input d grows like 3 * 2^(n-8), so it is not a fixed finite patch; it is probably the same catch-up mechanism on a smaller gap. It takes 5 rounds for both n = 13 and n = 15.
  • That every odd burst ends in E(n+1): checked to n = 11 only.
  • The round map itself at word level (normal round, reset round, catch-up round), from the transition table.

Proof stage (2026-10-14)

Funded by Dennis ("fund 693", 2026-10-14). About 2 h. Outcome: parked (STUCK). The catch-up law was not closed, and it turned out not to be the right lemma. Working version in proof.md, checker scripts/check_proof.py (C1-C7 all pass).

The left-edge records are not the rounds. Between two records the machine runs a binary counter with 3-cell digits (000, 001, carry 010), big-endian, between the marker 1 and a 13-cell interface. Each shuttle of the head between the counter and the wall of the period-4 field is one counter step, and it moves the wall one block (phase 1) or half a block (phases 2-4) to the left. Each time the wall reaches the interface the field has rotated one step and a new wall starts at the right end (phase change). Once per revolution (4 phases) the wall "eats" the lowest digit (it becomes a frozen 011 and the interface moves 3 cells left). A left-edge record is a counter overflow: the carry runs to the blank end, the counter gains a digit and restarts at 0. Example: the record at 1,128,713 is followed by 127 = 2^7 - 1 counter steps (8 in phase 1, 119 in phase 2) and the next record at 1,173,120 (work/inner_shapes_12rounds_from_1128713.txt, scripts/inner_shapes.py).

Rules proved or checked this stage (proof.md sections 2-3): - C1 eight rotation rules (4 steps), with B reading only 0 inside them; C2 halting criterion: halt iff A or F reads 0 with a 1 to its right (the undefined entry is B1, not C1). - Phase 1: X1(g,c+1,d) -(16c+49)-> X0(g,c,d+1); X0(g,c+1,d) -(16c+49+6t)-> X1(g+1,c,d+1), t = trailing 001 digits. Independent of d. - Phase 2: Z1(g,x,y+1) -(8x+19)-> Z0(g,x,y); Z0(g,x+1,y) -(8x+43+6t)-> Z1(g+1,x,y+2). Independent of y. - Checked on random constructed tapes (40 per rule in the checker); the pass-by-pass derivations are not written.

Random-parameter tests (evidence): 620 runs from tapes the orbit never produces (counter far too long or too short, random digits, random wall position, 5 record skeletons), 1e8-2e8 steps each: 0 halts (work/randparam_*.txt, scripts/randparam_*.py). So safety does not seem to depend on the counter/wall/field correlations that the drop law and the bursts describe.

Open lemma: closure of the shape family 0^inf 1 <g> I_k (u_k)^x h_k (v_k)^y R_k 0^inf (g any word over {000,001}, x, y free, up to 16 phase types = 4 phases x 4 interface alignments, plus the transient shapes of phase change, eating and overflow) under the machine, with no A/F-on-0-before-1 step. P1a/P1b/P2a/P2b are 4 of its cases.

Route for a next stage: a symbolic shape explorer (literals, runs w^(>=m), a star {000,001}*, C1 as accelerations) to compute that closure, then one checked rule per explored transition. This is a closed-tape-language proof, so it bypasses the catch-up law, the burst tapes and the record-level simulator. Same method for the twins 528, 310, 548 with their own words.

Three bugs/artefacts found and fixed on the way, each visible as junk or a spurious halt: 1. A start-shape typo (0011001101 for 00110011001) made the first pass turn at the interface. 2. Eaten digits look like counter digits (001 is also a field group). Folding them back into the counter gave 2 spurious halts at K = 4. Fix: digit cells carry a position mark; a digit loses it when it takes a value outside 000/001/010, or when the head enters it from the right in a state other than A (from the left: other than B). Why it does not close. Runs: K = 3/4/5 to 400,000 single-step shapes; K = 20/64 with exact counts to 1.5 million; macro BFS at K = 64 to 2.5 million (Q = 0) and 2.6 million (Q = 3, short counters exact). Late shapes have counter-to-field regions far longer than the orbit's 13-cell interface, e.g.

0^inf 1 {000,001}* 1001 (0000)^1 01 (0000)^2 00 (0110)^3 0 (1100)^>=59 111 B> 011101 (1001)^3 (0011)^2 00011 0^inf

(34 cells before (1100)^>=59). 0000 blocks by themselves are real: the orbit has them in the interface while the head is out in the field during P1a.

Source 1 (identified): the counter's value is free in the abstraction, so a branch can avoid overflows for ever while the wall eats one digit per revolution. The counter drops to 0-1 digits, then overflows and eatings alternate every few counter steps. On the orbit this never happens because overflows add digits as fast as eatings remove them (k + j constant). Q = 3 does not help: the counter is exact only once written out in full; while it is {000,001}^>=m its value is free and each eating lowers m.

Source 2 (an explorer defect): an unsound diagnostic run (--no-empty: the counter never runs out of digits) still grows (K = 64: 2,848 skeletons at 1.9 M shapes) with the same long interface regions. scripts/ctl_replay.py replays the orbit exactly through the explorer (X0 with exact counts, counter written out): the eaten-digit rule fires at the real eating (step 1,222,705), but after it the head enters the low digits in state D during ordinary counter steps, and the rule unmarks four real digits within about 440 symbolic steps (replay steps 12,151-12,589). So the rule "entered by a state other than A means eaten" is wrong after the first eating. At step 1,642,188 the real tape is 1 000 001 001 001 001 001 001 001 001 100 110 011 ... (head in D at position 6) and the replay shows 2 marked digits plus 5 plain 001 groups. Plain digits never fold back, so even the orbit's own configurations drift to long counter-to-field regions (the period-3 001001... of the junk shapes). Soundness is not affected, but the closure question cannot be answered until the bookkeeping is faithful on the replay. A quick mark-free variant (--gap G: fold digit groups while G cells remain before the field run) grows faster (G = 13: 1,321 skeletons at 0.7 M; G = 16: 3,935 at 1 M).

What carries over to 528, 310, 548. The explorer, the validator and the decider driver are generic in the machine string; the start shape, the digit words (000/001, carry 010) and the eating rule (counter entered in A from the right, in B from the left) are 693's. Since the obstruction is the counter/field coupling, the twins would hit the same wall: this route does not settle them either.

Route for a stage 3, if funded. First make the digit bookkeeping faithful: replace the entering rule (it unmarks digits that D enters in ordinary counter steps after the first eating) and require the exact replay to keep every real digit marked over many revolutions while unmarking exactly one digit per revolution; then rerun the no-empty diagnostic. Then a counter token with a clock: digit count D plus the lemma "a counter of D digits overflows within 2^D increments", and the phase lemma "a phase has at least s/2 counter steps". Together they bound the eatings between two overflows and keep D >= log2(s) - c. That is the inequality form of the catch-up law, much weaker than the exact lists of stage 1. Alternatively prove that every malformed interface heals within a bounded number of revolutions, but the list of malformed interfaces kept growing.

Proof stage 3 (2026-10-14)

Funded by Dennis ("fund_clock" on decision.json, 2026-10-14). About 2 h. Outcome: parked (STUCK) by the stop rule: the shape set does not close within the stage. Both sources of the stage-2 obstruction are repaired, though, and the explored set now behaves very differently. Working version: proof.md section 9. Checker: scripts/check_proof.py --ctl3 (C10, C11). Answered decision moved to decisions/2026-10-14-fund_clock.json.

The counter is two blocks. The eating is two local rules: 001 <D -> <A 011 (an eaten 1 becomes the frozen 011 and is carried on) and 000 <D (an eaten 0 becomes the low end of a counter shifted by one cell). So after a few eatings the counter is 1 H sep L, two digit blocks in different alignments with a short separator (1, 0011, 011); the records' (010)^k 011 (001)^j is H, sep, L at an overflow. Stage 2's positional marks could not represent the shifted block, which is why real digits fell out of the counter (source 2).

(1) Writer marks (scripts/ctl_explore3.py). A cell written by a left-moving E, D or A step is marked e, d, a; the carry runs A, D, E right to left, so every real digit reads e d a in its own alignment. A digit = marks e d a and value 000/001; several S tokens allowed. Guided replay (scripts/ctl_replay3.py: symbolic shape and real machine in lockstep, the counter folded, the case containing the real tape picked at each split) from step 1,128,893 to 21,129,033: 931,457 symbolic steps, 41,151 splits, 125 overflows, the eatings between them and the odd burst O(7); inside the shapes at every step, at most 40 non-digit cells between marker and field (stage 2: unbounded drift). C10 checks the first 2e6 steps.

(2) Counter floor = the clock. S now means "at least Q+1 digits"; splitting it gives S 000 | S 001 | every exact word of Q+1 digits (an identity of languages). S can no longer be eaten to nothing, and a counter that really is short becomes exact, so it overflows within 2^(Q+1) increments, well inside one phase (>= K = 64 counter steps). No symbolic parameter is needed.
