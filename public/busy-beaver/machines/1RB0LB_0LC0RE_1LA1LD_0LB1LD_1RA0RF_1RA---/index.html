Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Trip and phase level: three short trips cross the unary counter 1001^c, the fourth converts one budget block; the budget sum is conserved and c grows by 2 per cycle (R1-R3 validated 60/60, 40/40, 40/40).
  • Epoch level: at a phase end the counter (about 8n cells) becomes a layer of (4n+r)/3 blocks, and n mod 3 picks the next phase (0: H6 shift phase, 1: R1 phase at once, 2: re-eat an older layer).
  • To 1e11: layers 5803, 9996, 13333, 17774; the old 9996 layer is re-eaten at 4.8e10..8e10 and rebuilt; no halt (halting needs B> 111, never written).
