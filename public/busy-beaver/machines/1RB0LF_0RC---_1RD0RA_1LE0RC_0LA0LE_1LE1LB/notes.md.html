Inner-record form. Every left turn of A onto a 0, where the right side matches, has the form 0^inf W A>(001)^p 00 D T. - D = digits (0b10001 with (100)^g gaps, LSB leftmost), value v, N digits. - T = tail. - W = wall. In normal mode W is empty and p is the lead a. - Z(g) := (010)^(g+1) is a large gap g packed into the wall (a "zone").

Pause rules (scripts/deepen_rules.py, seed 7: 150/150 each, 1050/1050; seed 1: 70/70; exact whole-tape match at the next inner record). What a pause is. 1. The overflow packs every large gap into a zone. The lead (NO) or the inner lead p (PO) goes into the wall. 2. The zones are rebuilt innermost first, with +2 per cycle. ZX restores each gap exactly; the gap-2 digit is ZX with p = 0. 3. The outer (100) wall is then eaten, also +2 per cycle. 4. The pause ends one of two ways: - WX first: back to normal mode with lead = wall + 1. This is the old "Ovf-short". - PO first: p becomes a new gap p-1 via ZXP, and the outer phase continues.
