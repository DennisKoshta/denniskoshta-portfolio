1RB0LF_0RC---_1RD0RA_1LE0RC_0LA0LE_1LE1LB never halts

  • Machine: 1RB0LF_0RC---_1RD0RA_1LE0RC_0LA0LE_1LE1LB, a BB(6) holdout. It is on every holdout list from 2024-06-14 through 2026-10-07 (line 437 of the 778-machine list). busycoq's verify/ has no proof of it, under any renaming or mirror.
  • Class: "Sync bouncer counter" (spreadsheet Results row 918, no notes or claims).
  • Result: Lemma nonhalt : ~ halts tm c0. in Coq 8.20.1 against unmodified busycoq BB6 (commit 0940bb9c). Print Assumptions nonhalt is closed (no axioms). It compiles in 10.3 s with -native-compiler yes, and coqchk re-checks it in 3.2 s (check_kill.txt).

Mechanism

  • A spread binary counter inside a bouncer. Right of the lead (001)^a 00, the tape is a counter in the tokens 00, 01, Z = 100: each digit is b Z 01 Z^g (bit b, gap g), LSB first. Every bounce off the left end adds 1 to the counter and 1 to a.
  • Overflow and pause. When all bits are 1, the carry runs through a block of gap-1 digits into a finite tail, which answers N or S. On S the counter wraps to 0. On N the left part freezes into a wall of zones, and a long pause eats it two increments at a time, rebuilding every gap and inserting new digits (the run shows gaps 28, 68, 260, 1028, 4097, 7).
  • The tail is a small counter too. Its 2-bit front unit cycles 10 → 01 → 11 → 00 → 10, and S can happen only at 11. The rest of the tail only decides which answer comes at an 11, and the proof covers both.

How the proof works

  • Window lemmas (es) and a carry lemma by induction on the carry depth give the increment and the seven pause rules. The pause rules are three generic lemmas over an arbitrary wall.
  • The overflow's leftward return is a 4-rule token transducer retf, proved sound once (retf_spec). On the tail its answer depends only on where the first 0 bit is: 14 cases by computation, plus an induction for the tail's growing end unit. No 16,384-entry table is needed.
  • Between normal-mode overflows only four paths occur (S, P1, P1S, P3). An interval invariant INV on (M = 2^N, s = a − c, front unit) is closed under all four, whatever the tail picks at 11 (step). init is one 92,299-step solve_init, and nonhalt is multistep_nonhalt plus progress_nonhalt_cond with P := INV.

Template and what changed

Adapted from busycoq's verify/CounterClass3v2.v, Module TM1 (1RB0LC_0LC1RE_1LD1LC_1LA1RD_---1RF_0RA0RF), a hand-written multi-level counter proof. The transition tables are unrelated, so the file follows TM1's architecture, not its lemmas. - Kept: es window lemmas and shift rules, a carry lemma by induction on the carry depth (TM1's E_stmt/E_all), macro lemmas chained with follow, and multistep_nonhalt. - Changed: TM1 closes by strong induction over a trajectory family (Traj) with progress_nonhalt_simple. Here the macro state is (a, G, r1, tail), closed by the interval invariant with progress_nonhalt_cond. - New: the token layer, the retf transducer for the overflow and the tail, one IR shape KP for normal and pause mode, and the zone-rebuild induction Zones, whose no-overflow condition is stated on the final value only.
