1. The simulator (scripts/c2s2_tsim.c). - The tape is two stacks of runs of 2-cell tokens (proof.md §1.3), with the pairing fixed from the start cell. The token table is computed from the cell table. - Three exact accelerations: - chain: a whole run passed in one state; - zig-zag: a 3-visit cycle that turns twice and writes the hole token back, applied to a whole run. These are the S-type sweeps at the other token parity; without them a sweep costs 3 visits per token; - carry jump: at a K-config, the right-hand block of digits with u, v ≥ 1 is advanced n times by rule M in closed form. n is the largest count for which every step is a plain M: the stop digit has v ≥ 2, the swept digits have u ≥ 2 and v ≥ 2, and the carry stays inside the block. It works at both K parities (R lines are the odd parity). - K-configs at both parities are detected exactly. The output format is kdump's; c2s2_tk.py converts R lines and parses digits, and c2s2_dv.py prints the parity-free defect view (constant runs Z_k / O_k and the alternating gaps between them).

5. Verdict. - At the top, halting is a residue condition: a regime-I exhaustion with N ≡ 2 (mod 5). - Whether that condition is met depends on the next digit's residue when that digit runs out first (roughly mod 3, with exceptions), and those exceptions come from deeper digits. - Every one of these values comes from the piecewise-affine maps of stages 1–2: - per cycle, the top changes by (−5, +1) in regime I and by (+3, −5, +2) in regime II; - digit d loses 12·2^(d−1) or 5·2^(d−1) of its u per cycle and gains 2^d in v; - exits split on residues mod 5, and merges add (P + Q + 3). - This is mixed 2-adic / 5-adic arithmetic over an unbounded hierarchy. An invariant would have to control residues at every level at once. No finite family of residue classes was found, and the u1 table is not periodic in any simple modulus. - So halting is decided by pseudo-random-looking arithmetic. Under the cryptid protocol (PLAN.md rule 6) both machines are flagged and proof work stops. - Heuristic, not evidence: if each top-level regime-I exhaustion is a fresh draw with roughly a 1/5 chance of halting (less after lower cancellations), and the top keeps cycling, the random model leans to an astronomically late halt, not to non-halting. This differs from Antihydra-type cryptids. R4's current top cycle already has the halting residue.

3. The obstruction, stated precisely. Non-halting needs a family of configurations, closed under rule G, with no member in H1, H2 or U0–U3 (proof.md §3.6, §5). Every probe so far says that membership in such a family would have to be decided by the following data: - Halting at the top happens at a regime-I exhaustion with N ≡ 2 (mod 5). This is exact on N = 0..44 from one base. - Whether that exhaustion is reached depends on which lower digit runs out first, and on its residue at that moment (stage 3: u1, roughly mod 3 with exceptions). - Whether the halt survives that exhaustion depends in turn on the digit below it, once that digit runs out first (section 2 above: u2), and not monotonically. - The per-cycle maps that produce these values are affine with 2-power rates (u_d − 12·2^(d−1) or − 5·2^(d−1), v_d + 2^d), with mod-5 exits and P + Q + 3 merges (stages 1–2).

So a closed family would have to fix a residue condition at every level of an unbounded hierarchy of floor-divisions by different moduli. No finite set of residue classes is visible, and the same picture now holds one level deeper than in stage 3. This is the cryptid flag of stage 3, reinforced, not a new route.
