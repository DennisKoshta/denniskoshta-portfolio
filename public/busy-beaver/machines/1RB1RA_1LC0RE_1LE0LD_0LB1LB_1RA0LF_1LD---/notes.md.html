Observation

  • Halting: F1 is the only undefined cell. F is entered only by E1 (0LF), and E reads a 1 with a 1 on its left only after C0 (1LE). So the machine halts iff C reads 0 with 11 on its left: 11 0 <C -> halt in 3 steps (rule H, 40/40 random contexts).
  • No halt to 2e9 (C sim to 1e9; record tracker to 2e9). At 1e9: cells -30659..1159, 22,003 ones. The left edge grows like t^0.5; the right edge grows only in bursts (hi 566 at 9.4e7, 866 at 1.44e8, 878 until 2.66e8, 1057 at 3.9e8, 1159 at 1e9, 1219 at 1.45e9).
  • The sea. Almost the whole tape is u = 01001111 in some rotation (10100111, 11010011, ... are the same cyclic word). Between u-runs sit short seams, e.g. (110100)^2 1^6, (010011111)^2 (one extra 1), 0100 1^k, 1 (10100)^2, (10100111111)^2, (01001)^2, plus long 1-runs (deposits).
  • One bounce per cycle (cycle = from one left record in state B to the next). The head sweeps right through the sea at 20 steps per 8-cell block, turns at a wall, and sweeps back at 40 steps per block (Fig: cycle_Q). It never reaches the right end in a quiet epoch; everything right of the wall is fossil (untouched).
  • Per cycle: the left edge moves 3 cells (LT), the left reservoir u^a gains one block, the active sea moves -3 cells (= +5 mod 8), seams ride right at +5 cells per cycle, and the wall eats one block from its left and leaves 111 behind, so the deposit 1^c grows by 3 and the wall walks left 3 cells per cycle.
  • Segment map (img/segmap_1e9.png, img/segmap_2e7.png; scripts/segmap.py): every cell is classified as sea / 1-run / (10)-run / other, bright if it changed since the previous cycle. It shows the light-blue active sea from the left edge to the green turning point, grey wedges of 1-deposits growing left behind the wall, dark-blue fossil u-runs on the right, and red seams riding diagonally right into the wall. When a seam reaches the wall, the deposit is rewritten as a fossil u-run and the wall resumes.
  • Reservoir freezes. At 22 cycles to 2e9 the left reservoir u^a stops growing and becomes a new fixed segment, behind a new seam, and a fresh u^a starts at the left edge. Sizes at the freeze: 9, 20, 12, 41, 62, 45, 61, 59, 156, 74, 402, 11, 606, 678, 29, 752, 641, 1434, 921, 2483, 29, 2535 (steps 9431 ... 1.15e9). The trigger seems to be the head returning from a wall event in a different phase and rewriting seams all the way left in one pass (e.g. cycles 3723-3748), but I have not pinned it down.
  • Distinct seam words between u-runs (compress tokens, 1-runs abstracted; a crude count): 170 by 1e6, 375 by 1e7, 526 by 1e8, 800 by 1e9, 829 by 2e9.

Cryptid check (ground rule 6)

Not flagged. No Collatz-like or parity branching has been found. But the freeze sizes are irregular and the epoch grammar is open, so pseudo-random control cannot be ruled out yet. If the seam-alphabet stage shows that the freezes depend on arithmetic of the reservoir sizes, re-flag as CRYPTID_CANDIDATE.

Proof idea (if funded)

A closed tape language: u-runs of any length separated by seams from a finite set S, plus the wall and deposit forms. Show one cycle maps the language into itself and that no word in it contains the halting pattern 11 0 <C at the head. RS, LS, LT, RO and Q are the inside-epoch lemmas; the seam collisions (seam meets wall, b = 0, freeze) are the finite case table. This is CTL-like, so a mechanised closed-set search over phase-tracked sea tokens is the first experiment.

Closure evidence that points the other way (scripts/fuzz_lengths.py, work/fuzz_lengths.out; seed 7). I took real cycle-start configurations (cycles 300-8,000) and re-drew the run lengths, then simulated each for 5e7 steps: - V0 unchanged: 0/300 halt. - V1 u block counts 3..60 with end phases kept, deposits with c mod 8 kept, (10)-runs with parity kept: 0/300 halt. - V2 deposits and (10)-runs free: 0/300 halt. - V3 u-run lengths free in cells as well, so the phases change: 0/300 halt.

The skeleton language with free exponents does not lead to a halt (1,200 instances). Non-halting does not seem to rest on the length arithmetic. The obstacle is describing the language, not a fragile orbit.

Near-halt census (scripts/census.c, census_b.c; work/census_1e10.txt, work/census_b_1e9.txt). C is entered only by B0 (1LC), and halting needs C to read 0 with 11 on its left. From blank to 1e10, C reads 0 33,051 times, exactly once per cycle and always at the blank left edge. It never reads 0 inside the tape. To 1e9, B reads 0 66.7M times inside the tape and always has a 1 on its left: 50.2M entered from A0 (which writes that 1 itself), and 16.6M entered moving left from D (16,588,734 with 11 on the left, 9 with 01). So non-halting is equivalent to one local invariant: D never steps left onto 0 0 inside the tape (00 <B occurs only at the blank left edge). A closed language needs to carry only that.
