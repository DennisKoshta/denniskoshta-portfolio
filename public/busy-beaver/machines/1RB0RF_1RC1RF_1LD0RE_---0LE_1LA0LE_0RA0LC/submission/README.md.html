1RB0RF_1RC1RF_1LD0RE_---0LE_1LA0LE_0RA0LC never halts

  • Machine: 1RB0RF_1RC1RF_1LD0RE_---0LE_1LA0LE_0RA0LC, a BB(6) holdout. It is on every holdout list from 2024-06-14 through 2026-10-07 (line 748 of the 778-machine list) and on the current 740-machine list (line 710). busycoq's verify/ has no proof of it, under any renaming or mirror.
  • Class: "Bell eats counter" (spreadsheet Results row 1053, no notes or claims).
  • Result: Lemma nonhalt: ~halts tm c0. in Coq 8.20.1 against unmodified busycoq BB6 (commit 0940bb9c). Print Assumptions nonhalt is closed (no axioms). It compiles in 15.5 s with -native-compiler yes, and coqchk re-checks it in 67.7 s (check_kill.txt).

Mechanism

  • A marker bounce feeding a counter. The hub is 0^inf (110)^a 0 (011)^b C> X 0^inf. Each bounce moves the 0 marker one block to the right, adds 1 to the counter word X and spends one 011 of the budget b. X is binary, LSB first: 3-cell blocks d01 (001)^e separated by 000, ending in a 4-stage tail.
  • Partial eats leave a residue. At b = 1 the low block is eaten. An a-block (d = 0) gives new budget. A b-block (or a bare tail) sweeps (110)^a into a left record, and the next epoch restarts with budget N = k + 1 and a residue that is not a clean counter.
  • Recursive digestion. The first bounces of an epoch digest the residue. At a b-block one bounce runs a whole sub-epoch on the rest of the word, and the next level runs nested inside the block it leaves. The machine can only halt at an eat that comes before the digestion ends.

How the proof works

  • Every finite run is one es lemma, with symbolic exponents and any left context. Words have a syntax with an encoding into tapes. The local pass LP and the epoch map Phi are mutually inductive relations (the nested sub-epoch is an LP constructor whose premise is a Phi), and sound turns every derivation into a TM run.
  • The digestion fits in the budget (the write-up's Lemma D). One potential over counter positions bounds the positions a digestion creates. A weighted one, with future positions prepaid, bounds the digestion's cost and the merged block exponents. An invariant on epochs (block count, two weighted exponent masses, the residue's prefix, a lower bound on N) is closed for N ≥ 17,705.
  • nonhalt: init (solve_init to K(1,8,010001)), then 691 epochs of a computable fuel model, proved sound, by vm_compute to N = 17,713, where the invariant holds; then progress_nonhalt_cond with the invariant.
