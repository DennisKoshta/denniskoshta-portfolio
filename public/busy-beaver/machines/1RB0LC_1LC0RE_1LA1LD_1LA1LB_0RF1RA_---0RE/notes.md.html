Mechanism

Turn configuration (C has just turned left at the bell):

R(u, e) = 0^inf u <C 0 1^e 0^inf        u = w 1 (101)^k, k maximal

w is the counter, 1 a separator, (101)^k the middle field, 1^e the bell. enc(v) = v in binary, MSB first, digit 1 = 10, digit 0 = 00.

  1. Bounce (R1, R6). The head sweeps left through the middle field (CDA per block: 101 -> 011), increments the counter at its right end, sweeps right (EAB per block: 011 -> 101) and turns at the bell, which loses 3 cells: R(w 1 (101)^k, e+3) -> R(inc(w) 1 (101)^(k+1), e), steps 6k + 4t + 9 for clean w (t = trailing 1-digits). inc on a clean word is +1 (R1). Separators: 110 is transparent to the carry (R2); a lone 1 before a 00 digit lets the counter after it count until its top digit is 1, then it is a 110 (R3). On all 2,000 sampled real words inc does not depend on k or e (R6).
  2. Eat (R4, R5). When the bell is down to e = r in {0, 2} the next pass fills the middle field with 1s, increments twice and drops the counter's low end: - z = inc(inc(w)) ends in 00: R(p 00 ..) -> R(p 1, 3k+3+3[r=2]) (one digit eaten); - z ends in 10: R(q c 10 ..) -> R(q 101, 3k+2+3[r=2]) (the last 3 cells become a middle block). On clean counters this is R4: v = 2m -> enc(m+1); v = 2m+1 -> enc((m+1)//2) with a 1 101 tail (m+1 odd) or a 010 1 tail (m+1 even). R5 matches all 631 checked real eats (287 of the 289 eats of start A to 5.1e7, skipping the first two, whose counters are shorter than 4 cells; all 344 of start B to 4.2e7). Hence e mod 3 is always 0 or 2 (33,987 / 26,014 turns), never 1.
  3. Certified start B stays on the clean orbit R7: R(enc(s) 1, 3s) -> R(enc(s+1) 1, 3s+3) (s bounces, then an even eat). Only R1 and R4's even case are ever used, which is what the hlin certificate (side_binary_inc_rule on 10/00 digits) covers.
  4. Start A leaves that orbit: the bell and the counter value are not tied (s != e/3), odd eats happen (e = 2 mod 3 cycles), and the 010 tail turns into lone-1 separators inside w that move towards the low end and are eaten later. To 5.1e7: 90 of 289 cycle starts have a junk counter; 83 eats act on junk words, with 29 distinct junk-eat shapes. Every rule above still applies.

2. Token rules (validated)

Read the counter word left to right in tokens: digit 1 = 10, digit 0 = 00, F = 110 (frozen separator), S = a lone 1 followed by 00. scripts/stage1/tokrules.py checks four rules against exact simulation, on 400 random cases each (353 for T4), with 0 failures:

rule statement
T1 F is inert. inc(H 110 L) = H 110 (L+1); if L is all ones, the carry passes through F: (H+1) 110 0^len(L)
T2 S freezes on its first carry. inc(H 1 00 L) = H 1 00 (L+1); if L is all ones: H 110 0^len(L) (the carry stops there)
T3 Odd eat on clean z = H 1 0^m 1 (bits): w' = H S enc(0^m 1), i.e. S takes the place of the lowest 1 above bit 0; if m = 0, w' = H with k' = 1
T4 Even eat on clean z = p 0: w' = p

So junk is made of particles: - Each odd eat creates an S at the lowest 1 above bit 0. - The S turns into an F at the first carry into it. - Each F stays on its cells. Every eat brings it one digit closer to the low end, where it is eaten. - An F that reaches the low end is absorbed into the middle field at the eat (k0' = 1 or 2; a stack of F's gives a larger k0'). - An odd eat whose S would land inside an F (z = H F 0^m 1, m >= 1) leaves shift junk instead: an 11 pair, E, giving the word H 11 0^m 1. It clears within a few bounces. These are the 10 words of the run to 5.1e7 that do not tokenize (steps 5.30e6 and 1.14e7).

Of the 59,991 turn words that tokenize (to 5.1e7), each has exactly one tokenization. Junk shapes seen: F d, F F d, F d F d, S d, S, S d F d, F S, F S d.

5. Coupled closure (window on n)

scripts/stage1/cclos_*.c compute the closure of the cycle map when the adversary may pick any cycle length n inside a window tied to the counter. The check also tests whether n' (from the real rule) lands in the successor's window.

Window maintenance. The value window is almost inductive. On the 20-cell value closure (42.0M checks), n' leaves the window 2,875 times (0.007%). - 99% of the 18-cell exits (1,270 of 1,283, data/stage1/value_window_violations_18.txt) come from unparsable predecessors. - These are E words, H 11 0^m 1, made when an odd eat puts its S into an F (z = H F 0^m 1). - An E resolves within a few carries, mostly into FF. In doing so it can cut the live value by a factor of about 4: H = 1001, m = 4 gives an effective value of 65 against H·2^(m+1) = 288. The effective value is not a simple function of H (H = 100 gives a negative offset). - The other exits come from S at the top (z = 1 0^m 1 gives v' = 1 while n' stays large) and from F near the top.

So the invariant needs a value with pending-loss terms for S, E and a top F, or wider windows for those shapes. Ordinary F and S particles keep the window as it is.

Effective value (first try at the pending-loss terms). scripts/stage1/cclos_veff.c sets v_eff(w) = V(inc^J(w)) - J, with J = 2^ceil(|w|/2). After J bounces every particle has had a carry, so v_eff is the value the counter behaves as from then on. The window becomes v_eff - 1 <= n <= 10(v_eff + 2). Each state's incoming n' range is checked against its own window when v_eff >= 32.

max cells states states checked window exits
18 8,083 4,664 0
20 20,770 13,443 6

The 6 exits at 20 cells: 5 low (n' = v_eff - 3 at v_eff = 33..35, or n' = 1 handed on by exempt small states) and 1 high (not inspected). The lower ratio bound has to be 0.1 rather than 0.2: an S created high up over a small H (for example 10 00 S 00 00 00 00 00 10) leaves v_eff/n near 0.12.

The 2 bell-residue-1 events come from one exempt state with v_eff < 32 that was explored at n = 1. They are not real dynamics, since n only grows. So an effective-value window, with ratio in [0.1, 1] and a few units of slack at the lower end, is the candidate invariant. The open part is a closed form for v_eff per junk shape.

1. Closed form for the effective value (validated)

Tokens as in stage 1: 10 = digit 1, 00 = digit 0, F = 110, S = a lone 1 together with the 00 after it (100). - A normal digit is a 10 or 00 that is not S's own 00. - The weight of a token is 2^(number of normal digits below it).

Phi(w) = sum of weights of the normal 1-digits  -  sum of weights of the S tokens

Why: the time until the first carry leaves a tail X, and the period after that, follow a recursion. A 1-digit keeps the time and doubles the period. A 0-digit adds one period and doubles it. F changes neither. S adds one period but does not double it, because S's 0 freezes into F at its first carry. Phi(w) = P(w*) - T(w) gives the formula above. It is exactly "a pending loss of one place value per live S".

Checks (scripts/stage2/phi.h, cclos_phi.c): Phi equals the simulated v_eff = V(inc^J w) - J on every parsable closure state, with 0 mismatches on 2,416 / 5,989 / 15,089 states at 16 / 18 / 20 cells. Words that do not parse (E junk, a lone 1 at the low end, 11 at the top) fall back to the simulated value: 842 / 2,094 / 5,681 states, needing at most 31 / 31 / 63 incs.

2. The bounce at every length (validated, with a cell-level argument)

G0 = canonical words in (10 | 00 | 110 | 100)*. On G0 the carry runs right to left: - C or D reading 0 writes 1 and goes to A. - A reading 1 writes 0 and goes to C (a 1-digit, now 01). - A reading 0 stops: 00 becomes 10 via B and E. - C reading 1 goes to D (the top cell of an F, now 101). - D never reads 1 inside G0.

The right sweep in E then restores 01 to 00 and 101 to 110. S never sees the carry, since the carry stops at S's own 00, which leaves 110 = F. So inc maps G0 to G0, never halts, and adds exactly 1 to Phi, at any length. An F that reaches the low end is absorbed into the middle field.

Exhaustive check (g0check.py 16): all 6,111 canonical G0 words up to 16 cells, 0 failures.

So the two bounds that hold on every reachable state (n >= Phi + 1 and n >= 2^(D-1)) are not inductive. What keeps the real set inside G* is a memory of the n at which the fresh S or the L was made. That n is about 2^(depth) and is not a function of the word. A closed invariant would have to carry it as a third coordinate, for example (w, n, n at the last odd eat), or a per-particle "time to freeze". That is a new design, not a formula for g.
