0. The map

twin 0 1 parent 0 1
A 1RB 0RE ↦ D 1LA 0LE
B 1LC 0RA ↦ A 1RB 0LD
C 1LD 0LB ↦ B 1RC 0RA
D 1LA 1LF ↦ C 1RD 1RF
E 1LE 1RA ↦ E 1RE 1LD
F 0LC --- ↦ F 0RB ---

The renaming is φ: A↦D, B↦A, C↦B, D↦C, E↦E, F↦F, and every direction flips (L↔R). For every twin entry, the entry (write w, move d, go to q′) in row q becomes the parent's entry in row φ(q), which is (w, flip(d), φ(q′)). This holds for 12 of 12 entries, including the undefined F1 ↦ F1. No other renaming works, with or without the mirror: check_proof.py §M tries all 2 × 720 candidates and finds exactly this one. A renaming without the mirror never fits.

Lemma C (conjugacy). Let μ(q, (l, s, r)) := (φ(q), (r, s, l)), which swaps the two sides of the tape and renames the state. Mirroring the tape turns move_left into move_right and back. So c -[twin]-> c' holds iff μ c -[parent]-> μ c', and c is halted for the twin iff μ c is halted for the parent. By induction on the number of steps, the twin halts from c0 iff the parent halts from μ(c0) = (D, tape0), after the same number of steps. In the .v, the direction that is needed (twin halts ⇒ parent halts from (D, tape0)) is perm_twin plus busycoq's flip_halts and perm_halts (§4).

Why this is not "identical up to renaming". μ(c0) is (D, tape0), not the parent's c0 = (A, tape0). The twin is the parent run from a different start, and its orbit is a different orbit of the same family. K0′ below is not K0. No canonical configuration of the twin occurs among the parent's in the first 3·10⁵ steps. In lockstep, the two abstract orbits differ at every macro step, by one unit (§3.9).

Halting transition. Only D1 (1LF) enters F, and only C0 (1LD) enters D. So the twin halts exactly when it reaches 1 1 0 <C, the mirror image of the parent's B> 0 1 1.

3.9 Why the trajectories look the same (informational; not used). Run in lockstep, the two abstract orbits apply the same rule at every macro step, and their states differ by exactly +1 on one far-end block. check_proof.py §L checked this to L = 120, about 2^124 macro steps. The extra unit moves around a fixed loop: - it is on the far q at an E-start; - the E-cycle's Ov moves it to the new last pair's p (t₀, a tail block); - EndLast moves it into the tail T1; - the next Ov moves it back to the far q.

On an X1 detour it rides T2(k) and then X2's new pair (3, k, 1). It is never on x or on an interior block, which is why the rule sequence cannot differ. It also explains the 67,345 vs 67,343 cells at 10⁹: one extra 2-cell block, on the mirrored side.
