The key change of view: visits, not trips. Count visits at a fixed block boundary, as busycoq's Longitudinal segRLs does (TM31 in SBCv8.v uses the same machinery). A visit is the head crossing the boundary rightward and coming back. Blocks are 4 cells in 2-mod-4 alignment (block k = cells 2+4k..5+4k). The boundary at cell 18 sits between the inner zone and the MS word. After a pause ends, the visits there are exactly DC^a AC AC AC ... (D or A in, C out). Every rule below is per visit.

Inner zone (exact, finite table). At the pause end the inner zone (s3', t0, t1) emits a DC visits at cell 18, then only AC visits. a is a fixed 27-entry table (scripts/exact/validate_cl_tab.py; for example PRQ -> 4, PQR -> 5, PRR -> 0). It does not depend on the MS word: 27/27 states, 8 random words each (data/exact/innertable.txt).

CL, the sweep (exact). Read the word from cell 18 as ternary digits, LSB first, with .=0, 2=1, 1=2. 0 = 0000 is a transient junk block. The frontier is the first non-. block. - A DC visit adds 2 to the word, with the LSB at cell 22 (d1). - An AC visit acts on the frontier: - frontier 0 -> . - frontier 2 -> 0 - frontier 1 -> ., and the rest of the word gets +4. Inside the visit the 1 block cycles x -> v -> P -> d -> ., sending four B signals right, and a B signal is a ternary increment (.->2 stop, 2->1 stop, 1->pass). - an all-. word passes the visit on into the middle. - Glider = 12, the only fixed point: 12 -> .12 (1 + 4 = 5 = 12). Funded stage (2026-10-10, f3_first)

Scope (decision f3_first): find a closed invariant family for zone T under the MO bursts B^j A^n, decode the big-region counter (0011/0111/0001), and test j up to 1e6 natively. Stop rule: if no closed family emerges, the far-future regime (m ~ 236 on) is open, so park or make a priority call. New scripts are in scripts/f3/. tzone.c is a C zone-T driver with a per-visit step cap (TZCAP); tz.py wraps it and has the tower parser. New data is in data/f3/.

Result: no closed family. The big region is a fourth zone with the same shape as M. Its gliders build new throttle levels at the right end, so the far future is an unbounded tower of levels. A T level is safe for the burst shapes tested so far, but not for every shape the deeper levels can receive. The stop rule applies.

1. The big region BR is an M-type ternary word (exact). Read the region after the marker as 4-cell blocks (3-mod-4 alignment, LSB next to the marker): s = 0011 (0), y = 0001 (1), x = 0111 (2), _ = 0000 (junk). Its left boundary sees only AC and DC, the same alphabet as b18. - BA: an A visit adds 1 at block 0. - BD: a D visit follows the frontier rule: _ -> s, y -> _, x -> s with +4 at the next block. If the word is all s, the visit runs to the end and appends one s (a record).

This is M with the roles relabelled: BR's A is half of M's D, and BR's D is M's A. So CL's map g, the glider xy (value 5) and the length bound all carry over. Validation (validate_br.py, 2 seeds × 300 random words, --nojunk): 40,766/40,766 visits exact, every answer C (data/f3/validate_br.txt).

2. Interface T -> BR (exact). - IA: an A visit sends D^c to BR, where c is the carry out of the throttle. At K = -1 it sends D D (that is the e+2). - IB: a B visit on an all-P throttle passes as A A (+2). The throttle is unchanged.

Validation (validate_iface.py, 2 seeds × 400 random throttles K = -1..4 with random BR words): IA 16,971/16,971, IB 2,637/2,637, with answers E at 618 and C at the BR boundary (data/f3/validate_iface.txt). So an MO burst B^j becomes +2j' on BR, where j' is j minus the B's the throttle frontier absorbs. The A-run then sweeps it with g, using one D per throttle carry-out.

3. New levels (exact in tests). - GX: a BR glider leaves the end of BR as exactly five B visits (BE^5). Later passes through a clean BR arrive there as A visits (AE). - NB: five B's on blank tape build a new throttle level 1 θ2 Q (0011)^e2 with K2 = 3. In general, B^j on blank tape builds K = j-2 (checked for j = 1..10). From then on BR1 stops growing. Each level hands the next one the same {A, B} -> E interface that M hands T.

validate_gx.py seed 2 (300 random bursts with small e): 33 runs built a second level, GX 33/33 and NB 33/33 (K2 = 3 in all 33). 256 runs kept one level and 11 ended in a dead slot (data/f3/validate_gx_seed2.txt). So the closed family has to be a tower [θ1 | BR1] [θ2 | BR2] ... (tz.parse_levels). A new level is added each time the last BR emits a glider, so the tower is unbounded.

4. B^j A^n with j up to 1e6 (bigburst.py, C). - Small e: 300 runs, K = -1..2, e = 30..60, j up to 991,164, A^2600 (data/f3/bigburst_small_e_seed11.jsonl). Results: 0 halts, every answer E. 250 runs end with one canonical level, 48 with a two-level tower (K2 = 3), and 2 with a dead slot. Both dead slots came from j = 1. - Large e: 85 runs (stopped at the time limit), e = 2000..4000, A^20000, j up to 864,393 (data/f3/bigburst_large_e_seed12.jsonl): 0 halts, all E, every run in the tower family. 76 runs keep one level, sometimes with a glider still in flight in BR1, and 9 have a second level.

5. What a level is safe against (multiburst.py, twoburst.py; data/f3/multiburst*.txt, twoburst.txt). - Safe for glider bursts: - B^5 A^n B^5 A^2600 on every canonical throttle with K <= 3, n = 0..30: 3,751/3,751 safe. - 4 bursts of B^5 with random gaps 0..8 or 0..40: 400/400 safe. - 5 bursts of B^4 with gaps 0..6: 200/200 safe. - Not safe for small bursts with short gaps: - B^1 (6 bursts, gaps 0..3): 15/200 halt, and 28 more answer C or D. - B^2 (5 bursts, gaps 0..6): 1/200 halts. - B^3 (5 bursts, gaps 0..6): 2/200 halt. One example is T(RP; 34) on B3 A4 B3 B3 A6 B3 A4 B3, which halts in the 5th burst. - An interleaved word with junk on BR (A on junk, item 6) also halts (data/f3/halt_interleaved_example.txt).

These are real halts of zone T on constructed input words. They are not halts of the machine.

6. Further non-canonical modes. - JA (A on junk): an A visit that meets a junk frontier turns it into a permanent 0110 block. After that, BR only counts up (A +1, D +6) and never sweeps again. - Dead slots: a single B (j = 1) on some throttles leaves a dead slot, as in F2 at K = 3, and it can persist. Dead slots were reached in 2/300 runs with j up to 1e6 and 11/300 with j up to 1e4. - Dead slot plus short A-runs: epochs.py runs repeated level-1 epochs B^j A^2600, with up to 8 rounds and j up to 3,000 (data/f3/epochs_seed31.jsonl). Of 60 runs, 0 halted, but 11 answered C or D on a later burst. Every failure came after a j = 1 round: 11 of the 34 runs that had a j = 1 round failed, and none of the others did. Two of them were replayed: RQP e=40 with j = 169, 5, 1, 14, 14, and QPP e=33 with j = 20, 9, 4, 1, 2, 1019. Before the failing burst both are in a dead-slot variant Q [1101] P Q with an unswept BR word. With A^100000 per round, the same j sequences stay canonical and safe (11/11 rounds). So level 1 also needs a quantitative "the A-run is long enough" lemma, plus a rule for what a single-B epoch does. j_m = 1 epochs can occur when MO starts.

7. Burst sizes the deeper levels can receive (BR arithmetic, exitsizes.py). - A glider exit is 1 + 4 units, contiguous because BR is not clean in between. - If new units reach BR while the glider is between its two carries, the rest of the exit is 4 units (2,637/3,000 cases) or 1 + 1 (363/3,000), so the next level gets B^5 or B^3. - Gaps between bursts at level k+1 are the clean passes of BR_k, and they can be anything from 0 up.

At level 1, M guarantees one burst per epoch followed by >= 2,700 A's. Below level 1 nothing guarantees a shape: B^3 bursts and short gaps each need a level-1 burst to land within a few D visits of a BR1 event. In the MO regime that is heuristically a chance of about 2^-m per epoch, from m ~ 236 on. The machine almost surely never meets the unsafe patterns, but a proof has to exclude them. That needs control of pseudo-random timing (burst times against glider exits), which is cryptid-like for a formal proof.

Verdict. F3 has no closed family; the stop rule applies. - The near-term argument (m < ~236, proof.md section 6 given F1 and F2) is unchanged. - From m ~ 236 on, the regime opens an unbounded tower of levels with K = 3 births, dead slots, JA and short-gap bursts. The zone tests show that T levels are not robust to all of these. - 0 halts of the full machine in any test. The zone T halts above come from constructed input words that the level-1 burst shape excludes and that the deeper levels could only receive through timing coincidences.

Confidence in non-halting stays med, on heuristic grounds. Confidence in a proof along this route is low.

Still open. - A tower invariant that includes dead slots, JA, K = 3 births and the input languages between levels. - A timing lemma: bursts never land in a sweep or exit window. It may be unprovable without number theory. - The safe input language of a T level. It is finite-state in the throttle head and could be computed, but that is a research stage. - Recommendation: park R8. A new decision.json asks for that priority call.
