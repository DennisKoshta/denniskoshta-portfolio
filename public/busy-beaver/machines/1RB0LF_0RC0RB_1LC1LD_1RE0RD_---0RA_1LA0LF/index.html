Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Tape = alternating 10 background plus a few walls (00, 11, (010)^2, dense 1011 blocks).
  • Sweeps flip 10<->01 and bounce off walls; phases (fixed sweep programs) chain into epochs, span x3 per epoch.

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

Per-phase cycle lemmas (es + ind), chained with follow into Epoch: P(a,c,T) -->+ P(3a+6,3a+c+2,T'). Then induct over epochs with progress_nonhalt_cond (invariant: a even, c odd, Inv(T)). Inv(T) = tail language closed under one epoch, via Longitudinal sideRLs/segRLs_n (Bouncer_v3, LongNv1) or LongN closed sets.

  • ▲Tail T is rewritten every epoch (73-100% of it is read); its rule T->T' is unknown and may be chaotic.
  • ▲Each epoch has many 5-7-counter phases; a hand proof could exceed 50 KB.
  • ▲Halt pattern D> 1^k 00 exists and the tail has 0^2/0^3 walls; non-halting depends on the tail invariant.
