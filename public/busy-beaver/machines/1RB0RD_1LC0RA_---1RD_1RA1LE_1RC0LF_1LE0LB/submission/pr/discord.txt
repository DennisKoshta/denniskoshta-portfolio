Informal write-up: attached

Watched each time the head reaches new ground on the right, the tape is a binary counter, and every carry burns fuel in each digit it touches. A digit that runs dry sheds a spare wall toward the head (walls drift right and refill the digits there), but a digit with a single marker has none to shed. New digits are born at the right end, and the time between births grows by a ratio that oscillates with growing swings; when the third swing crosses 2, a single-marker digit is left with bit 0 and no fuel, and the next carry into it halts the machine.
