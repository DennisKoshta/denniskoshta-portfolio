Halting condition (from the table alone)

  • Only B0 and E0 enter C, and C reads 0 = halt.
  • E0 → 1RC moves onto the cell that D1 or F0 just wrote as 1, so it is safe.
  • B0 → 1LC after A0 is safe too (A just wrote the cell C reads).
  • B0 → 1LC after F1 (0LB, moving left) halts iff that cell is 0.
  • So the machine halts iff the head in F reads a 1 whose two left neighbours are 0 0.
  • F is entered only from E1. Inside an alternating stretch a left sweep in F/E never meets this, so the danger is a 00 wall with gap 0 before 1s (0011).

Wall view (the representation that works)

Read the tape left of the head at a right record (state A on the blank cell). - A wall is a run of ≥ 2 equal symbols: 00, 11, 1111, 1^5, 1^n. - A gap is the number of cells between two walls; those cells alternate. - The 11 walls cut the tape into units. A unit is 11 x M m_1 P m_2 … P y: - lead marker M ∈ {00, 1111} (the bit: 00 = 0, 1111 = 1); - k − 1 passive walls P = 1111, so k markers in all; - gaps x (left), m_i (inside), y (right, the fuel). - The tail is 11 t (t = 2a − 1 odd, a = number of increments since the last reset). - Left of the first 11 is the prefix (the fossil zone). - In pair notation a k = 1 unit is (10)^p [1111] (01)^q, #11's digit shape with marker 1111 instead of 00. - Tools: work/walls.py, work/unitlib.py, work/build.py (builds a tape from wall tokens).

Rule M (one increment = one right record in state A): exact

The carry runs from the right. Every unit with lead 1111 is passed; the first unit with lead 00 stops it. - Passed unit: 1111 → 00, x += 3. - Stopping unit: 00 → 1111, x −= 1. - Every touched unit, k = 1: y −= 1. - Every touched unit, k ≥ 2: m_1 += 1, y −= 2. - Untouched units and the prefix are unchanged; t += 2. - Steps (old values; for k = 1 there are no m terms): - 2t; - plus, per passed unit, 20 + 16(k−1) + 2(x + Σm + y); - plus, for the stopping unit, 16 + 16(k−1) + 2(Σm + y).

Validated: - 27,033 / 27,033 orbit increments to 1e9 whose shape M covers: exact wall lists, 0 mismatches (work/unitcheck.py; 14,702/14,702 to 3e8, 4,530/4,530 to 3e7). - 3,000 / 3,000 random constructed instances (work/randM.py): 1–6 units, up to 3 markers each, random leads, random stop unit, a fossil-like prefix. - The step formula fits all 3,000 exactly (work/fitsteps.py, exact rational fit, max error 0).

So level 0 is #11's per-carry M macro with a different encoding. Every touch burns fuel, and the bit sense matches #11 (00 = 0).

Fossil zone = Pascal's triangle

The prefix is a list of rows. Row k is a block 1^{n_k}, then k passive walls, with inner gaps 2·C(k,i) − 1 (i = 1..k−1).

At 2.73e9 the prefix reads:

1^690 | 1^526 P | 1^283 P 3 P | 1^151 P 5 P 5 P | 1^82 P 7 P 11 P 7 P | 1^46 P 9 P 19 P 19 P 9 P | 1^25 P 11 P 29 P 7 …
  • Rows 1–5 are exact.
  • Row 6 (11, 29, 39, 29, 11) is complete inside unit 1 at 2.49e8.
  • Row 7 is forming at 2.73e9 (15 55 111 139 111 57 vs 2C(8,i)−1 = 15 55 111 139 111 55 15).
  • The leading blocks grow by 3 per overflow; n_k ≈ ×1.85 per row.
  • One new row per ~1 reset generation, so the number of walls grows like log t: 28 → 46 walls, 8 → 9 units between 2e6 and 2.5e8.

Cryptid check

Not cryptid-like. - All updates are linear with exact formulas (M; the clean O; the R end shape). - The fossil zone is binomial (Pascal), not pseudo-random. No parity- or mod-driven branching was seen beyond binary carries. - The irregular part is when glue/split events happen (which unit's fuel runs out first). That is fuel arithmetic, the same kind as #11's drops.

Comparison with #3 / #11 / TM21

  • #3 (down-counter, per-step J with ghost phase): does not fit; this counter increments.
  • #11 / TM21: level 0 is #11's per-carry M (one macro step per carry, fuel burned per touch), so the per-step INV approach is the right shape for the active units. But: 1. There is no global re-pairing O. New digits enter at the right (R: the tail becomes a new LSB unit's fuel), and old units fossilize at the left. 2. Units carry passive markers (k up to 10 by 1e9). Their fuel burns 2 per touch, and they glue and split (G, S). 3. The fossil zone is an unbounded Pascal triangle, i.e. a multi-level structure whose depth grows like log t. #11's INV (three phases, 2^j bounds over one digit list) cannot express it.
  • R4 / #32 (fossil-K walls): a similar level of difficulty, but a different mechanism and encoding. No shared rules.

Open (stages 2–3)

  1. Exact rules for G, S, the prefix-entering carries and R (rule closure). Then validate them all on the orbit, aiming at 27,818/27,818 to 1e9.
  2. An invariant: - every k = 1 fuel ≥ 1; - glue happens only for k ≥ 2; - an inductive description of the rows, with fuel bounds per row derived from the binomial gaps.

Natural route: a per-step INV over the unit list as in #11, plus a recursive row predicate for the prefix. Feasibility is unknown; it is far beyond a delta of #11.

Representation

Read the wall tokens left of the head at an A right record (as in stage 1). Every 1-block is classified by its length mod 3:

token class meaning
1^(3g+2): 11, 1^5, 1^8, … boundary a 11 with g passive walls of the left unit glued on (g = 0 is a plain 11)
1^(3j+1): 1111, 1^7, 1^10, … passive Q a stack of j glued 1111 walls
00 / 1111 right after a boundary lead M the bit (00 = 0, 1111 = 1)
the leftmost block 1^e edge E grows at the left end

The tape is

E U_0 b_1 U_1 b_2 … b_N U_N b_T [t] A>
  • U₀ (left boundary E): leadless [x, Q, m, …, Q, y], empty [y], or a digit.
  • U_j, j ≥ 1: a digit [x, M, m_1, Q, m_2, …, Q, y] with k − 1 passive walls; y is the fuel.
  • The tail [t]. For one increment after a reset there is no tail.

The stage-1 "prefix" is not special: it is E, U₀ and the units whose boundaries are glued blocks (1^5, 1^11, …), and its big blocks are passive stacks.

The rules

The carry runs from the right and treats one unit at a time.

situation rule
tail, boundary 11 t += 2, continue
digit, lead 1111, boundary 11 M pass: lead → 00, x += 3; k = 1: y −= 1; k ≥ 2: m₁ += 1, y −= 2; continue
digit, lead 1111, boundary 1^(3g+2), g ≥ 1 S unglue (below); continue
digit, lead 00 M stop: lead → 1111, x −= 1; fuel as in M; stop
leadless unit every wall +2: x += 2, y −= 2; continue
empty unit continue
U₀ a digit with lead 1111 (E then has e ≡ 1 mod 3) the lead moves like a passive wall: x += 2, y −= 2; continue
edge, e ≡ 0 mod 3 e += 3 (one cell left, two right); U₀'s first gap −2
edge, e ≡ 1 mod 3 E becomes U₀'s first passive wall: U₀ = [1, 1^e, x₀, …]; a new edge 111 starts two cells further left

Unglue S (the left unit's glued walls enter this unit; the first one becomes the lead and is passed at once; the old lead 1111 turns passive; the boundary becomes 11):

g = 1:  [x, 1111, m_1, …, y]  ->  [2, 00, x+1, 1111, m_1, …, y-2]
g ≥ 2:  [x, 1111, m_1, …, y]  ->  [2, 00, 0, 1^(3g-2), x, 1111, m_1, …, y-2]

Glue G: whenever a last gap reaches −1, the last passive wall 1^q merges with the boundary 1^r on its right into 1^(q+r−1). If U₀ is empty and its gap reaches −1, E absorbs b₁ instead (e += r − 1; U₁ becomes U₀).

Reset R (two increments):

… 1^5 [t]          ->  … 11 [2, 00, t+2]            (no tail; the carry continues left)
… 11 [2, 00, t+2]  ->  … 11 [1, 1111, t] 11 [1]     (the digit stops the carry; new tail)

Event counts on the orbit (27,813 increments, step 342 → 1e9): - plain M: 27,255; - overflows: 373 with e ≡ 0, 10 with e ≡ 1; - absorptions: 10; - resets: 17 (16 first halves in the window); - unglue: 202 with g = 1, 47 with g ≥ 2 (g up to 52); - glue: 98 for k ≥ 2 units, 106 for leadless units, 3 in U₀, 22 right after an unglue.

What the rules say

  • Binary counter. Every touch flips a digit's bit. U_j is touched exactly once per two touches of U_{j+1}.
  • Markers flow right.
  • A lead moves 1 cell per touch on average (x: −1, +3 alternately).
  • A passive wall moves 2 cells per touch.
  • When the last passive wall reaches the boundary it glues. At the next touch of its unit it becomes the right neighbour's lead, and that neighbour's old lead turns passive.
  • Edge and fossils.
  • The edge grows by 3 per overflow.
  • When U₀ runs empty, E absorbs b₁ (e ≡ 1 mod 3). At the next overflow it fossilizes as a passive stack, and a fresh 111 starts.
  • On the orbit the edge fossilized 10 times, at sizes 10, 10, 13, 19, 28, 40, 58, 91, 157, 286 (steps 884 … 365,107,435).
  • The stacks 1^286, 1^154, 1^85, 1^49, 1^25, 1^10 seen at 1e9 descend from these blocks, less the walls they have shed.
  • Stacks make the Pascal rows. A stack of g walls leaves one wall behind (the new lead) in each unit it enters, and travels on as a stack of g − 1.
  • Bounded width. The unit count stays at 8–9: one absorption per reset on average (8 resets and 8 absorptions in the 3M-increment run).

The only danger: the k = 1 fuel race

Under the rules, the machine halts only if a single-marker unit (k = 1) runs out of fuel. Every other precondition held in all 3,000,000 macro increments, for example: - odd last gaps for k ≥ 2; - x ≥ 0; - only 1^5 ever glues to the tail.

A k = 1 unit j waits for its left neighbour i to hand it a wall (the rescue). Write T for the number of touches of i until the rescue, and bit for j's bit. Then: - j gets n_r = 2T − bit touches up to and including the rescue; - its fuel just before the rescue is y − n_r + 1.

So μ = y − n_r must stay ≥ 0. It is constant under ordinary touches: - a stop touch lowers y by 1 and raises bit, so n_r drops by 1; - a pass touch lowers y by 1, T by 1 and bit from 1 to 0, so n_r drops by 1.

T is local:

neighbour i with k ≥ 2 (or leadless), boundary b_j unglued:  T = (y_i + 1)/2 + 1
boundary b_j glued:                                           T = 1
neighbour i with k = 1:                                       T = (y_i + n_r(i))/2 + 1
  • Check (work/margin.py, work/margin_epochs.py; 400k increments from step 54,043; 66 episodes):
  • between resets the prediction is constant and equals the fuel left before the rescue, in all 66;
  • a reset (its no-tail touch takes one extra cell) lowers it by 1 in 12 episodes and leaves it unchanged in 28;
  • 26 episodes see no reset.
  • Started at step 342 instead (1.5M increments, 93 episodes): the prediction is exact within the last reset epoch in all 93. Across a reset it drops by 1 in 20 episodes; in the first bootstrap episodes it can also rise (e.g. 1 → 3 at increment 9).
  • Slack (work/fuelrace.py, 94 episodes over 1.5M increments from step 342): the fraction of the starting fuel left at rescue is:
  • 0.178–0.197 at depth 1 (next to the tail);
  • 0.36–0.41 at depth 2;
  • 0.54–0.80 at depth 3;
  • 0.71–0.99 at depth 4.
  • The minimum fuel at rescue past 1e9 is 2,383. It grows about ×1.8 per reset generation.
  • The depth-1 race is the reset race. The LSB unit born at reset n gets fuel 2a_n − 1 and must survive until reset n+1, a_{n+1} increments later. So non-halting needs a_{n+1} < 2a_n for every n.
  • a_n (25 resets: 17 on the orbit, 8 in the macro run): 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, 17, 28, 50, 93, 173, 317, 574, 1036, 1875, 3407, 6205, 11304, 20577, 37419, 67969, 123257, 222949, 401914, 721916, 1292815.

Comparison with the templates (updated)

  • Level 0 is still #11's per-carry M.
  • New compared with #11: walls migrate between digits (glue/unglue), digits are created at the right (R) and absorbed at the left (edge), and an unbounded, Pascal-structured fossil zone feeds the digits.

How we got past 3M increments

work/fast.py is an exact accelerated form of rules3.step. - Between structural events every unit changes linearly in its touch count. - The touch counts follow binary-counter arithmetic: a unit with q digits to its right is touched ((C mod 2^q) + d) >> q times in d increments, where C is the counter value of those digits. - So the simulator jumps to one increment before the next structural event and runs that increment with rules3.step. - Structural events: glue, unglue, k = 1 fuel-out, resets, absorption, and any carry that reaches U₀ while U₀ is a digit or the edge is not ≡ 0 mod 3.

Step formulas (work/steps_exact/costs.py)

An increment costs the sum of its carry actions' costs, computed from the old values. Notation: - Σm is the sum of a unit's inner gaps. - Qc sums (14L − 8)/3 over the unit's passive walls 1^L: 16 for a 1111, 14j + 2 for a stack of j walls.

action (rules3 branch) TM steps
tail 11 [t] 2t + 11
glued tail 1^5 [t] (reset, first half) 2t + 24
no tail: stop on [x, 00, …, y] (reset, second half) 2(Σm + y) + Qc + 12
digit pass, boundary 1^(3g+2) (g = 0: rule M; g ≥ 1: unglue) 2(x + Σm + y) + Qc + 14g + 20
digit stop (any boundary, U₀ included) 2(Σm + y) + Qc + 5
leadless U₀ 2(x + Σm + y) + Qc + 3
empty U₀ [y] 2y + 3
U₀ digit with lead 1111 (edge ≡ 1) 2(x + Σm + y) + Qc + 19
edge e ≡ 0 (growth or absorption) 14e/3
edge e ≡ 1 (fossilization) (14e − 2)/3
glue, absorption 0 extra

Stage 1's M formula is the case g = 0 with the tail's 11 added to the stop constant: 5 + 11 = 16.

  • How the formulas were found. An exact rational fit over all orbit increments, with constants per action. The constants were then fixed by per-region step attribution on the TM (work/steps_exact/attrib.py).
  • Which constants matter. Only sums of constants are identified by the orbit. Every action pattern on the long run lies in the span of the orbit patterns, so the split between them cannot change N.
  • The one class absent from the orbit: an empty-U₀ overflow without absorption. It fixed the empty-U₀ constant at 3 (constructed tapes).

Batches (work/steps_exact/stepsexact.py). A batch adds closed-form sums over the touches of each unit: - Tail: 2dt + 2d(d − 1) + 11d. - Digit with m touches: p passes and s stops alternate from its current bit. Σm + y drops by 1 per touch, and x rises by 2 per pass/stop pair. The sum is m·Qc + 2m·S₀ − m(m − 1) + 20p + 2p·x_first + 2p(p − 1) + 5s. - Leadless U₀: every overflow leaves x unchanged and lowers y by 2, so the sum is m·(const) − 2m(m − 1). - Edge: e rises by 3 per overflow, so the sum is m(14e₀/3) + 7m(m − 1).

Last three increments (work/steps_exact/final3.py, certify.py): - 091: unit 21 is passed with fuel 1 → [x+3, 00, 0], and the carry stops at unit 17. The table above applies. - 092: the LSB stops. - 093: the carry passes the LSB and halts in unit 21. It costs t + x₂₂ + 24 (start-of-093 values; the halting step included).

Representation

  • Wall list (work/indep/walls.py): the tape left of the head at a right record in state A is written 0^inf g1 W1 g2 W2 … Wn g.
  • W = a maximal run of ≥ 2 equal symbols.
  • g = the number of alternating cells between two walls.
  • The encoding is lossless and covers every tape, so events need no special shapes.
  • L1 (work/indep/l1.py): an exact TM simulator on run-length pieces.
  • It moves through an alternating piece in one go when the table has a pair q/q2 that flips cells in the same direction. For this table the pairs are E/F going left, A/D and A/B going right.
  • It has no unit rules.
  • Long 1-walls (> 40) are shrunk to a short wall of the same length mod 3, and restored afterwards. A long wall is crossed by a 3-periodic process (1^3 B1 001 → B1 001 011, 9 steps). Each shrunk run is done twice, with two replacement lengths, and the restored results must agree.

Rule G: one rule for a carry (work/indep/rule_g.py)

Classify the walls:

class walls effect of a passing carry
sep 11 fixed
lead (counter bit) the first wall right of a sep, if 00 or 1111; a 00 right of a long wall (touched at most once per batch) pass 1111→00: left edge +3, right edge +1; stop 00→1111: left edge −1, right edge +1
shift other 1111; 1-walls of length ≥ 7 and ≡ 1 mod 3; a 1111 at gap 1 right of such a wall both edges +2
left the leftmost wall: length ≥ 7, ≡ 0 mod 3, gap 0 to the blank, a long wall at gap 1 to its right length +3
irr everything else (1^5 glue, 1^3, other residues, …) must not be reached
  • An increment carries through the leads with bit 1 and stops at the first 0. Every wall right of the stop is passed, and the head moves +2.
  • A wall with r leads to its right is passed floor((V+N)/2^r) − floor(V/2^r) times in N increments from counter value V.
  • Domain:
  • the carry never reaches an irr wall;
  • shrinking gaps stay valid (P to sep ≥ 1, lead to sep ≥ 0);
  • a stopping lead has x ≥ 2.
  • Rule M of stage 1 is the special case with no long walls. The fossil-zone overflow, the transparent 1^n 0 1111 passes and the units bounded by long walls are also rule G.
  • Everything outside the domain is one exact L1 increment: glue, unglue, resets, P migration, fossilisation, fuel-outs, and the halt itself.
