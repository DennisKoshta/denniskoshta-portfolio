  • Unit view. Read the tape in 3-cell units, in the alignment of BECv1 tm108 (w0 = 101, w0' = 010, w1 = 00):
  • A-swept units are s = 010 (spacer), z = 000 (digit 0) and o = 100 (digit 1).
  • B-swept units are S = 101 and D = 111 (a carried digit).
  • K-moment, once per round: K(W,b,c) = 0^inf W (010)^b A> 101 00 (101)^c 0^inf, where W is the counter (MSB on the left), (010)^b is the unary bell, 00 is the marker and (101)^c is the eaten part.
  • Round. The marker steps one unit left. B sweeps back: spacers become 101, 1-digits carry (100 -> 111), and B turns at the first 0-digit (000 -> 100). A sweeps right again (101 -> 010, 111 -> 000). Net effect: W += 1 in binary, ignoring spacers; b -= 1; c += 1.
  • Overflow. B reaches 0^inf and writes a new 100 digit, so the left edge grows by 3 cells. At every clean left record c = 2^D - 1 (2,911 of 2,919 records to 1e10).
  • Eat. When b = 0, the A sweep runs from the turned digit straight into the marker (100 A> 00 or 000 A> 00). A leftward C/D cascade follows, and the effect is a one-cell shift of the 3-cell grid:
  • re-read in the new grid, old 1-digits become spacers (100 -> 010), 0-digits stay (000), and old spacers become t = 001;
  • the old (101)^c becomes the new unary;
  • the marker is reborn at the right end (R8). The shifted segments (t units) then trigger mini-eats: the A sweep meets pseudo-markers inside them (K-moments with b <= 4 and a long unswept "rest" region beyond). The mini-eats re-align W, and by the next left record W is a clean word over {111, 101} again. At late times, eats mostly rewrite the leading run of digits, e.g. d^7 SSSdSdSSSdd -> SSS d^5 SSSdSdSSSdd at step 9.87e9 -> 9.90e9.
  • Growth. The epoch length is about N rounds, where N = b + c (the unary size). N grows by 0..38 units per epoch, N ~ (4t)^(1/3) (52 at 5e4, 1,018 at 2.6e8, 3,452 at 9.5e9), and the span is about 3N. W stays short: at most 141 cells to 1e10.

This is the round that empties the bell. - Level i: write W_i = Y x o^k, with k >= 0 trailing o's and x the unit before them (0^inf counts as z). - x = z: stop. - x = s: write Y = Y0 s^j, where Y0 is empty or ends in a digit, and set W_{i+1} = inc^(j+1)(Y0). Here x becomes the marker, and the trailing spacers of Y are its bell, which gives j+1 rounds. - x = t: halt. This cannot happen for a t-free W, because inc never creates a t. - Then X = reread(Y) o, with reread o -> s, z -> z, s -> t, and P = cascade(X), the first round in the new grid. The bell is b' = c + delta, where delta = the number of units removed by the levels (k of the last level, plus k+1+j for every x = s level). - Derivation from the phase table: - D passes o (Do: o -> u). Ds turns s into the marker t and goes on in B, which gives the mini-rounds. Dz writes D = 111 and E enters the unit y to its left. - E turns on y and writes a 1 into its third cell: Es s -> u, Eo o -> S, Ez z -> t. - The A sweep then turns u^M into o^M, the new bell, and the marker is reborn at the right end (R8). - Read one cell to the left, the E-modified y gives exactly reread(y), and its third-cell 1 turns the x position into the new digit o. - No c appears anywhere, which explains the deepen stage's "P and delta do not depend on c". - Validation: P and delta are exact on 1,070,595/1,070,595 words: all t-free words up to 13 units, plus 20,000 random words of 13..90 units, against the cell-level oracle (eatu_validate.json).

2. Lemma H: the Hub potential (lemma_h.py). Hub(X) = #{s,z strictly between the top and the lowest t} + #{z,o below the lowest t}. Invariant I: no o lies above the lowest t. On {z,o,s,t} words satisfying I, one CASCADE round: - (a) never meets the uncovered case (a turn on o); - (b) keeps I; - (c) adds exactly 1 to the value (o = 1, z = 0, s and t skipped); - (d) never increases Hub while a t remains; - (e) creates no t; - (f) leaves a value of 2^m with m <= Hub when it removes the top t.

Proof sketch: - I, (a) and (b). A STOP writes its o either where B stops, below the lowest remaining t (B cannot pass a t), or at the lowest unit of a run, which is directly above the t just entered and below every t the run passed. So I is kept. A C run only walks above the lowest t, where I says there is no o, so a turn on o never happens. - (c). By I every digit above the lowest t is z, so the z digits that C runs insert (s -> z) or delete (turn z -> s) do not change the value. The rest is a binary +1. - (d) and (e). Hub counts, below the top t, the z/o units and the s units that have a t below them. No unit ever becomes a t, and t's never move. The only conversion to a digit is s -> z inside a C run, which sits above the entered t, so that s was already counted. Every other change only removes counts: - a turn z -> s that ends up below the lowest t; - an s whose last t below it is gone; - an entered t, which becomes an s with no t below it. - (f). The carry that removes the top t turns every digit below its o into z, and by I every digit above is z, so the value is 2^m. The m digits below that o are units Hub counted at the start of the round. Usually the top t is the lowest t by then; the case where two adjacent t's go in one round (tzt -> ossss) is in the exhaustive check.

So from value(P) = 2 the top t goes at round k0 with k0 + 2 = 2^h and h <= Hub(P). This is the size stage's exact t-lifetime law, now with a proof.

Validation: - (a)-(f) hold on all 649,539 I-words with a t up to 11 units (39,424 of them remove the top t; 20,481 of those are tight). - CASCADE equals the cell-level TM round on all 17,496 I-words up to 8 units (lemma_h_L11.json).

3. Proof of the generic lemma (generic_chain.py). For every t-free W:

h <= Hub(P) <= Hub(X) <= G(Y)+1 <= G(W_final) <= G(W_1) = G(W),

where G = the number of digits strictly below the top spacer and W_final = Y z o^k is the last level. - h <= Hub(P) <= Hub(X): Lemma H. X satisfies I, since its only o is the last unit. - Hub(X) <= G(Y)+1: the t's of X are the spacers of Y. Its counted units are the digits of Y between its top and lowest spacer, the z digits of Y below its lowest spacer, and the appended o. - G(Y)+1 <= G(W_final): W_final = Y z o^k. - G(W_final) <= G(W_1): inc keeps the spacers in place and only prepends digits above all of them, and a level removes a suffix s^(j+1) o^k.

If the first unit of W is a digit, G(W) <= D(W)-1. Then 2^h <= 2^(D-1) <= value(W) = c+1, so k0 = 2^h - 2 <= c-1 < b' = c+delta: every t clears before the next eat, and the next eat word is t-free with value = c'+1 again. The eat cannot halt on a t-free word (EATU, x = t impossible), and neither can a round (I-words, Lemma H (a)). Every link holds on all 294,590 words with a t in P (348,151 t-free words up to 12 units), with 0 failures (generic_chain_L12.json). So the generic case now rests only on validated, finite unit rules: the phase table, the A-sweep law, ROUND, EATU and CASCADE.

4. The leading-spacer case is exactly one short. For W = s ..., G(W) = D(W), so the chain only gives h <= D. A t survives the epoch iff h = D and value(W) + delta + 1 < 2^D. Every link of the chain must then be an equality, so all of the following hold: - every level has k = 0; - Y has no o below its lowest spacer; - no round lowers Hub, so no C run turns strictly between two t's below the top.

These conditions are logical consequences of the proof and were not tested separately.

1. Equality cases (proof). Let W = s^a M (a >= 1, M t-free and starting with o) be a leading-spacer eat word with value(W) = c+1 and D digits. A t survives the epoch iff h = D and value(W) + delta + 1 < 2^D (recovery stage, section 4). h = D forces every link of the chain to be an equality: - G(W_final) = G(W_1). A level with k >= 1 removes k digits below the top spacer, and inc never adds any there. So k = 0 at every level. Since W_1 = inc(W) ends in a digit, k = 0 means it ends in z, and the eat stops at level 1. Hence W ends in o, the eat has a single level (W_final = inc(W)), and delta = 0. - Hub(X) = G(Y)+1. Y = inc(W) minus its last z has no o below its lowest spacer. - h = Hub(P) = Hub(X). Hub never drops during the epoch. - Consequences: value(W) is odd, so V := value(W)+1 is even, and V >= 2^(D-1) + 1.

2. TF: tightness in closed form (proof sketch from CASCADE; exact in every test). Write Y = s^a Z and split Z = g_0 s^e1 g_1 … s^em g_m into maximal digit groups g_i (o = 1, z = 0; value o_i, length L_i) and spacer runs. Then W is a tight failure iff: - W ends in o, m >= 1, g_m is in z*, value(W) <= 2^D - 2; - for i = m-1 down to 0: o_i + N_i ≡ 0 (mod 2^L_i), where N_(m-1) = e_m and N_i = e_(i+1) + C_(i+1), with C_i = (o_i + N_i) / 2^L_i.

Why. In X = t^a s r(B1) o, read every group above the lowest t-run as one old binary counter (s = 1, z = 0, t-runs as spacers). Entering a t is one old increment, by the C run above it. All the t's of a run T_i are entered in one round, which exposes the group G_(i-1) above them to the new counter. Hub does not drop in that round iff the last of these increments rolls G_(i-1) over from all ones. Otherwise a turn leaves an s in G_(i-1), below the lowest t. G_(i-1) receives e_i increments plus one carry per rollover of G_i, which gives the recursion. The C_0 carries out of the top group pass the block t^a and build an old counter above it, starting from 0^inf.

Consequences: - For every layout there is at most one tight bit pattern: the recursion fixes every o_i. Seen as exactly 1 per layout on all 942 tight layouts to 12 units. - Validation (tf_validate.py; TF against the exact EATU + emodel chain): - all 265,642 leading-spacer words to 13 units: TF agrees 265,642/265,642 (1,986 tight; tf_exh_L13.json); - 100,000 random words of the forced shape s^a M0 z V with 10..45 units: 100,000/100,000 (175 tight; tf_both_100000_s5.json); - 3,000 words built from random layouts by the recursion (2,145 with 20..50 units): all 3,000 are exact tight failures, 0 halts over 6 epochs (tf_build_3000_s11_E6.json).

3. T1: the word a tight failure produces (proof). With B := inc(W)[a:] = Z z, the next eat word is

W_next = bin(C_0) t^a B (bin in s = 1, z = 0, no leading z).

Proof. - Give every unit position below the top a type: spacer positions hold t, or s once entered; digit positions hold s (an old 1 not yet converted), z or o. CASCADE keeps the types (t -> s; s -> z, z -> o, o -> z and the turn z -> s on digit positions; nothing becomes t), and t's are entered from the bottom up. - Hub = (digit positions below the top t) - (unconverted s below the lowest t), and the first term is D. - Every t below the block is entered before the next eat: the highest such t has at most D-1 digit positions below it, and the value reaches V > 2^(D-1). The block goes all at once, and only at value 2^D > V, so it survives. - No unconverted s is left below the block: that would make Hub < D, so h < D. - So the region below the block is t-free, has B's layout and has value V, and a layout plus a value fix a t-free word: the region is B. Above the block lies the old counter of section 2, holding C_0.

Validation (tight_validate.py): - all 265,642 words to 13 units, 8 epochs: 1,986 tight failures, single level / delta 0 / W ends in o 1,986/1,986, and the exact form 1,986/1,986 (tight_exh_L13_E8.json); - every bit pattern of the 4 real layouts with D <= 14: 2/2 (tight_real_E6.json); - 10 random layouts with D = 12..15, all patterns: 6/6 (tight_rand_10_D12-15_s20261011_E5.json); - 150,000 random words of the forced shape: 84/84 (tight_struct_150000_L8-40_s20261011_E5.json); - the predicted bin(C_0) prefix matches in all 1,986 + 175 + 3,000 TF cases.

4. The eat after a tight failure (proof, from TF). - N0. V is even, so B ends in z, and the eat's first round is inc(B), inside B. No carry reaches the block. - N1. The level recursion has at most two levels and never reaches the block: - g_m nonempty: level 1 has k = 1 and x = z, so it stops; delta = 1. - g_m empty: level 1 has k = 1 and x = s (the bottom of s^em), so it runs e_m mini-rounds on the prefix. By TF, o_(m-1) + e_m ≡ 0 (mod 2^L_(m-1)), so g_(m-1) ends all z and level 2 stops at x = z; delta = e_m + 1. - The mini-rounds are CASCADE rounds on an I-word (no o above the block), so they cannot halt, even when their carry overflows into the block and converts it. - Checked on all 942 tight failures to 12 units: stops inside B 734, mini-rounds overflow into the block 208, reaches the block 0 (next_eat_L12.json). - In the overflow case the block is converted inside the eat, and the word left at level 2 is t-free. Its post-eat word has no v. A t can still appear at the next eat (73 of these 208 to 12 units), through the ordinary leading-spacer mechanism; that case is not analysed further. - N2, first contact with v. When the levels stay inside B, the reread turns the block into z o^(a-1) and B's top o into v = 110. In epoch 2 the groups below v replay the same TF arithmetic: the same o_i and e_i; the top group loses its top bit, which keeps the congruence mod 2^(L_0 - 1); case g_m empty is the same after the mini-rounds' carries. So every exposure is again a full rollover, no C run is empty, and the B phase never rises above the lowest t-run. The first unit to reach v is therefore a C run (Cv: v -> u, then F), never the B carry. Seen in all 511 such epochs to 12 units: the real and shadow bodies agree below v until that round (cv_event_L12.json).
