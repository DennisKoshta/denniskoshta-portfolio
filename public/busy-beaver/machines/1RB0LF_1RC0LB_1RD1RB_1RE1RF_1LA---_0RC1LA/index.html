Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Two zones split at the head: left = 1-blocks split by single 0s, right = (10)-pairs split by zero runs (plus a rare lone 1). A/F sweeps left turn a block into pairs; B/C passes right turn pairs into blocks. E-event K(L; a; r; R) = L 1^a E> 0^r R.
  • Exact rules (12,000/12,000 random, all 34.5M E-events from blank to 1e9): even a -> Ee eats the last two closed blocks y, b: K(L; y+3; b+1; (10)^(a/2) 0^(r-2) R). Odd a -> Eo closes a block 2, then a C-pass turns pairs into blocks until a run 0^n, n >= 3 (a = x+2, r = n-2) or a lone 1 (a = y+3, r = x+1). The block list is shuffled chaotically.
  • Halting needs a C-pass to meet exactly 0^2. Closed blocks stay even and zero runs odd (1.7M E-events to 5e7), and every rule keeps this, but only under side conditions (no lone pair before a run; a = 2 only at the edge) that are not yet shown inductive.
