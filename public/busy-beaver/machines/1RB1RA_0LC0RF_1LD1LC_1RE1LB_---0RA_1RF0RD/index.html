Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Bouncer: a solid 1-block gains one 1 per bounce; left of it sits a chaotic, aperiodic edge (~0.6 bits/cell).
  • Each bounce C dives into the edge in zigzag waves; the edge eats ~0.06 block cells per bounce on average, up to 11.
  • No 00 ever lies between head and rightmost 1 (checked to 1e9, local proof): it halts only if the block falls to 2 cells.

Proof strategy & riskshow the formal proof will go · 3 risks to watch

1) Prove the no-00 invariant as a closed predicate on the right side (progress_nonhalt_cond; checks the 11 transitions). Then halting needs A> 0 11 0^inf or A> 0 101 0^inf. 2) Show the edge never eats the block below 3 cells. This needs a potential that bounds the edge's growth per bounce (open). 3) Get from the blank tape to bounce ~30 (n=29) with es/execute, then apply 1) and 2) with an arbitrary left side l.

  • ▲The crux is a bound on erosion, and the edge's growth looks statistical: 0.057/bounce on average, up to 11 in one bounce, 60 in 50 bounces. If there is no deterministic bound, the machine is cryptid-like.
  • ▲There was an early close call: the block was 2 cells long at bounces 10 and 14-16. The init phase must simulate past it.
  • ▲Twin #26 1RB1RA_1LC1RE_---0LD_1LE1LD_0RA0LF_1LF0LB differs in 6 transitions, so we must re-derive the invariant before claiming 2 kills.
