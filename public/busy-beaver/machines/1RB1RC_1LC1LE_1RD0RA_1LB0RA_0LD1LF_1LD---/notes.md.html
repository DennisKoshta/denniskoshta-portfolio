Notation (tokens)

At every D:R event (transition C0 -> 1RD at the right end), the tape is

0^inf  [f_1] s_1 [f_2] s_2 ... s_{k-1} [f_k]  00 11 D> 0^inf        [f] := (10)^f 1
  • A field f is a run of 1s with single 0s between them: 1 (01)^f.
  • A separator s is the gap between two fields:
  • | is 00 (gap 2), the normal one;
  • + is no extra 0 (gap 0, 11); these appear after a bounce;
  • # is 000 (gap 3); this is a dead field.
  • The last field L = f_k is the growing end; P = f_{k-1}.
  • Tools: work/tokens.py (build/parse) and work/fields.py.

Mechanism: a binary counter whose digits are field parities

  • Each half-cycle runs from one D:R to the next. The head walks left from the right end, over even fields joined by |, and stops at the first odd field (or at a + or #). Then:
  • ODD: the field just left of the stopping point loses 1;
  • PLUS: at a +, the field left of it loses 2 and the + becomes |.
  • In both cases L grows by 3, so the parity of L flips every half-cycle.
  • So the parities of the fields are the bits of a binary counter. L's parity is the clock, P loses 1 every second half-cycle, the next field every fourth, and so on: a ruler sequence.
  • Fig counter_parity_3e8 shows this.
  • The turning depths of the head at 3e8 follow the same ruler pattern (positions 13.2k / 10.9k / 10.5k / 3.5k / 2.5k).
  • Fields are budgets. They decrease, except L and the +1/+2 that a bounce gives.

Far end (event c)

  • The far end loses about 1 unit of the first field per factor of about 1.5 in time. Each far-end bounce adds 1 or 2.
  • Line 75's first field reaches 2 at 2.4e14, so its natural far-end death is due soon after. 265 is 26 units behind, 179 is 24 and 597 is 4.
  • Hand-built far-end deaths (work/farend_small.py, data/farend_small_{a,b}.txt):
  • Setup: the real far-end block at 2.4e14 (122 | 292 | 34 | 2114), with prefix ∈ {none, 1 +, 0 |, 1 + 1 +, 1 + 0 |} and first field v = 0..6, followed by a short right part (3000 | 3001 | 2000) so that overflows come quickly.
  • All 35 variants ran 12 far-end events each: no halt.
  • The dead first field disappears and the next field (122) becomes the first field, eaten one unit at a time.
  • Prefix shapes seen: 1 +, 0 |, 1 + 1 +, 1 + 1 + 1 +, 1 + 0 |, 0 # x, 0 + x, 0 | 0 |, 1 + 2 |, x + y. A small set, but not yet shown to be closed.
  • The far end eats fields one at a time, and births at the right replace them. It does not glue new structure the way K42's escapes do.

Halting

  • The machine halts only via B reading 1 with 11 to its left (B1 -> E, E1 -> F, F1 halts).
  • Random tiny token lists (4,000 lists, fields 0..8, separators | + #) halt in 255 cases (3 | 4, 4 | 1, 0 + 0 + 4 | 6, ...). So a proof needs an invariant that excludes the bad local patterns. Tiny far-left fields alone are not bad; the hand-built deaths above survive.

Cryptid check (stop rule)

  • Not a cryptid stop.
  • Every event is a fixed affine map on the field list with a case split on parities.
  • The parities are the digits of a binary counter. L's parity alternates deterministically, and the other fields' parities flip only when they are decremented or bounced.
  • Nothing branches on a residue other than mod 2 of a counter digit. There is no x·m/k growth law; the only growth is L + 3 per half-cycle.
  • The four starts share every event through 2.37e14.
  • The qsim "Collatz" flags are this counter, which is the critic's point (1).
  • Points (2)-(4) of the critic are answered above: far-end deaths are built by hand and survive; birth = bounce; the word set is the same as W4-6 but the mechanism is now explicit.

Template check

  • Nearest kill: #3 1RB0LD_1RC0RA_1RD1RF_1LA0LE_1RE1LD_0RB--- (Skelet 1-like Class 2). It is a binary down-counter on explicit digit blocks s(d), with merges and a re-pairing overflow. Its proof uses one macro rule per decrement and a per-step invariant J.
  • Applies partly. The architecture carries over: a rule per half-cycle, merges, a re-pairing bounce, and an invariant on budgets with the parity defects held by the slow blocks.
  • Differences: here the digits are the field parities (no digit blocks); there are 3 separator kinds plus far-end prefixes; and births happen at every bounce.
  • Upstream Sk1Machines.v (mxdys, Sk1Counter with 6-phase digit words) and Sk1like_TC_v*.v (K42's) are not templates: different word sets and digits.
  • One start-parametric argument covers all four lines. The starts merge into one orbit up to the first field (all even offsets, identical event times through 2.37e14). Each extra start is a #24-style delta: its own base segment to a common token-list family.
