The mechanism in one paragraph

In TP only A1 = 1LF enters F, and A1 writes 1 on the cell to the right of F. So when TP's F reads a 1 at cell p, cell p+1 is 1, and

TP:   x 1 <F 1 r   --F1 (1RC)-->  C reads p+1 = 1 --C1 (1LA)--> A reads p = 1 --A1 (1LF)-->   x <F 1 1 r   (3 steps)
W4-2: x 1 <F 1 r   --F1 (1LF)-->                                                              x <F 1 1 r   (1 step)

Same tape, same state, same head. TP from 0^inf F> 0^inf is therefore W4-2 from F with every F1 stretched into three steps (the detour), and 349 from blank is its mirror image. In 349's own letters the detour is 1 A> 1 x --(A1 D1 B1)--> 1 1 A> x where TM518 tm does it in one A1 = 1RA step.
