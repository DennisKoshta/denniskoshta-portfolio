In each case this is the only one of the 720 renamings, with or without the mirror, that fits. So the other three machines are the parent run from 0^inf B> 0^inf, 0^inf C> 0^inf and 0^inf D> 0^inf (the last two mirrored). These are different orbits of the same macro map.

In simulation the B-start and C-start runs lock onto the parent's run. After 54 macro steps their states equal the parent's except for one exponent near the top (v_1, u_2 or u_1, depending on the class of the invariant below), larger by 4 (start B) or 3 (start C). From then on the same rule fires at every macro step (checked on the abstract orbits for about 1.2·10^6 macro steps).

The D-start run is a different orbit: 76,245 cells at 10^9 steps, against 77,685 for the parent.

Proof for the parent (verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0LE_1RC0RC.v).

The tape is a binary counter on a list of digits d_1 .. d_m, with d_m least significant, followed by a fixed block:

K(d_1 .. d_m; a, b) = 0^inf 10 01 d_1 .. d_m (10)^a 000 (10)^b B> 0^inf

(u,0,v) = (10)^u 00 (01)^v      bit 0
(u,1,v) = (10)^u (01)^v         bit 1

A carry M adds 1. Every digit it touches pays one 01 from its v, and the digit flipped 0→1 also gains a 10. A flipped digit with no 01 left merges into the next digit (a drop), and the counter loses a digit.

When all bits are 1, the overflow O swaps the halves of every digit:

K((u_1,1,v_1) .. (u_m,1,v_m); a, b)
  --> K((0,0,u_1), (v_1−1,0,u_2), .., (v_{m−1}−1,0,u_m), (v_m,1,a); b+3, 1)

So the counter restarts at 1 with one more digit. The rules fail only if a carry reaches a bit-0 digit with no 01 left.

The rules.

  • 20 window lemmas: the shifts S1–S6 (one block by es, then shift_rule_R/shift_rule_L, or induction for S5) and the turns T1–T14 (each a plain es).
  • They compose into the digit lemmas Lb, Lf, Rb, Ro.
  • List induction over the digits (LbS, RbS, RoS) gives the three rules, each K .. -->+ K ..:
  • M_spec;
  • Mdrop_spec, M with a drop: M_spec plus a tape equality by lpow_add;
  • O_spec.

The invariant. Which digits drop, and when, is irregular, so a cycle has no closed form. The proof rests on a per-step invariant:

INV = Common ∧ (A ∨ B ∨ C)

The classes follow the run:

  • A: a cycle with no drops;
  • B: the next cycle, while its top digit (0,0,1) is about to fall;
  • C: the rest of that cycle.

The clauses are stated through R_j, the number of carries still to come into digit j before the next overflow (Rof, by recursion on the least-significant-first list). They are:

  • parities;
  • fuel bounds v_j ≥ R_j + 1;
  • lower bounds 2^(j−1) resp. 2^j on ubar_j, the value u_j will have at the next overflow.

All of them are in additive form, with no truncating subtraction. Common's v ≥ 1 for every digit is exactly what rules out the failure case.

The closure. Four arithmetic facts F0–F3 give the step lemma:

  • F0, prefix bound: Rof_bound;
  • F1, touch: F1;
  • F2, drop: F2;
  • F3, overflow: Oz_AB/Oz_CA.
step : INV s -> exists s', Kc s -->+ Kc s' /\ INV s'
init : c0 -->* K [d1 1 2] 8 5        (* 483 steps by solve_init, to a class-A state *)

A touch leaves ubar and vbar = v − R (the next-overflow values of u and v) unchanged, so only drops and overflows need arithmetic.

Finally, nonhalt is multistep_nonhalt with init, then progress_nonhalt_cond with P := INV.

How the other three files reuse it.

verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0RB_1LC0RA.v (start B) is verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0LE_1RC0RC.v with three changes:

  • the new TM string;
  • the renaming applied to every state name: {{B}}→{{A}} 22 times, {{C}}→{{B}} 18, {{D}}→{{C}} 5, {{E}}→{{D}} 18 (the file names neither A nor F);
  • a new start: init runs 732 steps to K [d1 1 10] 8 1 (class A).

Every proof script is unchanged (the es/follow proofs re-close on the renamed table). So are the three rule specs, INV, step and the tactic line that closes the base in nonhalt.

verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0RE_1LC0RE.v (start D, mirrored) is a separate adaptation, proved directly on its own table without busycoq's flip/permute lemmas.

Its machine part (window lemmas, digit lemmas, sweeps and edge chunks) is verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0LE_1RC0RC.v mirrored by hand, with π = A→B, B→C, C→D, D→A:

l {{q}}> r       ↦   r <{{π q}} l
l <* xs          ↦   xs *> l
shift_rule_R     ↔   shift_rule_L
list literals    unchanged

The three rule statements come out word for word. So the abstract part (INV, F0–F3, closure) is the text of verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0LE_1RC0RC.v byte for byte, except that step is renamed macro_step so that it does not shadow the framework's step. New: init, 215 steps to K [d1 1 3] 4 1 (class A).

verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0LD_1RC0RE.v (start C, mirrored) is verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0RE_1LC0RE.v renamed, with the same three kinds of change as above:

  • the TM string;
  • the renaming A→B, B→C, C→D, D→A on every state name, with no further mirror: {{A}}→{{B}} 5 times, {{C}}→{{D}} 22, {{D}}→{{A}} 18, {{E}} fixed;
  • init: 687 steps to K [d1 1 9] 8 1 (class A).

Every proof script, the three rule specs and the abstract part are the text of verify/Skelet1LikeC2_1RB0RE_1LC0RE.v.
