  • A, quiet cycle: no drops; every digit vbar ≥ 1, ubar odd.
  • B, top (0,0,1) about to fall: safe parity v+β odd; ubar_j ≥ 2^(j-1).
  • C, top has fallen: safe parity; ubar_j ≥ 2^j.
