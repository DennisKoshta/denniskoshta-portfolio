Candidate rules

Notation is wiki directed-head; x and y are arbitrary words. Each rule is the mirror image of a #5 rule under σ: #5's C (the diver) is #26's A, and #5's A (the walker toward the wall) is #26's D. I validated every rule with tools/sim.py on 60 random instances (random x, y, k, n, j), all 60/60 (work/validate_rules.py, work/validate_rules.json).

  • Case split.
  • A reading 0 sees one of the right contexts 00, 010 or 011, so T, Ca and Fo cover it completely.
  • D reading 0 with no 00 on its left sees one of 1110, 11010, 10110 or 101010 (E1, E2, P1, P2), or the wall (bounce).
  • Between rules, A walks right over 1s and D walks left over 1s.
  • B, C, E and F occur only inside rules.
  • Halting door. It is the only route to the halting transition C0: D0 → E1 → F0^j → F1 → B0 → C0.
  • Invariant: the cells from the leftmost 1 to head−1 never contain 00.
  • I checked it at every step from the blank tape to 1e9 with work/inv00.c: 0 violations.
  • Its sanity variants fail as they should: the region widened by 1 cell gives 23,551 violations in 1e6 steps, and widened by 2 gives 84,719.
  • A brute-force Python rescan (work/inv00_brute.py) agrees exactly on 3e4 steps: 0 / 1,664 / 6,078.
  • It is the mirror of #5's no-00 invariant, which has a local proof (#5 notes).
  • Consequence: #26 halts only via 0^inf 1 1 0 <D or 0^inf 1 0 1 0 <D, that is, only if the edge eats the block down to 2 cells (j ≤ 1 by the invariant).
  • Twin map (see Observation): checked 102,240/102,240. Why it holds for all time:
  • the tables are identical under σ∘mirror;
  • the configurations agree at bounce 2 (with block n+1 for #5);
  • the block length only changes the walk time, as long as the rules never reach the wall end of the block. That is #5's wall-model condition n ≥ e+3; here n ≥ 33 and e ≤ 15 after bounce 40.
