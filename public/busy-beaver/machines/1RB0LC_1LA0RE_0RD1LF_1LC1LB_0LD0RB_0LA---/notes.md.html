Deepen pass (overnight 2026-10-10)

Confidence: med, up from med with open regimes. Every regime up to 1e10 is now covered by one exact rule table. What is still missing is an inductive invariant, which is a proof obligation. Recommendation: proof (the invariant is the main lemma), after Dennis looks.

New view: tokens. The tape is a word over four tokens: 0=00, 1=10, O=100, I=110. The parse is unique. - The background block is 10110 = 1I. A digit 110 / 100 is I / O. - The left counter is a token word w: - old phase X/Y = the last token of w (1/0); - old separator s = an O/I token inside w.

Configuration ("departure"). E has just entered the first background block after w: 0^inf w E> (10110)^g d_1 (10110)^G_1 ... d_k (10110)^G_k 11 0^inf. One sweep takes one departure to the next.

Rules. Formulas are in deepen.json → model; code is in scripts/deepen/model3.py and linc.py. - strip: while w ends in I1, drop it and add 1 to g. That I1 is the C block 11010, which the returning F (R2) turns into 10110. This is how a separator leaves w. - GEN (g ≥ 2 or d_1 = 0): w → inc_L(w) and (g,D,G) → inc(g,D,G), where inc is the old R3/R4/R5. - inc_L is "+2": skip the last token, then carry right to left. - A carry turns 1 into 0. It stops at the first 0 (which becomes 1) or O (which becomes I). - A carry that reaches an I gives x I 1^k t → inc_L(x) O 0^(k-1) t 1. - Off the left edge, the left end grows by one token. - ABS1 (g = 1, d_1 = 1, w ends in 1): w → drop_last(inc_L(inc_L(w))), right part → inc(G_1+2, D[2..], G[2..]). - ABS0 (g = 1, d_1 = 1, w = u0): - v0 0 1^k 0 (k ≥ 1) → v0 1 0^(k-1) O 1 1; - v0 0 1^k 0 0 → v0 O 0^(k+1) 1 1; - if no 0 sits left of the 1-run, a leading 1 or O replaces v0 0; - right part → inc(G_1+1, D[2..], G[2..]). - ABSO1 (g = 1, d_1 = 1, w = xO1): w → inc_L(x), and the right part becomes (1, [1]+D'[2..], G'), where (0,D',G') = inc(1,D,G). This rule only appears in random tests.

Open items from the observe pass, now closed: - Phase-Y absorption is just ABS0 or ABS1 chosen by the last token. - A separator is created by ABS0 (it appends O11) or by a carry crossing the left edge (a leading O/I). - A separator moves only as a carry rewrites it: O→I, or an I-crossing. - A separator dissolves through the I1 strip. - Configurations with two O/I tokens or a leading O/I, such as 100 10 10 10 100 … at step 86,806, were unparsed in the old hub picture. The token model covers them.

Evidence: - Lockstep check of the C departure dump against the model, from step 2,000 to 1e10: 1,096,578 sweeps with 0 mismatches (GEN 1,094,493 / ABS1 1,561 / ABS0 524). - Random instances simulated directly to the next departure: 8,540/8,540 pass (GEN 5,487 / ABS1 1,647 / ABS0 1,311 / ABSO1 95). - Sizes: words of 4–40 tokens, 2–16 digits, g up to 300, gaps up to about 600. - Results are in scripts/deepen/valreal_21..23.json. - Invariant probes on the 1e10 data: - G is non-increasing at every departure. - G_1 ≥ 1 everywhere except once, at step 2,343 (initial segment). - min g = 1. - At most 2 O/I tokens; words have at most 14 tokens. - At absorptions, ABS0 always sees an all-0/1 tail.

Still open (main lemmas for proof.md): 1. Word-shape invariant: - O/I tokens are at least 4 apart; - the last token is 0 or 1; - at an ABS0 step, no O/I sits right before the trailing 1-run. Random words outside this set break the rules, so it is essential. 2. Spacing invariant: G is non-increasing and G_1 ≥ 1 at every absorption, which keeps g ≥ 1. 3. Parity coupling: the transient tails xI0, xO1 and xI1 never meet an absorption. The informal argument is that g ≥ 2 after ABS1 and that d_1 alternates. 4. Initial segment: simulate directly from the blank tape to the first configuration in the invariant. 5. A per-rule step count, and a proof that F never reads 1.

Not a cryptid: every rule is a counter increment or a fixed rewrite.
