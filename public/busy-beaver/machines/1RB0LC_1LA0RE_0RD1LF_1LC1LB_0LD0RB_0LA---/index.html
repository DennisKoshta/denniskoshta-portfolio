Mechanism

How it works: 3 notes
  • Period-5 tape 10110. Right counter: little-endian digits 110=1 / 100=0 in the stripes; each sweep adds 1 with carry.
  • A carried digit moves 5 cells left (digit k drifts 1 block per 2^k sweeps); overflow adds two 0-digits at the right end.
  • Left counter: big-endian bits 10=1 / 00=0, +1 per 2 sweeps; absorbing the lowest digit halves it (phases X/Y follow).

Is anyone working on any of these Structural counter + counter / Counter Class 4 holdouts, or has anyone tried them and got stuck? We found no sheet notes, threads or claims, and none are on mxdys's UBRRBA list or dyuan01's longitudinal list.

1. `1RB0LC_1LA0RE_0RD1LF_1LC1LB_0LD0RB_0LA---`, `1RB0LC_1LA0RE_0RD0LA_1RE1RA_0LD1RF_0RB---`, `1RB1RC_0LA1RF_1RD0LE_1LC0RB_0RA0LC_0RD---` (identical trajectories to 1e9). Period-5 background `10110` carrying a binary counter whose digits are 3-cell defects (`110` = 1, `100` = 0). Digits drift left on carries, and a bit counter at the left edge absorbs them.
2. `1RB---_1LC1RE_1LD0LC_1LE1LC_1RF0RE_1RA0RB`. Period-5 tape cut into segments by phase-shift walls. Each wall moves 3/2 times as often as the one to its left, so segment lengths run 12, 18, 27, 41, 61, 92, 138, 206 blocks.
3. `1RB1LC_1RC1LB_1LD0RB_0LE1RD_---0LF_0LA1LF` and its twin `1RB1LA_1LC0RA_0LD1RC_---0LE_0LF1LE_1RF1LB`. Run-length counter whose epochs double in length. Each epoch starts at `0^inf <A 0 1^3 0^2 1^2 0^2 1^(2k) 0^2 1^(2k+1) 0^P 1 0^Q 1 0^inf` with P+Q = 2k and P irregular. No halt in 1e11 steps; in a macro simulation every even P closes the epoch for k <= 13.

What we checked: direct simulation (1e9 steps for groups 1 and 2, 1e11 for group 3), plus the local rules above on random instances. No proofs yet. If nobody has them, we'll write Rocq proofs, starting with group 1.
