Cryptid check (ground rule 6)

Not a cryptid. The dynamics are an exact binary counter with deterministic carries and a deterministic re-pairing. There is no parity-of-growing-number branching that decides halting. Halting would need a carry to reach a bit-0 digit with v = 0, and #11's INV, already proved in Rocq for this table, rules that out. Here INV holds from step 215 on.
