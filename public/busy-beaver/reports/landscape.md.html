BB(6) community landscape for Track B targeting

Compiled 2026-10-09. Scope: the bbchallenge wiki (all 2026 monthly reports, TYBR 2025, BB(6), Holdouts lists, Cryptids, BMO, 103 per-machine pages, user pages), the annotated spreadsheet (12 CSV tabs, link and strikethrough sidecars, and xlsx comments), GitHub (busycoq PRs, 2026 community repos), the discuss.bbchallenge.org forum, and web search. Discord is the primary venue, and we cannot read it. Everything here is second-hand from wiki/TMBR summaries, sheet notes, and GitHub.

This complements the busycoq git-history forensics done in parallel and does not repeat it.

Caveat on sources: the TMBR pages for May to October 2026 are marked "in progress and has not yet been released" on the wiki and may still change. Jan to Apr 2026 are released.

0. TL;DR for Track B

  1. The maintainer is the biggest AI user in BB(6). mxdys has used LLMs to write Rocq proofs since at least July 2026. - Aug 15: 20 machines (all of Counter Class 1 plus 4 Dekaheptoids), 32 more Rocq proofs, and decider settings for 10 others. - The fall in holdouts from 1,064 (Jul 28) to 778 (Oct 7) is largely his LLM-assisted proofs and new decider families. - Consequence: "easy" machines disappear fast. Anything that one of his decider families can absorb will probably be absorbed before we finish.
  2. AI help is normal and openly disclosed. The bar is that the proof is checked, not who wrote it. - Rocq proofs that compile against BB6 are accepted whatever their origin. - Lean-only proofs, AI-made deciders, and informal AI claims are labelled "unofficial", "not independently verified", or "moderate rigor". They do not change the Rocq-verified count. - Wording seen on the wiki: "Note that the formal proofs were made with the help of Claude Opus and Aristotle AI"; "(Note that the new holdout lists are unofficial, as the correctness of the deciders has not yet been verified)".
  3. Proofs are accepted through Discord, not PRs. - The pinned branch (ccz181078/busycoq, BB6) has 13 PRs in total. Two were merged: sligocki's Antihydra (2025) and univerz's "vibecoded" CounterRegular.v. CounterRegular.v was deleted the next day in mxdys's commit 605d26d ("add BMO9, S18, SOBCv11,v12"). - A Claude-written halting proof (JacobRSchwartz-AI, PR #2, the "Odometer") was closed within 5 hours: "with your hint, this TM was newly proven in verify/UBRRBAHaltv2.v". - 8 PRs from an AI agent (mira-quine) have had no response for 9 days. - The pattern: mxdys takes the insight and re-proves it in his own decider or family framework. A hint plus a short, readable argument is what actually moves the list.
  4. Machines that are already claimed or have a proof pending (avoid, or coordinate first): - 21 machines in mira-quine's open PRs (§7.1); - 1RB0RA_0LC0LA_1RF1LD_1LE0LD_1LC1LF_---1RA, which has a public Lean 4 proof (M-Abozaid, Sep 28) that busycoq does not include; - dyuan's Oct 1 list of 24 machines that are "easy by longitudinal analysis" and mxdys's Aug 24 list of 86 machines that are "easy to accelerate with UBRRBA". Both are Discord-only; Dennis should fetch them.
  5. Where the open machines are (my per-class count on the 778 list, which matches T English's sheet comments almost exactly; §5): - Shift-overflow counter 128, Skelet 1-like Class 1 101, Unknown 93, Bell-like 80, Quadratic Mess 51, Cubic Mess 50, Bell eats counter 40, SO bouncer counter 33, and so on. - SOC is the maintainer's active front (418 → 128 in 2026). - Classes nobody has touched in 2026 are either tarpits or neglected: Chaotic Bell Class 3 (22, 0 solved), Chaotic 1dCA-like in bell (9), Structural counter + counter (5), Counter Class 4 (2).
  6. Tarpits to exclude from the pilot (§6): - the 20 cryptids and 7 potential cryptids; - the open BMO problems (1, 5, 6, 8, 10, 11); - the "Machines at the Edge" (tetrational or probviously-halting simulation lotteries); - most of Skelet 1-like Class 1.
  7. Spreadsheet staleness: the Results tab covers the 1,534 list (Nov 2025). The Statistics tab itself says "Results outdated, Direct Sim up-to-date". Use it for notes and people, not for status. Status comes from the 778 list and its equivalence file.

Methods that killed BB(6) machines in 2026, roughly in order of volume

  1. mxdys's decider families, versioned and extended week by week: SOC*/SOCSS, SOBC, BTC, SOTC, RRBA/UBRRBA, CubicCap, BLEC, Inductive, and others.
  2. mxdys's C++ FAR with the DFA generator, run by Andrew Ducharme, prurq, Peacemaker II and mxdys himself.
  3. Equivalence finding (Eqv_v2/v3; -d's help in April).
  4. LLM-assisted individual Rocq proofs by mxdys (Jul to Sep).
  5. Accelerated simulation to halting (prurq's Cascade, Quick_Sim, LongAcc, and bespoke simulators).
  6. Translated-cycler detection with long preperiods.
  7. Individual human proofs, most of them formalized by mxdys (hipparcos, dyuan, fifi_49513, Racheline, Peacemaker).

3.3 Reading of community norms on AI

  • Disclosure is the norm and costs nothing: "Co-Authored-By: Claude …" trailers, wiki headings such as "Analysis by Opus 4.7 / DrDisentangle", and TMBR notes such as "made with the help of …". I found no hostility to disclosed AI use on any page.
  • Verification is what matters. A result counts toward the Rocq-verified count only once it is in busycoq's BB6 branch, in practice through mxdys. Lean proofs and AI-written deciders get a separate "unofficial" or "to be independently verified" label.
  • Volume does not equal acceptance. Large, generated, multi-file PRs (mira-quine, zero-context, and to a lesser extent the Odometer) get either silence or "thanks, I re-proved it in my framework". The maintainer clearly prefers to fold results into his versioned family files over merging bespoke proof developments.
  • Readable informal arguments are valued: rule tables, BMO-style reformulations, "why it can't halt" sections. Both TMBR and the wiki surface who explained the behaviour, not only who produced a .v file.
  • No explicitly AI-attributed incorrect proof was found in the sheet or on the wiki. The sheet tab "Unverified probably incorrect proofs" (§4.3) holds 6 human-attributed claims and says nothing about AI. One LLM-flavoured crank post on the forum ("Antihydra copycat", Mar 2026) got a patient, polite correction from aparker.

4. How proofs get accepted (submission norms)

4.1 The actual pipeline

  1. Discuss on Discord. Use the #bb6 channel (1239205785913790465) or a dedicated thread per machine. sligocki on the forum (Sep 2026): "The bulk of the discussion is happening on our Discord server … there is a dedicated #bb6 channel".
  2. mxdys formalizes or verifies. This is often the same day: hipparcos in June; fifi_49513 on Sep 4 to Sep 5; prurq's halters; "confirmed in Rocq by mxdys".
  3. The proof lands in the busycoq BB6 branch as mxdys's commit, usually in one of his family files.
  4. The next holdout list drops the machine. TMBR records who did what. RobinCodes updates the sheet when he has time.

4.3 What "house style" means, from the busycoq README and the merged code

  • Use the existing framework. Proofs are individual .v files in verify/ built on Individual62, with TM_from_str, es/follow/ind, and multistep_nonhalt plus progress_nonhalt_*. Equivalences go through Eqv_v2 or Eqv_v3.
  • "Don't refactor anything" (README). The ~3,000 individual proofs and the decider pipeline are not compiled by make ("about a month").
  • Two methods the README endorses:
  • a proof template: a parameterized proof plus a parameter search;
  • "copy and modify existing proofs".
  • Comment blocks with the human-readable rule tables at the bottom of each file. Some of mxdys's 2026 files say "generated", e.g. QuadraticMess30.v: "The entire route is generated by a proved RLE parameter interpreter."

4.4 Relevant to our check_kill.sh

zero-context's PR #3 shows the BB6 branch contains unsound Admitted lemmas: - BBinf.all_qs_spec and all_syms_spec are provably False. - Similar admits sit in TMLR.v, TMAddOneSymbol.v and TMLocalHistory.v. - They are registered with #[export] Hint Resolve … : core, so a bare auto could silently use False.

Our gate already guards against this: Print Assumptions must match the baseline {functional_extensionality_dep}. Still, our proofs must not Require BBinf, TMLR, TMAddOneSymbol, TMLocalHistory or Inductive_inf. It would be worth adding a check that greps for those imports.
