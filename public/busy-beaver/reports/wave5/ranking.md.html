The lesson from Wave 4

Wave 4 had 8 picks. The ones with a live template were all killed: W4-1 (#30's table), W4-2 (1 transition from BECv5) and W4-3 (3 starts, SBCv6 TM21 shape).

The new proofs in Unknown classes all parked: W4-4, W4-6 and W4-8. So did W4-5 (a counter with no invariant) and W4-7 (hlin decider, wide digits).

So Wave 5 leans on templates on purpose. The strongest common signal is W4-2's own architecture: Bell eats counter, records (10)^a (01)^b 1^c, a binary-ruler transfer between the two fields, and a size invariant. 349, 134, 525 and 29 all show it, so four picks reuse it. This concentrates risk in one class, but the merged scores are not close: those four average 0.43-0.91, and the best non-BEC family 0.27.

  1. W5-6 (88/280): the W4-7 argument was backwards. The ranking said W4-7's obstacle does not apply because hlin already handles this table.
