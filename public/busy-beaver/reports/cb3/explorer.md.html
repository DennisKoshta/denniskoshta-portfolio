What the explorer is

It searches for a regular tape language that contains the real orbit and is closed under single machine steps. A closed language with no halting step is a non-halting certificate.

Segment view of a tape side. Each side of the head is read nearest-first and cut into segments: maximal alternating blocks (no two equal neighbours). Two consecutive segments meet at a defect (two equal cells), so a side is fully given by its first cell and its list of segment lengths.

In this view: - the stripe (01)^n is one long segment; - a separator 00110 between stripe runs is the short-segment cluster (2,2); - a glider is a lone defect between two long segments (the empty cluster).

Shapes. A side shape is (first cell, segment tokens, tail): - a token is an exact length below K, or "length ≥ K with a given parity"; - the tail is the blank tape, or X(q), the rest-of-tape automaton of that side started in state q.

The rest-of-tape automaton X. A unit is a cluster of short segments (length < T) followed by one long segment, with its length class (exact T..K-1, or "≥ K even/odd"). The automaton's state is the previous unit (history 1). Its transitions are "unit u may follow unit q" and "after q the tape may end with this short cluster". There is no condition on long lengths beyond their class, and no parity condition on long gaps.

Exploration. This is a lazy closed-tape-language search in the dual-head semantics of the certificate checker: - a key is (left shape, right shape, state, direction); - a step pops the scanned cell (exact splits) and pushes the written cell onto the side behind the move; - a pushed side keeps its first N long segments explicit and folds the rest into X(q), after checking that the rest is in X(q); - missing automaton transitions are added (discovery), and the run is repeated until a run adds nothing.

Every shape's language is exactly the union of its split edges. These are the edges that the exported certificate and the checker use.

Checks

  • validate_steps.py: takes sampled symbolic transitions, builds random concrete members, runs one step of tools/sim, and checks membership of the result with a structural test that does not use the edge lists.
  • check_cert.py: an independent certificate checker. It reads only the exported JSON and checks closure (every edge into every scanned state, existential push, listed successor key) and acceptance of the concrete entries by NFA simulation.

What was built. In segx.py: - A pack token P(n): a run of m length-2 segments, with m ≥ n and m of the same parity as n. - Every maximal run of 2-segments and packs merges into one token. - An exact run of 2s becomes a pack once it is M long. Pack counts above M+1 are capped to M or M+1 by parity. - Split stays exact: it never caps, and a leading P(1) splits into one exact 2 or a P(3). - Fold expands small packs into canonical units. - The rest-of-tape automaton X became an NFA, with moves (q, unit, q2). - Stage 1's fold forced X(q) ⊇ X(q2), which merged unrelated contexts. Now the last folded unit goes straight into the tail's own state. - Short segments in front of an X tail become a derived state ('m', cell, shorts, q2). Its moves are computed from q2's moves when needed. - Two options: a different window per side (N=(NL,NR)) and history 1 or 2. Snapshots: logs/segx_stage1_final.py, logs/segx_stage2_packs_hist1.py, logs/segx_stage2_nfa_v1.py.

Why it does not close (why_wide.py, realruns.py). Every "wide" trace has the same shape: 1. The head sweeps left over a left side made of lone defects 5 cells apart. 2. Each 5-cell segment it crosses becomes one separator on the right, so the separators touch: 00110 00110 00110 .... 3. The left automaton lets 5-segments repeat without bound.

The real orbit never does this. To 3e6 steps, there are at most 2 consecutive 5-segments on S0LC and on T1, and every cluster has at most 7-8 segments (14 cells).

So the invariant would need a run-length count on the left side. History 2 does not give it: an unbounded run of 5s comes back through the fold's tail move and through long classes whose split leaves a 5.

Each leak fixed so far (gap length classes in stage 1, then packs, then the NFA fold, then derived states) uncovered the next one. Every fix has stayed inside the family "finite cluster alphabet plus gap classes".

Checks. - Step semantics: 17 runs, 25,359 sampled symbolic steps compared with one tools/sim step each on random concrete members, with 0 mismatches (validate_steps.py, with pack instantiation). - Shape algebra (test_packs.py): about 4,000 random members per run on S0LC and T1. Three properties were checked: - every member is covered by a split edge; - every edge stays inside its shape; - push followed by fold only enlarges.

There were 0 failures after one fix: split had been capping exact 2-runs into packs, an enlargement inside split that the test caught. - walk_cert.py (a multi-step tools/sim walk against an exported certificate, using only the certificate's NFAs) is written but has not been run, because nothing closed.
