Wave 6 ranking (2026-10-14)

These are the 8 picks to attack now. They come from merging the four Wave 6 scout tables in reports/wave6/: templates, families, dynamics and claims.

Scout signals are evidence only. Only a tools/check_kill.sh PASS counts as a kill.

The short version. There is no exact sibling of any of our 31 kills, and only one machine (96) lies within 2 transitions of one. So the picks lean on the two routes that worked in Wave 5: - the same table as an upstream hand proof, started from another state (W5-7's route); - the budget-plus-round-counter architecture (W5-3, W5-4 and W5-5).
